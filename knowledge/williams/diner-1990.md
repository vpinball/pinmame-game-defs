# Diner (Williams, 1990)

Coverage: **partial - every controller address enumerated and named, wiring, polarity, mechanisms and recreation
behaviour validated; two requirements stay open: spatial placement (the G.I. sockets come only from the retained
table, the DINE-TIME playfield flasher has no table object and three cup flashers are not modelled) and variant
differences (the PA-0 prototype ROM is not in the local corpus, so its hardware is unknown).**

The definition is generated by `tools/curate_diner.py`; the spatial review is `reports/spatial/williams/diner-1990.md`
and the runtime evidence is derived by `tools/diner_runtime_evidence.py`.

## Identity and variants

DINER is Williams model 571 (IPDB 681, OPDB `GRWBd-MLy5p`), manufactured from June 11, 1990 on a System 11C CPU board
(D-11883-571) with the D-12232-1 Master Display Board (two sixteen-character alphanumeric rows) and a separate System
11C audio board (D-11581-571); `GEN_S11C` is PinMAME's "No CPU board sound" generation. The Operations Manual is
16-571-101 (June 1990), corrected by Amendment #1.

All eight pinned `diner_*` drivers share `dinerGameData` through `CORE_CLONEDEF`:

| Driver | Firmware | Compatibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `diner_l4` | Williams LA-4 production, the parent, the driver the retained table and every diner_l4 run use | identical |
| `diner_l3` | Williams LA-3 (U27 LA-3 with the U26 L-2) | identical |
| `diner_l2`, `diner_l1` | Williams LU-2 and LU-1 European revisions | identical |
| `diner_f2`, `diner_g2` | Williams LF-2 French and LG-2 German language revisions | identical |
| `diner_l4fr` | 2025 community "Fixed Right Ramp" modification of LA-4 | identical |
| `diner_p0` | Williams PA-0 prototype, catalogued 1989 | unknown |

The Coil, Single Lamps and Switch Levels tests of `diner_l3`, `diner_l2`, `diner_l1` and `diner_l4fr` pair every
address with the same name as `diner_l4` (`evidence/runtime/system-11/diner-l3-service-tests.json` and its siblings), so none of
them addresses a device the others lack. `diner_f2`, `diner_g2` and `diner_p0` are not in the local ROM corpus; the
first two are language revisions of the production game, but nothing describes the prototype's hardware.

## Controller facts that matter to a recreation

- **Matrices.** Switches and lamps are System 11's sequential 1-64 (column `(n-1)/8+1`, row `(n-1)%8+1`). The switch
  matrix is read uninverted, and the ROM's Switch Levels test names every fitted switch at public 1, so every matrix
  contact, the opto ones included, rests open.
- **A/C select relay at 12.** The eight switched drivers serve an "A" coil at 1-8 while 12 is released and a "C"
  flasher at 25-32 while it is energized; PinMAME publishes them separately. `S11_MUXSW2` copies 12 into switch 2, the
  relay's C-side contact, which the ROM checks in its C-Side test; never drive switch 2.
- **Special solenoids 17-22** (left jet, left sling, right jet, right sling, lower jet, shooter lane feeder) are driven
  by the ROM through its PIA lines. `dinerGameData` maps no switch to them, but in play the ROM fires each jet and
  sling within one poll of its switch closing, so a consumer drives only the switch.
- **Game-on 23** rises when a game starts and drops at the third plumb-bob tilt and at game over; the retained table
  enables its flippers from it.
- **Flippers.** `FLIP_SWNO(58,57)`: drive the buttons at 84 (left) and 82 (right). PinMAME copies them into the lane-
  change optos 58 and 57 and fabricates 45-48. Writes to 57/58 are overwritten.
- **G.I.** Output 10 drives two relays, one on a relay board under the playfield and one on the insert board, which
  switch the playfield and the backbox G.I.; energized means G.I. off. The ROM holds it through a tilt.
- **Displays.** Two rows of sixteen 16-segment characters (layout indices 0 and 1, segment starts 0 and 20). The ROM's
  font draws 0 and O, and 5 and S, with the same segments.

## Mechanisms and behaviour

- **Trough.** Three balls are installed, at most two in play. A drained ball rests on the outhole switch (9); the
  outhole kicker (1) moves it into the trough, whose switches 13 (left), 12 and 11 (right) count the balls; the
  shooter lane feeder (22) kicks the lead ball into the shooter lane (14). The ROM feeds again 2 s later if trough #1
  has not opened. With an empty trough at power-up the ROM runs a ball search (4, 1, 2, 5, 8, 6).
- **Cash Register (left) ramp.** The B-11304-2 Ramp Elevator raises the ramp with Ramp Up (4) and drops it with Ramp
  Down (2); its microswitch (10) reports the position (the retained table closes 10 when the ramp is down). At game
  start the ROM pulses Ramp Down until 10 closes, six tries about 0.8 s apart. D-I-N-E-R raises the ramp so a ball can
  be locked; the lower left eject (50, kicked by 8) is the table's lock saucer. Left Ramp Exit (36) advances the cash
  register (lamps 1-5).
- **Right ramp, diverter and cup.** Right Ramp Entry (27) and Exit (28); the diverter (14, coil AE-26-1500 per the
  amendment) is energized as the ball passes 28 and sends it into the coffee cup, past Cup Entry (29) to the Cup switch
  (17), which releases it (3.75 s in the gameplay run).
- **Ejects.** Upper Left Eject (49 / coil 5) and Lower Left Eject (50 / coil 8); with the switch held the ROM retries
  every 1.5 s.
- **Today's Special sub-playfield shooter.** A drop hole feeds a below-playfield bell-armature shooter (6) whose
  switches 15 and 16 count the waiting balls; it fires the ball up a wireform, from which the upper left eject can be
  reached. In play the ROM fired it about 6.9 s after 15 closed.
- **Drop target banks.** Centre bank Hot Dog/Burger/Chili (22-24, reset 3) and left bank Root Beer/Fries/Iced Tea
  (30-32, reset 13), each on a C-13205 opto board. Public 1 is a dropped target; the ROM resets a bank only when all
  three are down (apart from the reset at Start) and retries every 0.5-0.6 s while they stay down.
- **DINE-TIME clock (backbox).** A stepper motor (14-7948) on Clock Wheel A (16) and B (15) turns one hand over a
  twelve-hour dial lit by lamps 49-60 and the clock flashers (31); the D-12045 board's opto (59) marks home. The
  Wheel Test homes the hand at 12 o'clock and steps it 001-191; in Manual-Down the flipper buttons step it by hand.
  PinMAME's smoothed solenoid state shows the stepper as long on-spans, so the retained table's 60-step
  `vpmMechStepSol` model on 15/16 with 59 at position 0 is the consumer contract that is known to work.
- **Customers.** Haji, Babs, Boris, Pepe and Buck are playfield cutouts at the front, each with a #555 lamp (33-37) and
  a #89/906 flashlamp (25-29, one playfield bulb plus one backglass bulb each).
- **E-A-T.** The top lanes 19-21 light lamps 22-24; each of these lamps has two bulbs, the second in the raised E-A-T
  sign at the back of the playfield.

## Manual notes

- The Switches parts list reverses the trough's left and right (#1 "left", #3 "right"); the Switch-Matrix Table and
  the switch drawing put #1 (11) on the right, where the feeder is.
- The Lamp-Matrix Table and Lamps list print Grill Bonus 250K and 500K for lamps 42 and 43; the ROM and the playfield
  inserts read 150K and 250K.
- The text before the Lamp-Matrix Table says the columns are on 1J6 and the rows on 1J7; the table itself prints the
  opposite (columns 1J7, rows 1J6), which the definition follows.
- The Locations Diagram labels the G.I. relay board "Sol. 10" and its parts list "Sol. 11"; the Solenoid Table and the
  ROM put the G.I. relay at 10.
- The Switches list gives the clock opto as "p/o D-12046"; the clock's Motor Control Board page prints D-12045.

## Evidence

- Manual: IPDB 681 Operations Manual (106 pages, 150 dpi with an OCR layer used only to find pages) and Amendment #1;
  excerpts under `evidence/excerpts/williams.diner.1990/`.
- Runtime: pinned LibPinMAME (`97aa922b`) runs of `diner_l4` (Coil, Single Lamps, Switch Levels, C-Side and Wheel
  tests, twelve power-up probes and a gameplay run) and of four other local sets; raw runs under the working root's
  `review-artifacts/williams.diner.1990/runtime/`. An earlier three-ball run settled the game-on output 23.
- Table: Flupper's `Diner VPX 1.2.vpx` (known working, `cGameName = "diner_l4"`), its extraction and a world-space OBJ
  export for the baked primitives.

## What would complete the record

  model.
