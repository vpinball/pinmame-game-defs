# Tales from the Crypt (Data East 1993) - Lamp bulbs and sockets, and the playfield major assemblies

Transcribed by hand from native-resolution renders of printed pages 36 and 39 (PDF 40 and 43) of the GameEx-hosted scan `Data_East_1993_Tales_from_the_Crypt_Manual.pdf`. The scan has no text layer, so every cell was read from the render.
