## Drawing on the right half of the page

The right half of the page is a top-down playfield drawing (apron/lower cabinet area at the bottom) whose numbered
black balloons carry the item numbers, each with a short leader line to the device. Balloons are not transcribed
by position. Balloons that sit OFF the playfield outline (in the cabinet/apron/front area, outside the rectangle
that bounds the playfield drawing): 11 (lower right, beside the shooter lane below the outline's corner), 12 (left
of the outline's lower-left corner), 13 (below the bottom edge of the outline) and 34 (inside a small separate
rectangle below the bottom edge of the outline). Balloons that straddle the outline edge (balloon centred on or just
beside the line, leader pointing onto the playfield): 72 (outside the left edge, leader runs to the left edge of the
playfield), 45 (on the left edge), 55 (on the top edge), 58 (on the right edge) and 25 (at the bottom of the
shooter lane). Balloons 15, 16, 17 and 18 sit on a slanted trough/outhole outline drawn inside the lower playfield
area. Items 14, 21, 22, 23 and 24 (the asterisked Plumb Bob, Slam Tilt, Coin Door Closed, Ticket Opto. and Always
Closed rows) have no balloon in the drawing; items 67-68, 73-75 and 77-88 (Not Used) also have none.
