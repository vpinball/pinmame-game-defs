# Black Rose — Mechanism Assembly Parts Pages, Table of Contents and ROM Summary

Transcribed from `Bally_1992_Black_Rose_Manual.pdf`, PDF pages 3-4 (table of contents), 19 (printed `BLACK ROSE 1-1`, ROM summary), 74-90 (printed `BLACK ROSE 2-16` to `2-32`), 98 (printed `2-40`) and 103 (printed `2-45`). Windows OCR with obvious character errors corrected against the rendered pages; every row was compared with the rendered page (300 dpi image-only scan).

Reading notes: columns are `Item | Part Number | Description` as printed; a lettered sub-item such as `a)` is a sub-part of the numbered item above it. Text is as printed, including printer's slips, which are not corrected (for example `Coil& Retaining Bracket` and `Soc. Hd. Scew`). Exploded-view drawings carry numbered balloons that match the item numbers and are not transcribed. Where a page prints a part number in a different form from another page, both are kept and listed in the inconsistency notes at the end of this file. There are no `[illegible]` cells in the tables; the one reading flagged `[?]` is in the separate cannon maintenance excerpt (service-tests.md), not here.
