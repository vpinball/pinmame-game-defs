# Black Rose — Power Driver Board A-12697-1 Connector List

Transcribed from `Bally_1992_Black_Rose_Manual.pdf`, PDF pages 117, 118 and 119, printed pages `BLACK ROSE 3-13`,
`BLACK ROSE 3-14` and `BLACK ROSE 3-15` (footers read on each page), the `POWER DRIVER BOARD A-12697-1` connector
list (continued on the next two pages under the heading `POWER DRIVER BOARD CONTINUED...`). Read from the rendered
page (300 dpi image-only scan), not from the OCR text.

Each row is one printed line, in printed order, split only into the connector-pin designator (column 1) and the rest of
the printed line (column 2). A line printed without a pin number (for example `J103 Not Used`) has the bare
connector designator in column 1. The blank lines that separate connector groups in the printed list are not shown.
Spelling, capitalization, punctuation and the mix of `-` and `/` in colour names are as printed (for example the
J133 row wires use `Red-Brown` while J134 and J135 use `Red/Gray`, `Red/Violet`; J137 and J138 use `Yellow/Brown`).
Printed oddities kept literally: `J101-5 Blue-White,  loop from J101-4` has two spaces after the comma;
`J102-9 Black-Yellow,16VAC` has no space after the comma; `J117-2 Gray-Yellow, +12VDCJ606-7` has no `to` or separator
between `+12VDC` and `J606-7`; `J127-7` and `J127-8` have two spaces before `to playfield coil`; `J136-3 Yellow/Gray , Col 8
to cabinet lamp` has a space before the comma; `J121-6` prints `to Insert` with a capital I. Small scan specks after
the `J125-3` and `J125-6` lines are not text. No cell is illegible. Page 117 also prints a board layout drawing (right
half) whose connector outlines are labelled J101 to J138; it is not transcribed. Each page prints the
`P.C. Board Legend` box, transcribed once below. No pin line printed anywhere is blank; no connector J122 or J124
or J128 or J129 or J131 has pin lines (each is a single `Not Used` line).
