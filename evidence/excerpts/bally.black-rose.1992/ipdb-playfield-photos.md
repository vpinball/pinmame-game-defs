What the photos show, read by eye from the images:

- The jet-lane "sword" on the right of the playfield (the inserts the Lamp Locations list numbers 12-17, below the
  arrow insert 11) carries, from the top: round inserts printed `8K` and `4K` (the latter visible at the lower right
  edge of `image-8.jpg`), further round inserts partly hidden under the wire ramp, a small round insert printed `1K`
  just above the sword's guard, the round red jewel insert, and a triangular insert printed `COMBO` at the bottom.
- The values therefore run 8K at the top down to 1K, then the jewel, then the COMBO arrow. Lamp 12 is `Jet Enter 8K`,
  15 `Jet Enter 1K`, 16 `Jet Enter Jewel` and 17 `Combo Shot Right` in both lamp tables and in the ROM's T.8 names,
  so the inserts carry lamps 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17 from the top.
- The factory lamp drawing (PDF page 99) prints the balloons beside the same six inserts in the order 12, 13, 17, 16,
  15, 14 from the top; its four lower numbers are reversed against the photographed legends.

Nothing else is read from these photos.
