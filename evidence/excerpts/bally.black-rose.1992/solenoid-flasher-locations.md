## Drawing balloons relative to the playfield outline

The playfield drawing on the right half of the page carries black numbered balloons with arrows. Balloons drawn outside (beyond) the playfield outline rectangle:

- Two balloons `25` above the top edge of the outline (consistent with the `(2)` on item 25).
- Balloon `2` (Outhole) below the bottom edge of the outline, its arrow pointing up at the outline.
- Balloon `1` (Ball Popper) sits straddling the top edge line of the outline, with its arrow pointing into the playfield.
- Balloon `17` sits on the left edge line of the outline (partly overlapping it), its arrow pointing into the playfield.

All other balloons (3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 11, 13, 14, 15, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24, 26 (two), 27, 28) are drawn inside the outline, although 19 and 20 are close to the right edge and 4 and 27 lie in the lower apron / outlane region. Balloons 7 (Knocker), 12, 16 and the `*` rows are not drawn (items marked Not Shown / Not Used). Two balloons `26` appear at the lower left and lower right of the large circle near the bottom of the playfield (consistent with `Cannon Flasher (2)`).
