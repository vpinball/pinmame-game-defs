# Black Rose — Solenoid/Flasher Table

Transcribed from `Bally_1992_Black_Rose_Manual.pdf`, PDF page 109, printed page `BLACK ROSE 3-5`, the
`SOLENOID/FLASHER TABLE` (solenoids 01-28, general illumination circuits, flipper circuits), with the `Cannon Block Diagram` printed below it. Read from the rendered page (300 dpi image-only scan), not from the OCR text. Compared cell by cell against the reprint on PDF page 2 (front matter, `Solenoid/Flasher Table` beside the `Jumper Charts`).

Columns as printed: `Sol. No.`, `Function`, `Solenoid Type`, `Wire Color`, `Connection`, `Driver Trnstr`, and `Part No. Flashlamp Type` (header printed on two lines: `Part No.` / `Flashlamp Type`). There are no separate Voltage / Playfield / Backbox / Cabinet connection columns. Blank cells are printed blank: the Part No. / Flashlamp Type cells of rows 12 and 16, the Sol. No. cell of the `G. I. Circuits` heading row and of the three flipper rows, the Solenoid Type cells of the flipper rows, and every cell of the one empty spacer row between the G.I. block and the flipper rows (shown below as an empty row). The `G. I. Circuits` line is a heading row (printed bold in the Function column, other cells blank). The flipper rows have no Sol. No. and no Solenoid Type. Typographic notes: the Wire Color cells use abbreviations such as `Vio-Brn`, `Brn-Org`, `Blu-Grn`, `Blk-Org`, while the G.I. rows print full colour words (`Brown`, `Orange`, `Yellow`, `Green`, `Violet`). Rows 23 and 24 (`Locker Open Flasher`, `Left Sword Flasher`) print Solenoid Type `Low Power` whereas the other flasher rows print `Flasher`; transcribed as printed. Row 03 prints `14-7965 20V` (two spaces between the part number and `20V`). There is no printed footnote on page 109 itself; the footnote `J1XX = Power Driver Board, J9XX = Fliptronic II Board` appears only on the page 2 reprint (see below). All characters verified at 300 dpi; nothing is illegible.

## Footnote (printed on the page 2 reprint only)

`J1XX = Power Driver Board, J9XX = Fliptronic II Board`

## Cannon Block Diagram (drawing below the table on page 109)

A small block diagram titled `Cannon Block Diagram`. Text labels it carries: dashed blocks `CPU BOARD` (connectors `J206` pin `3`, `J208` pin `5`, and `J211`) joined by a `Ribbon Cable` to `J113` on the dashed `POWER DRIVER BOARD` block (connectors `J107` pins `3` and `6`, `J123` pin `3`, `J130` pins `9` and `4`). Wire labels: `VIO-YEL` (J107 pin 3 to the `Cannon Kicker Sol. #08` coil), `RED-WHT` (J107 pin 6 to the common node feeding the `20V Cannon Motor Sol. #03` and, via the lower branch, the `Cannon Flasher #26`), `VIO-ORG` (J130 pin 4 to the cannon motor), `VIO-GRY` (J130 pin 9 to the cannon kicker coil), `WHT-GRN` (J208 pin 5 to the `Cannon Kicker Switch #35`), `GRN-ORG` (J206 pin 3, the other side of the cannon kicker switch). The `Cannon Flasher #26` lamp connects to J123 pin 3.

## Comparison with the page 2 reprint (PDF page 2, front matter)

Every row 01-28, every G.I. row and every flipper row was compared cell by cell (Function, Solenoid Type, Wire Color, Connection, Driver Trnstr, Part No. column). All data cells are identical. The only difference between the two printings is the last column header: page 109 prints `Part No.` / `Flashlamp Type`; page 2 prints `Solenoid No.` / `Flashlamp Type`. Page 2 additionally carries the footnote quoted above and a faint stray speck after the row-15 `AE-26-1200` cell (not text). Page 2 shows no printed page number in the footer region.
