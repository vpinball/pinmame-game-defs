Notable Features, as printed: "Flippers (3), Pop bumpers (3), Ramps (3), 3-bank standup targets (3), 2- or 3-ball
Multiball. Left ramp is a mechanically raised or lowered ramp which is raised at certain times to load the ball cannon.
Lower playfield has a below-the-playfield ball cannon (catapult-type) which oscillates when loaded, to be fired by player
pressing the Fire button on lockdown bar. The Broadside VUK kicks the ball to the center habitrail above the playfield
the and sends it to the flippers."

Design by: John Trudeau, Brian Eddy. Mechanics by: John Krutsch, Ernie Pizarro. Software by: Brian Eddy.

The page's ROM list names Revision L-1, L-3 and L-4 game ROMs and Revision P-1 and P-17 prototype game ROMs, and its
documentation list names the English Manual, the Operator Handbook, a Parts List and the WPC Schematic Manual (Revised
May 17, 1993). The manual's cover (PDF page 1) prints `August 1992` and `16-20013-101`, matching model number 20013.
