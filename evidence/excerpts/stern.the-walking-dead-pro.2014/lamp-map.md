# The Walking Dead Pro — lamp map excerpt

Source: `WD-PRO-MAN.pdf`, PDF page 19, crop box 0.06,0.08,0.95,0.90. The crop transcribes the Pro lamp-location drawing and typical lamp wiring diagrams.

The drawing labels lamp groups 1–80 around the playfield, including trough/arrow inserts, Well and orbit inserts, ramp arrows, jackpot and multiball groups, drop-target and slingshot lamps, and the lower apron inserts. The wiring diagrams identify a typical #44 bayonet lamp with RED-XXX lamp return and YEL-XXX column drive, a black LED-wedge option on a terminal strip, a #555 wedge lamp on an auxiliary strip, and the lamp-driver PCB connector/circuit convention.
