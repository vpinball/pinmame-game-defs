# FunHouse — maintainer-supplied playfield photograph

Source: `funhouse-image-A13.jpg`, a maintainer-supplied 500 × 375 photograph of the lower playfield.

The two visible Gangway inserts read `100,000` and `200,000`. The photograph independently confirms that the physical insert represented by lamp address 12 is `Gangway 100,000`, resolving the older operations manual's isolated `Gangway 10,000` lamp-location entry as a one-digit typographical error.
