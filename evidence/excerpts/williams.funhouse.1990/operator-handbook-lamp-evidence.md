# FunHouse — November 1990 operator handbook lamp evidence

Source: `Williams_1990_Funhouse_Operator_s_Handbook_November_1990_OCR_searchable.pdf`, PDF pages 7-8, visually checked against 250 dpi full-page renders and 600 dpi crops.

## Gangway award

The Lamp Matrix on PDF page 7 prints address `12` as `Gangway 100,000`. This is a second Williams-printed source that agrees with the older operations manual's lamp-matrix page and disagrees with that older manual's isolated `Gangway 10,000` parts-list typo.

## Steps lamps 54-56

The Lamp Locations drawing on PDF page 8 shows three separate bulb sockets in one vertical stack at the left-side Steps assembly. The callouts are `56` at the top, `55` in the middle, and `54` at the bottom. This matches the retained VPX table's three five-unit-radius hotspot objects `Top_finger_1`, `Mid_finger_1`, and `Bot_finger_1`; the additional `_2`, `_3`, and `_4` objects for each address are larger render layers around the same insert effect rather than additional physical bulbs.
