Source: `Stern_1980_Galaxy_Manual.pdf` (IPDB machine 980, "English Manual (may be incomplete, with paginated schematics shown in halves)", 35 pages, each a 300 dpi grayscale scan with no text layer), PDF pages 9-13, section `VII CREDIT/COIN AND GAME ADJUSTMENTS —`. Read by eye from zoomed thirds of each page rendered from the scan; every cell was checked against the render. The printed text is quoted below; dot leaders in tables are omitted.

Page 9, `A. Playfield Post Adjustments:`, verbatim: "Posts that control left and right outlane openings on playfield can be moved to make access to outlanes conservative or liberal for ball to enter. See Figure 2." and, as a separate paragraph: "Conservative entry will decrease playing time and scoring. Liberal entry will increase playing time and scoring."

Page 9, `B. Back Box Game Adjustments:`, verbatim: "Each game has thirty-two switches located on the MPU module, located in the back box, that allows play to be customized to the location. See Figure 1. Credits per coin, maximum credits, credit display, balls per game, match feature, high game feature, special award and melody are selectable by means of switches. The switches contained in four-sixteen lead packages numbered S1-8, S9-16, S17-24, and S25-32 for easy identification. The "On toggle" position is marked on the assembly. TURN OFF POWER BEFORE MAKING ADJUSTMENTS. MAKE ADJUSTMENTS BEFORE TURNING POWER BACK ON."
