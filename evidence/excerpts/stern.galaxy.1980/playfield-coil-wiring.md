Every coil except the flippers is drawn as one winding with a diode across it; the flippers are drawn as two windings, each with a diode across it.

Flipper section (top of the coil area): `LEFT FLIPPER` and `RIGHT FLIPPER`, each one assembly drawn as two windings in series between the supply rail and the drive wire, each winding with its own diode. An end-of-stroke contact (two small circles with a pivoting blade) with a diode symbol after it is drawn from the node between the two windings round to the bottom node, that is, across the lower winding of each assembly. No `EOS` text is printed on this sheet. Drive wires: `A3J1-8 (G)` to the left flipper's bottom node, `A3J1-9 (O)` to the right flipper's bottom node.
