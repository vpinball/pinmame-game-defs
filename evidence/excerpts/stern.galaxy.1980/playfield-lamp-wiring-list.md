CHICAGO 60614`, `PLAYFIELD` / `WIRING DIAGRAM` / `FOR GALAXY`, `DRN. CW.`, `DATE 10-11-79` (cut by the seam between PDF pages 22 and 23). The page is upright. The typed lamp list was read from crops at 400 dpi, no rotation, in four vertical strips.

The list is typed in columns headed `DESCRIPTION`, `WIRE COLOR`, and `JACK A PIN` over two sub-columns `LDA-J1` (the jack) and `NO.` (the pin). Some rows have a dotted leader from the description to the pin; the leader is only a reading aid and is shown here as `(dotted)`. Rows are in printed order. The wire from the feature lamp bus on PDF page 23 (`A2J5-7 (W-BLU) FEATURE LAMP BUS`) runs towards this list.

That is 53 rows plus the second row of the `SAME PLAYER SHOOTS AGAIN` brace (54 printed lines carrying a pin). Typed spelling is kept as printed: `JUPITOR`, `"A"2 KICKER`, `SPOT No`. A few rows carry a hand-drawn underline or tick over the jack text (for example under `J3` of `"G" KICKER`, `"L" KICKER`, `"L" ROLL-OVER`, `"X" KICKER`, `MERCURY` and `MATCH`); these are marks on the scan and change no character.

Pin collisions visible in the printed list, stated without resolving them: `J1` pin 8 is printed for both `SPOT "X"` (G-B) and `SPOT No` (PUR-Y); the `8` on `SPOT No` is overprinted. Wire colours repeated on different pins: `Y-G` (BONUS 2 on J3 2 and BONUS 30 on J1 7), `B` (BONUS 40 on J1 16 and `"Y" KICKER` on J3 26), `GREY-Y` (JUPITOR on J1 15 and MATCH on J2 1), `GREY-O` (HIGH SCORE TO DATE on J2 22 and PLUTO on J1 14), `GREY-B` (L. SPECIAL on J3 10 and TILT on J2 10), `PUR-B` (TOP 4,000 on J2 4 and 5X BONUS on J1 25), `GREY-G` and `GREY-R` as listed.
