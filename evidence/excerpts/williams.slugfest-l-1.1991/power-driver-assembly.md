PDF 12 identifies the fitted Power Driver Board as **A-12697-1**. It places WPC
circuit boards at the back of the cabinet, accessible through the backdoor.
These game-specific fitment facts are distinct from emulator generation names.

transcribed in item order. `[blank]` denotes an empty cell. Printed microfarad

normalized for readability; numeric values and designators remain literal.
There is no quantity column. Not Used ranges remain explicit.
