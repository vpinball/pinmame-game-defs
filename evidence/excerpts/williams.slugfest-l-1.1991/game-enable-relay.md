# Factory game-enable relay circuit

Operations manual PDF 118, schematic 16-9057, power/triac sheet. The native-scan
crop shows T7 through R208 (560 OHM) and Q99 (2N4403) to the relay coil, diode
D39 (1N4004), the literal **RELAY DPDT** symbol, **R FLIP RETURN** and **L FLIP
RETURN** contacts, jumpers W4/W5 and cabinet connector J110. It proves a physical
board relay circuit; flipper-return labels do not prove that SlugFest fits any
flipper or external load to those contacts. The assembly BOM separately says
**Relay 4PDT 6VDC5A VS**; both source readings are retained.
