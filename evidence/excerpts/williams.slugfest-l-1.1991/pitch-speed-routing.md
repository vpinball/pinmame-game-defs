# A-14381 motor-regulator circuit

Operations PDF 106/printed 3-4, complete circuit region. SOL22 BLU/BLK reaches
DRIVE1, SOL23 BLU/VIO DRIVE2 and SOL24 BLU/GRY DRIVE3. The resistor/inductor
network joins those speed branches to one PITCH MOTOR across RED and BLK.
J1 also carries RED/WHT +20VDC, and J2 MOTOR+ / DRIVE-; the third J2 position
is KEY. The three public outputs must not be counted as three physical motors.
