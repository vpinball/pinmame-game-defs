# Class of 1812 instruction cards 28290 and 28291 (Inkochnito reproduction)

Source: `Gottlieb_1991_Class_of_1812_Reproduction_Score_and_Instruction_Cards_Including_French.zip` (SHA-256
`9defdeb17247db5023d8afd87c02fd73126b49692ca988ac76e95af0d1560e49`), the IPDB file for machine 528 retrieved from
https://web.archive.org/web/20180814233009id_/https://www.ipdb.org/files/528/Gottlieb_1991_Class_of_1812_Reproduction_Score_and_Instruction_Cards_Including_French.zip
and retained under `manuals/by-machine/gottlieb.class-of-1812.1991/ipdb/`. Its
`Gottlieb_Class_of_1812_instruction_cards.pdf` (SHA-256 `af6e107d7b0e896555c9fc8b5b90a94c9b55086eb05ba3c74f41db5991d6404b`,
3 A4 pages, Pdf995 / GPL Ghostscript 8.15, 2010-03-31) carries the English cards 28290 for 3 and 5 balls per game on
page 1 and the French cards 28291 for 3 and 5 balls on page 2; page 3 is the reproducer's note: "28290 instruction card
(5 ball) confirmed. 28291 instruction card (5 ball French) confirmed. Score cards are available in a separate file." Transcribed from the PDF's text layer and checked
against the rendered page; the bullets and the two arrow glyphs are drawn, not text, and are written here as `●`, `▶`
and `◀`.

Each card carries, to the right of the rules, a small outline drawing of the playfield with leader lines from each rule
to the feature it names. The drawing names no switch, lamp or coil number, so it is not used for placements.

## Card 28290, 3 balls per game (page 1, top)

| --- | --- |
| Title | HOW TO PLAY / CLASS OF 1812 / 3 BALLS PER GAME |
| BAT-O-METER | COMPLETE WHITE TARGETS TO ADVANCE BAT-O-METER. SHOOT SPINNER TO COLLECT FLASHING BAT-O-METER VALUE. |
| MULTIPLIER | COMPLETE TOP ROLLOVERS TO ADVANCE MULTIPLIER. COLLECT BONUS AND LIT CHARACTERS TIMES MULTIPLIER AT END OF EVERY BALL. |
| EXTRA BALL | ● TOP ROLLOVER EXTRA BALL LIGHTS AT 6X. ● SPINNER AWARDS BAT-O-METER EXTRA BALL WHEN FLASHING. ● OUTLANE EXTRA BALL LIGHTS FROM DOOR PRIZE. |

| BONUS | BONUS AT MAXIMUM LIGHTS A CHARACTER OR LIGHTS SPECIAL WHEN ALL CHARACTERS ARE ALREADY LIT. |
| MULTIBALL | COMPLETE LEFT DROP TARGETS WHEN LIT TO LIGHT RIGHT RAMP FOR LOCK. INSTANT MULTIBALL FROM DOOR PRIZE. |
| C.O.F.F.I.N. | RETURN ROLLOVERS LIGHT RAMP TO ADD A LETTER TO C.O.F.F.I.N. COMPLETE C.O.F.F.I.N. TO COLLECT 2,000,000 JACKPOT. RAMP VALUE INCREASES BY 100,000 FOR EACH CONSECUTIVE LETTER ADDED TO C.O.F.F.I.N. |
| ENTERING INITIALS | USING FRONT RIGHT CABINET BUTTONS, SELECT DESIRED LETTER. ENTER LETTER BY PRESSING CREDIT BUTTON. SELECT "▶" TO SKIP AHEAD ONE SPACE OR "◀" TO GO BACK AND ERASE ONE SPACE. |

The 5-ball card below it on page 1 repeats the same text under "5 BALLS PER GAME" and the same number, 28290.

the page rendered at 200 dpi rather than from the text layer. The apostrophes are printed as typographic `’`.
