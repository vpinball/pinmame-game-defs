# IPDB machine 528, photo 12: "Mechanical Chattering Teeth"

Source: `image-12.jpg`, 800x600, captioned "Mechanical Chattering Teeth" on the IPDB page (photographer Grégory
Bousquet), retrieved from https://web.archive.org/web/20180816012334id_/https://www.ipdb.org/images/528/image-12.jpg
and retained as `manuals/by-machine/gottlieb.class-of-1812.1991/ipdb/photos/image-12.jpg` (SHA-256
`44532a4e48027c976b1f4a1b4159372c4a516f5e9582be6897fc6fd166df9511`). The photographer keeps the copyright, so the image
stays outside the repository; this file describes it.

What the photo shows, viewed from the front of the playfield looking towards the backbox:

- A set of red-and-white novelty chattering teeth stands upright on a black bracket at the left of the frame, directly
  in front of the playfield's back panel, which carries a white spider-web print.
- Above the teeth runs the black lower edge of the backbox, with two screw heads.
- To the right of the teeth and slightly further back, a dark red sculpted heart sits against the same back panel.
- In the foreground at the bottom, a yellow plastic with a wiring loom running to a blue-bordered sign seen edge-on,
  whose two legible upper rows read "Special" and "Extra Ball" (the Bat-O-Meter sign photo 19 shows from the front),
  and, to the right, two yellow flasher domes.

The definition uses the photo only to place the teeth toy (solenoid 12, lamp 2) at the playfield's rear edge, left of
the heart; no coordinate is measured from this perspective view.
