# IPDB machine 528, photo 13: "Mechanical Beating Heart"

Source: `image-13.jpg`, 800x600, captioned "Mechanical Beating Heart" on the IPDB page (photographer Grégory Bousquet),
retrieved from https://web.archive.org/web/20180816012339id_/https://www.ipdb.org/images/528/image-13.jpg and retained
as `manuals/by-machine/gottlieb.class-of-1812.1991/ipdb/photos/image-13.jpg` (SHA-256
`aefe59867fe91b002da2d8f2cd68d4525145f8bc95af5e11c0f544d725f22445`). The photographer keeps the copyright, so the image
stays outside the repository; this file describes it.

What the photo shows, viewed from the front of the playfield looking towards the backbox:

- A dark red sculpted heart fills the middle of the frame, standing against the playfield's back panel, which carries a
  white spider-web print.
- Above it runs the black lower edge of the backbox with a screw head; below it a metal bracket spans the frame just
  above the playfield surface, whose artwork shows at the bottom edge.
- At the lower left, a red lit sign with white arrows reads "...YPT" (the end of CRYPT), left of and in front of the
  heart.

The definition uses the photo only to place the heart toy (solenoids 11 and 25) at the playfield's rear edge; no
coordinate is measured from this perspective view.
