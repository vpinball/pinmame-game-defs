# IPDB machine 528, photo 19: "Illuminated Whitewood"

Source: `image-19.jpg`, 1200x1600, captioned "Illuminated Whitewood" on the IPDB page (photographer Philippe Thibault),
retrieved from https://web.archive.org/web/20180816012336id_/https://www.ipdb.org/images/528/image-19.jpg and retained
as `manuals/by-machine/gottlieb.class-of-1812.1991/ipdb/photos/image-19.jpg` (SHA-256
`e90123b1910729ff6854f9a6eb35f196f1e9a4f3ad154e13f65db5c2eab16e61`). The photographer keeps the copyright, so the image
stays outside the repository; this file describes it. Despite the caption, the photo shows a fully populated, lit
playfield in a cabinet.

What the photo shows, the playfield seen from the front over the glass:

- At the rear, against the back panel, the chattering teeth on the left, the sculpted heart in the middle and a spider
  print on the right; at the upper right a large sculpted hand.
- At the upper left, in front of the teeth, a separate sign with a blue border and five yellow rows reading, top to
  bottom, "Special", "Extra Ball", "20000", "10000" and "5000" (the three values are small and soft in the photo).
  Each row has two dark round marks near its left end, unlit in the photo. To the right of the sign stand a small red
  sign and two red domes.
- Two signs on posts, each with two bulbs on top: one in the middle left reading "Add Letter to COFFIN When Lit" and
  "MILLION When Lit", and one in the middle right reading "Add Letter To COFFIN When Lit" and "LOCK BALL When Lit",
  with a red "TO CRYPT" sign right of it and a blue dome behind it.
- Three blue pop bumpers, a "DOOR PRIZE" sign at the right, the insert field, two slingshots and two flippers.

The definition uses the photo only to describe the Bat-O-Meter (lamps 10-14) as a sign and the order of its five values;
no coordinate is measured from this perspective view.
