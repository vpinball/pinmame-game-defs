# IPDB machine 528, photo 24: "Cabinet - Front"

Source: `image-24.jpg`, 1024x761, captioned "Cabinet - Front" on the IPDB page (photographer Jean-Pierre Renault),
retrieved from https://web.archive.org/web/20180816012341id_/https://www.ipdb.org/images/528/image-24.jpg and retained
as `manuals/by-machine/gottlieb.class-of-1812.1991/ipdb/photos/image-24.jpg` (SHA-256
`13a4f52c37db19370aa0568e04e813adf3fa19bc0b9e2f61c73e775145c63963`). The photographer keeps the copyright, so the image
stays outside the repository; this file describes it.

What the photo shows, the front of the cabinet seen straight on:

- In the centre, a black coin door with a single red-lit coin entry marked "10Fr", "5Fr", "1Fr" and "PUSH TO REJECT",
  one coin return below it, and the door lock with a key on a blue ring at its right.
- Left of the door, a red button under the printed words "start game".
- Right of the door, two white buttons between printed arrows under the words "top five enter initials", and above
  them the plunger in its housing.
- Two cartoon figures printed on the cabinet either side of the door, the Gottlieb logo below it, and the leg brackets
  at both lower corners.

The definition uses the photo only to show that this door, made for French coins, has one coin chute; it does not show
which of the ROM's three coin inputs it is wired to.
