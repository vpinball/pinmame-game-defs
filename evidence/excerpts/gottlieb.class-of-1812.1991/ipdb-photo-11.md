# IPDB machine 528, photo 11: "Right Ramp"

Source: `image-11.jpg`, 800x600, captioned "Right Ramp" on the IPDB page (photographer Grégory Bousquet), retrieved
from https://web.archive.org/web/20180816012342id_/https://www.ipdb.org/images/528/image-11.jpg and retained as
`manuals/by-machine/gottlieb.class-of-1812.1991/ipdb/photos/image-11.jpg` (SHA-256
`d32d60dffb64bdf3e9ffa2512b86cb1695d14809ada1e6b5087665a8a9ec3f4a`). The photographer keeps the copyright, so the image
stays outside the repository; this file describes it.

What the photo shows, viewed from the front of the playfield looking towards the upper right corner:

- In the centre, a sign on two posts whose orange face reads "Add Letter To COFFIN When Lit" on the left and "LOCK BALL
  When Lit" on the right, around a drawing of a face with snakes for hair. On top of the sign stand two bulbs under
  coloured caps: a red one on the left, above "Add Letter To COFFIN When Lit", and a blue one on the right, above "LOCK
  BALL When Lit".
- To the upper left, a blue flasher dome on a clear plastic, in front of a large orange plastic with a bandaged face
  and a striped roll.
- To the right, a red flasher dome on a clear housing above a red sign reading "TO CRYPT".
- At the top, a clear dome on the rear plastic, against the back panel with its spider-web print; at the upper left
  edge, part of the dark red sculpted heart.
- In the foreground at the lower left, two blue pop-bumper caps and a lit insert column.

The definition uses the photo for two things only: to tell which of the sign's two bulbs carries which rule (the red
left bulb is lamp 46, R BB ADD LETTER, and the blue right bulb lamp 47, R BB LOCK), and to corroborate that a blue flasher
dome stands to the sign's upper left, where the table puts solenoid 24's dome `domeB`. No coordinate is measured from
this perspective view; the dome's position comes from the table.
