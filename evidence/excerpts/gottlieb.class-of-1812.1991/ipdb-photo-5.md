# IPDB machine 528, photo 5: "Cabinet - Front"

Source: `image-5.jpg`, 800x600, captioned "Cabinet - Front" on the IPDB page (photographer Grégory Bousquet), retrieved
from https://web.archive.org/web/20180816012329id_/https://www.ipdb.org/images/528/image-5.jpg and retained as
`manuals/by-machine/gottlieb.class-of-1812.1991/ipdb/photos/image-5.jpg` (SHA-256
`004db446ee40e6c80477964eae76fb7129684402e13b9b587daedc9cb8879db9`). The photographer keeps the copyright, so the image
stays outside the repository; this file describes it.

What the photo shows, the front of the cabinet seen from slightly above:

- In the centre, a black coin door with two coin entries side by side, each with a red-lit reject button, and two coin
  returns below them; the door lock is at its right edge.
- Left of the door, a red button under the printed words "start game".
- Right of the door, two white buttons between printed arrows under the words "top five enter initials", and above
  them, at the upper right corner, the plunger.
- Two cartoon figures printed on the cabinet either side of the door, and the Gottlieb logo below it.

The definition uses the photo only to show that this door has two coin chutes; it does not show which of the ROM's three
coin inputs they are wired to.
