Printed below the legend: `Note: Rows 1 & 2 and Columns 1 & 2 should be run to both the cabinet and the
playfield.`

The sheet and the ROM's Lamp Test agree on every wire colour and on which reference sits in which column and
row, but not on connectors. The ROM prints the columns on `J5/6` pins 1-6, 8 and 9 and the rows on `J7/8` pins
1, 2 and 4-9; the sheet prints the columns on `J9/10` pins 1-5 and 7-9 and the rows on `J4/5` pins 1-3 and
5-9. The table on PDF page 75 prints no connectors. The sheet's names are often longer forms of the table's,
such as `(43) Right Toplane Diamond` for `43B RIGHT TOPLANE` and `(44) Kickback` for `44B KICKER`. Two cells
differ in substance: `(37) 8 Ball Option` against the table's `37B 9 BALL OPTION` (the ROM's Lamp Test reads
`8 BALL OPTION`), and `(23) Jackpot (two)`, whose `(two)` neither the table nor the ROM prints.
