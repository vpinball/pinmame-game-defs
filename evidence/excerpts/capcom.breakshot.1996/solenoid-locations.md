## Location drawing P62600 (the committed crop)

Every callout is a filled black disc with a white number placed on or beside the part:

- Backbox box (top): `3` in its upper right corner (the knocker).
- Upper playfield: `16` at the left top gate and `15` at the right top gate; `31` on the upper left
  bumper, `29` on the upper right bumper and `30` on the lower centre bumper; `7` on the right drop
  bank.
- Centre pocket assembly: `25`, `26`, `32` across the three pockets, with two `28` discs above them, one
  on each shoulder of the assembly; `12` and `14` stacked at the centre post at the foot of the assembly.
- Right side: `11` at the upper right flipper and `8` at the bonus saucer below it.
- Left side: `13` beside the left drop bank.
- Lower playfield: `4` and `5` on the slingshots, `9` and `10` on the lower flippers, `6` at the left
  outlane (the kicker), `2` at the trough towards the lower right and `1` below the flippers at the
  outhole.
