The Notes field gives the cabinet as `220lbs uncrated; 22 7/8" wide, 52 1/8" deep, 74 1/4" tall.` and
the project number as `"PB-6"`. Its prose describes the playfield as one level with no ramps, a
three-ball cue at the centre with a hole at its foot and three scoops at its top, behind a large metal
plug that rises after each captured ball and that the player hits to knock the locked balls into the
scoops for multiball; a dot-matrix display shows the scores as reel numbers.

The Documentation list carries one file, `English Manual [Capcom Coin-Op, Inc.]`, 20 MB, linked as
`files/3784/Capcom_1996_Breakshot_Manual.pdf`. That file, retained as
`Capcom_1996_Breakshot_Manual.pdf` (21,318,109 bytes, SHA-256
`270a8c5ed8b8093696c242082aad47060ddffed7d6636cab95fb9e4cd6398e6d`), is the Capcom operator's manual
`PM00147`, issue 05/02/96, whose pages the other excerpts of this machine transcribe. Its pages are headed
`BREAKSHOT™`, and PDF page 67 is headed `CPU BOARD ASSEMBLY A0015405 PARTS LIST`, the MPU above.

The ROMs list carries five PinMAME romsets and two Capcom ROM archives, `ROM V1.05 Beta` and `ROM V1.3`,
the firmware PinMAME's `bsb105` and `bsv103` drivers load.
