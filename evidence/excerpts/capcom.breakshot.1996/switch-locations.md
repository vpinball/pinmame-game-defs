## Location drawing P62500 (the committed crop)

The drawing marks each switch with a filled black disc (white number) and each opto with an open
circle (black number), placed on or beside the part. Callouts as printed, by area:

- Outside the playfield outline, along the bottom (cabinet): `30` and `5` at the left, `7`, `8`,
  `1`, `2`, `3`, `4` along the front, `9` towards the right; `21` and `6` side by side at the right
  cabinet button.
- Lower playfield: `33` and `34` at the lower flipper bats, `35` at the outhole, open circles `36`,
  `37`, `38` in a diagonal row towards the lower right, `43` in the shooter lane, `41` and `42` on
  the slingshots, `44`/`45` at the inlanes and `46`/`47` at the outlanes.
- Left side: `73` (lowest), `74`, `75` (highest) along the left drop bank with `76` beside it.
- Right side: `71` at the upper right flipper, `67` at the bonus saucer below it.
- Centre: `65` (open circle), `77`, `66`, `78` across the foot of the centre pocket assembly; `68`,
  `69`, `70` on the three pockets; `58` on the lower centre bumper.
- Upper playfield: `57` and `59` on the upper left and upper right bumpers; `55` and `79` in the
  captive ball lane at the upper left; `53` and `54` (open circles) at the left and right orbit
  entrances; `60`, `61`, `62` in the top lanes; `63` in the bankshot lane at the upper right.
- Right drop bank, upper right: discs `48` (upper), `49`, `50` (lower) along the bank, with `51`
  beside them behind the bank.

The right drop bank callouts `48`-`51` are one lower than the table above (`48 NOT USED`, `49`-`51`
the three drops, `52` the standup); `52` itself is not printed on the drawing. The left bank's
`73`-`76` agree with the table.
