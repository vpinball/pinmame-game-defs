# White Water — Solenoid/Flasher Table, General Illumination, Flipper Circuits

Source: `Williams_1993_White_Water_English_Manual.pdf`, PDF page 118, printed
page 3-8 ("Solenoid/Flasher Table"). Transcribed from a 200 dpi render. This
single page carries the standard solenoid table, the five-string General
Illumination table and the three-flipper-circuit table.

## General Illumination

| String | Playfield | Backbox | Cabinet | Qxx | Wire | Bulb (PF/BB) |

| 01 Playfield Upper | J120-1 | — | — | Q18 | Wht-Brn | #44 (PF) |
| 02 Playfield Center | J120-2 | — | — | Q10 | Wht-Org | #44 (PF) |
| 03 Playfield Lower | J120-3 | J121-3 | — | Q14 | Wht-Yel | #44 (PF) / #555 (BB) |
| 04 Backglass Boat | — | J121-5 | — | Q16 | Wht-Grn | #555 (BB) |
| 05 Backglass Sky | — | J121-6 | J119-3 | Q12 | Wht-Vio | #555 (BB) |

Strings 01 and 02 print **playfield-only** connections; 03 prints **both**
playfield and backbox; 04 and 05 print **no** playfield connection at all.
This matches the retained script's `UpdateGI(no, step)`, which dispatches
public GI address 0 to `GIUpperFade` (string 01), address 1 to
`GIMiddleFade` (string 02) and address 2 to `GILowerFade` (string 03), and
has no case at all for addresses 3 or 4 (strings 04/05) — full agreement
between the manual's own wiring and the script's own address handling.

Left. There is no fourth row for an upper-left flipper anywhere on this
page, matching `wwGameData`'s own `FLIP_SW(FLIP_L | FLIP_UR) |
FLIP_SOL(FLIP_L | FLIP_UR)` declaration (no `FLIP_UL` bit).
