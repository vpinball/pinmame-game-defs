# Idleman Flight 2000 release documentation

The locally retained text files are `f2k_v33_modifications.txt` and `f2k_v3374_readme_first_modifications.txt`. The latter's internal heading identifies **v3347M, 6.3.2020** despite its retained filename. Idleman identifies Pure Penalty as the speech collaborator. PinMAME names the V33-era driver `flight2m` and the exact 3347M CPU and speech members for `flight2mb`.

| Topic | V33 family and 3335 release | 3347M release |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DIP 23 | ON always makes the center Special award an extra ball. OFF uses the Special type from 31/32. Outlane Specials use 31/32 in either setting. | Same division of awards. Tilt preserves extra balls. |
| Credits | DIP 19 enables free play. Maximum credits fixed at 99. | DIP 19 enables free play with credit accumulation. Adjustment 21 selects the 0–99 limit and zero enables free play without accumulating credits. Adjustment 20 selects 0–3 high-score replays. |
| Multiball | DIP 18 selects whether qualification resets the 5-to-1 sequence. The walker ejects returning balls during multiball and the game awards briefly doubled scoring. | Adjustment 22 replaces DIP 18's difficulty role with four settings, including a two-bank countdown. A second walker shot can briefly raise scoring to 3× before it decays to 2× and 1×. Quick Shot is disabled during multiball. |
| Cabinet drives | The documented replay-knocker setup requires moving the former coin-lockout lead at A3J2-8 (public 18) to the knocker. The walker remains at A3J5-12 (public 9). The author documents DIP 14 routing selection from subversion 39 onward. | Compatibility requires wiring that matches DIP 14. ON keeps the walker at A3J5-12/public 9 and moves the former coin-lockout lead at A3J2-8/public 18 to the knocker if fitted. OFF expects a swapped harness with the walker at A3J2-8/public 18 and knocker at A3J5-12/public 9. A DIP/wiring mismatch can damage the driver board. |
| Speech | PinMAME retains stock VSU U9. | DIP 17 enables attract speech, with game speech always enabled. PinMAME uses two 2716 speech images at U9 and U10. The author also describes a single 2532 at U9 with U10 empty for real hardware. |
| Service | Coin-door self-test enters lamp test first. In his released-3335 post the author identifies the revised switch test that cycles through every closed contact with a zero separator. | Coin-door self-test enters lamp test first. Adjustment 19 sets 0–3 tilt warnings. The author documents adjustment-specific use of the credit and slam switches. |

Both versions add a LAST skill shot, a 19,000 bonus ladder, B/O inlane Quick Shot, spinner counts and changes to scoring with balls locked. The 3347M readme also describes a boot check of the leftmost trough position, four walker steps when that position is clear, and an inhibited start until it fills. The rule and wiring descriptions come from source records `release.flight-2000.idleman-v33` and `release.flight-2000.idleman-3347m`. The pinned PinMAME driver registrations establish the emulator configuration. The retained runtime runs cover `flight2k` and `flightfp`. The modified archives were absent from the authorized ROM library.

The author confirmed the released 3335 switch-test change separately from the earlier prototype discussion. The NVRAM replacement-MPU combo list documents stock, always-free-play and V33 selections in rows 087, 088 and 132 respectively. It identifies V33 DIP 19 free play. The combo row selects a program image and supplies no PinMAME member checksums.
