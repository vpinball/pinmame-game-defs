# Factory construction and operating checks

Stern Electronics instruction manual, PDF pages 4–17 and 19–21. The score display groups are separate first through fourth player units and the credit and match/ball-in-play fields in the backbox. The instruction manual lists rectifier A-430, sound module C-605 and voice synthesizer A-720 and mentions speech during solenoid testing.

Playfield assembly chart (PDF p17) identifies D-580-3-1 (three-bank), D-548-9A (five-bank), A-721-L-1 and A-721-L-3 (stand-up targets), B-758 (ball launcher), A-611-R and A-611-L (gates), A-563-15 (spinner), and B-634-R-Y / B-634-L-Y (lower flippers). The parts list (p16) lists two Apollo lane kickers, a ball launcher, the individual drop-target coils, the five- and three-target reset coils, flipper coils, outhole, slingshot and thumper coils.

The pricing table (PDF p12) assigns DIP 1–4 to coin switch 1 (BLU), 9–12 to coin switch 2 (BRN-WHITE), and 25–28 to coin switch 3 (RED-WHITE). Pages 13–15 give the play, sound and score options. No assignment to DIP 23 appears on those option pages.

In the stock game, B-L-A-S-T-O-F-F, a correctly ordered 5-to-1 drop-target countdown and two balls held at the launcher qualify multiball. Loading the third ball starts the sequence. The main targets and each countdown arrow can be tracked separately. The two captive-ball kickout stages score separately. Switch 40 meters release of the next rocket. The scoring prose p10 prints launch switch 29 and describes countdown as 29–32. The dedicated p18 chart and p19 drawing show 39 for the launcher and 28–32 for the full five-bank. The wiring diagram p37 and VPX confirm those two roles.

PDF p19 places a shared B rollover at the lower left and two B stand-ups above the thumpers. It also shows 33, 34 and 35 under the apron. The number 34 is printed a second time by the right outlane although the detailed switch identification lists right outlane 37. The physical sensor types and position names follow the switch chart corroborated by wiring and the retained table. The out-hole diagram gives relative order but supplies no precise location anchors for the three hidden contacts. The p21 drawing places the kicker effects at the corresponding assemblies and identifies the flipper enable and coin lockout as off-playfield.
