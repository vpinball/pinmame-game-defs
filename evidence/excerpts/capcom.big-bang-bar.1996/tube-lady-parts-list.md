Notes:
- Item 1A - the row an earlier curation read off the exploded drawing as "a
  genuine coil" - is a **shaft coupling** (SM00221). The mechanism is driven
  entirely by the 1F motor (MR00108, the same part number the Solenoids, Motors,
  & Flashers table prints for Refs. 30/31/32) through the 1A coupling, the 1G
  motor shaft, and the 2E belt assembly that carries the 2F figure inside the
  2G clear tube.
- The exploded drawing of the same assembly (printed page 108) shows the 1A
  coupling mounted on the 1E bracket directly above the 1F motor; the cylindrical
  coupling body is what a drawing-only reading can mistake for a coil body.
