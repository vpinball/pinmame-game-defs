Notes:
- The mechanism's position sensor is item 2 **MT00501 DISC, ENCODER** read by
  item 5 **A0020000 ASSEMBLY, PCB, OPTO** -- the same single-slotted-opto board
  the Opto Boards page (printed page 76) documents with part SW00148 "SWITCH,
  OPTO, SLOTTED, .375, PIN-IN". This is the positive construction evidence for
  switch 57 (Alien Motor): the location table's Opto Receiver/Xmtr P/N cells for
  its row are illegible in this scan, but the mechanism's own parts list names
  the encoder disc and the opto board that reads it.
- Item 8 **MR00108 MOTOR, 12 VDC, 65 RPM** is the same part number the
  Solenoids, Motors, & Flashers table prints for Refs. 30/31/32.
- Item 16 SM00221 COUPLING, SHAFT is the same coupling part number the Tube Lady
  Assembly's motor sub-assembly uses (printed page 109) -- a shared Capcom
  motor-coupling part, not a coil.
- The exploded drawing (printed page 104) shows item callouts 2 (disc), 3
  (washer) and 5 (opto PCB) clustered at the motor (item 8) on the bracket
  assembly (item 1), matching this list.
