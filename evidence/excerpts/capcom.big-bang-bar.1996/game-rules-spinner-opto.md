# Transcription: Capcom Big Bang Bar operators manual, printed page 6 (PDF page 10)

Source: `manual.capcom.big-bang-bar.1996` (SHA-256 `5fc11391e3092298e31775fdff5944554fc78db2bdb9240aa39fa9eab5dabca5`),
"PLAYFIELD FEATURES" scoring page. Transcribed from the rendered page (this
file's companion image is a native-resolution crop of the same region).

The SPINNER section reads, in full:

> **SPINNER**
> - 110 score per revolution (**the opto spinner** is fast; about 200 revs per solid hit!)

Neighbouring headings on the same page, transcribed for context and because they
are useful mechanism/scoring facts:

> **CENTER LANE**
> - No score
>
> **INLANES**
> - Temporarily qualify mode (if not yet qualified)
> - 100,000 score, 2,500 bonus
>
> **OUTER ORBIT**
> - Gates are closed when no mode is qualified, to divert ball to star bumpers
> - Gates are open when a mode is qualified, to allow complete orbits
> - Scoring: 100,000 score, 5,000 bonus
>
> **INNER ORBIT**
> - See "Underground" under "Modeless Features".
>
> **SLINGSHOTS**
> - 110 score
>
> **STAR BUMPERS**
> - 7,500 score
> - When no mode is qualified or active, changes flashing mode
>
> **3-BANK & 4-BANK DROP TARGETS**
> - Qualify "Looped In Space" (see "Modeless Features")
> - Scoring:
>   - Each target down: 15,000 score
>   - All targets in bank down: 50,000 score
>   - Standup targets behind 3-bank: 10,000 score
>
> **1-BANK (DOORMAN)**
> - See "Underground" under "Modeless Features".
>
> **EJECT SAUCER**
> - Re-light FREE SHOT kicker
> - Scoring: 500,000 score, 20,000 bonus

Printed footer: "Page 6".

Note: "the opto spinner" is the manufacturer's own construction statement for
switch 25 (Spinner), whose row in the Location of Switches & Optos table has
illegible opto-part columns in this scan.
