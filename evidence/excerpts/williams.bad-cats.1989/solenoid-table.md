The notes describe ground-side coil wires, except the orange flipper-switch leads. The bracketed flipper connections are from switch to coil. A-side circuits pulse with 12 de-energized and C-side circuits with it energized. The bracketed A/C wire colors describe the switched relay paths from the auxiliary board. Relay-board numbers are C-11998-1 (4a), C-11902-1 (4b), and D-12247 (5).

The location drawing on PDF page 44 places the six single playfield flasher circuits and the 6C assembly on the board. Its Doghouse flasher callout reads 7A, although its own parts list says 7A is unused and places Doghouse on 7C. The drawing list also describes 14 as #906, while the test table specifies #89 and backbox fitment.
