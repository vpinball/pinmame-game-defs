The upper-left corner of the drawing labeled Backbox on PDF 43 identifies the B-10686-1 knocker immediately above the audio board.

PDF 47 lists the five under-apron parts as 01-3569 ball trough runway, 01-3570-3 upper guide, 01-3571-3 lower guide, 12-6557 baffle, and B-8039-2 outhole kicker. PDF 40 describes a plunger-operated pivoting eject arm, with the Doghouse kickbig arm using a similar arrangement. The same maintenance page identifies sling arm pivots and the blades of the two drop banks as service points.

PDF 36 describes Wheel Test 08. Player 2 shows the last known wheel position. On Player 4, H denotes the home contact open or the opto interrupted. A healthy count spans 1–200 and a missing home signal can permit a count up to 256. Auto-Up spins then waits two seconds. Manual-Down allows Advance to spin to the next counterclockwise position or Credit to step an idle wheel. Test error is displayed as 08Err when the program cannot find home.

PDF 37 identifies the following test as Cat Test 09. PDF 43 shows the insert-board motor panel and its two driven figures, separate from the master display board and feature lamps. PDF 33 names the cat and woman motor lines individually.
