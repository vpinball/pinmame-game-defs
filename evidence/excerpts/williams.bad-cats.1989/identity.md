# Bad Cats identity

IPDB machine 127 identifies the physical game as Williams Bad Cats, model 575, manufactured beginning November 6, 1989. Its production ledger lists 2,500 machines through December 22. The entry describes two flippers, two eject holes, a linear target, an eight-outcome playfield wheel and the animated cat and woman behind the glass.

PinMAME's Bad Cats family uses one System 11B hardware initializer for L-5, LA-2, LF-1, LG-4 and The Cat's Meow through 1.3. The publicly documented Cat's Meow installation replaces the two original program EPROMs. The author describes restoring the LA-2 style of linear target scoring and adding selectable rules, game adjustments and a ball saver to the L-5 foundation.
