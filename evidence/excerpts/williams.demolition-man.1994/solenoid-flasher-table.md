# Demolition Man — Solenoid/Flasher Table

Transcribed from `Williams_1994_Demolition_Man_Operations_Manual_English_OCR_searchable.pdf`, PDF page 102,
printed page `DEMOLITION MAN 2-46`, titled `SOLENOID/FLASHER TABLE`. The page carries three blocks: the main
solenoid/flasher table (Sol. No. 01-44, with the `*Note:` footnote), the `General Illumination` block (01-05) and the
`Flipper Circuits` block ((29)-(36)), followed by one connector-prefix legend line. Read from the rendered page
(308 dpi 1-bit scan), not from the OCR text. Blank cells are printed blank and are written empty below. No halftone
shading or other legend marks were seen in any cell; the only marks are the `*` on Sol. No. 37-44 (see the note) and the
parentheses around the flipper Sol. Nos.

The same table is reprinted on PDF page 109 (printed page `DEMOLITION MAN 3-5`); that copy is not a second source. It
was compared cell by cell, see "Differences from the reprint" at the end.

Normalization applied: the table is split into the printed column groups. Column headings are transcribed literally:
`Sol. No.`, `Function`, `Solenoid Type`, `Voltage Connections` (`Playfield`, `Backbox`, `Cabinet`), `Drive xister`,
`Drive Connections` (`Playfield`, `Backbox`, `Cabinet`), `Drive Wire Color`, `Solenoid Part No. / Flashlamp Type`
(`Playfield`, `Backbox`). The fourth heading is printed `Drive` over `xister`, the leading letter being clipped in the
scan; it is the drive transistor column and the cells hold `Q` numbers. There is no `Cabinet` sub-column under
`Solenoid Part No. Flashlamp Type`. In the scan the letters `rn` print so close together that they look like `m`
(`Brn`, `Grn`); they are written here as `Brn` and `Grn` throughout. Sol. No. cells are underlined in the scan; that is
not transcribed.

## Notes on the printed content

- The Function of Sol. 02 is printed `Bottom Popper` here and on the location page (PDF page 103), but the Solenoid
  Wiring drawing (PDF page 110) labels the same `sol. 2` box `Bottom Plunger`.
- Sol. 17 (`Claw Flasher`) and Sol. 37-44 carry Solenoid Type `Low Power` although their voltage connector is `J107-6`
  (the connector the flasher rows 21-28 share, and which the Flashlamp Wiring drawing labels `Red-White, +20V`), not
  `J107-2` (the low-power connector of Sol. 09-15, which the Solenoid Wiring drawing labels `Red-Blk, +50V`). Kept as
  printed.
- Drive transistor designators repeat across boards: `Q9` to `Q16` appear both on the 8-driver rows (37-44) and in the
  General Illumination rows (`Q10`, `Q12`, `Q14`, `Q16`, `Q18` on connector `J121`), and `Q9`/`Q11` also appear as the
  Fliptronic II flipper hold transistors. Per the legend these are three different boards (8-driver `J3`/`J4`, Power
  Driver `J121`, Fliptronic II `J907`/`J902`), so the shared `Q` numbers do not point to the same part. Kept as printed.
- Sol. 21-28 list `#89 (1)` as the Playfield part except Sol. 23, which lists `#906 (1)`; the location page (PDF
  page 103) item 23 agrees (`24-8802 ... Left Ramp Upper Flshr #906`).
- `Org-Grn`, `Org-Blu`, `Org-Vio`, `Org-Gry` are the Hold wire colours of the flipper block; `Org-` is the printed prefix.

## Differences from the reprint

Compared against PDF page 109, printed page `DEMOLITION MAN 3-5`, the Section 3 reprint, at roughly 0.7 of 308 dpi in
five full-width strips, cell by cell, for all three blocks. No difference was found in any cell text, any blank cell,
the `*` marks on 37-44, the footnote, the legend line or the heading structure. Differences in the page furniture only:

- Page title: `SOLENOID/FLASHER TABLE` on page 102, `SOLENOID / FLASHER TABLE` (spaces around the slash) on page 109.
- Heading over the last two columns: `Solenoid Part No.` over `Flashlamp Type` on page 102, `Solenoid Part Number` over
  `Flashlamp Type` on page 109. The `Solenoid Part Number` heading on page 109 runs into the right page border.
- Printed page identifier: `DEMOLITION MAN 2-46` (page 102) against `DEMOLITION MAN 3-5` (page 109).
- Typography and scale: the reprint is a smaller, differently scaled copy; the content cells match.
