# Demolition Man — Power Driver Board Connector List (A-12697-3)

Transcribed from `Williams_1994_Demolition_Man_Operations_Manual_English_OCR_searchable.pdf`, PDF pages 136,
137 and 138 (printed pages `DEMOLITION MAN 3-32`, `3-33`, `3-34`), the `A-12697-3 Power Driver Board` board
drawing and its connector-pin lists J101 through J138. Read from the rendered pages (308 dpi 1-bit scan), not
from the OCR text. Every listed connector pin is transcribed with its literal text; `N/C` is printed as
`N/C`. PDF page 136 also carries the board outline drawing (connector positions J101-J138, not transcribed
beyond the connector names and pin counts printed beside each header). The three pages are continuous: page 136
holds J101-J109, page 137 holds J110-J125 and page 138 holds J126-J138. J113 is printed as a single line (a
34-pin ribbon cable), not as individual pins. Spelling, capitalization, `sol.` abbreviations and the printed
`G.I.` are literal. The associated image shows the G.I. connectors J119-J121 (PDF page 137, right column).

## Notes on the printed lists

- J130 prints no pin for `sol. 6` or `sol. 8` (J130-3, J130-7 and J130-9 are `N/C`; J130-8 is `sol.7 drive, to
  backbox coil`) and J127 prints no pin for `sol. 16` (J127-9 is `N/C`).
- `J133-7 Red-Blue, lamp row 6, not used` is the only pin that states `not used`; the same Red-Blue lamp row 6
  wire is listed as `to playfield lamps` on J134-7 and the coin-door interface list (PDF page 130) takes it from
  `Power Driver Brd J133-7` to its J3-9 and onward to cabinet lamps (J7-2). The Power Driver Board list and the
  coin-door interface list therefore describe lamp row 6 on J133 differently (`not used` against `to cabinet`).
- The coin door connector numbering on this list is the reverse of the coin-door interface board's own list
  (PDF page 130): this list has J116-2 +12V to `Coin Door J2-4` and J116-3 ground to `Coin Door J2-5`, J119-1 to
  `Coin Door Brd J2-2` and J119-3 to `Coin Door Brd J2-1`, while the coin-door board list has J2-2 `+12vac` from
  J116-2, J2-1 ground from J116-3, J2-3 G.I. from J119-1 and J2-5 G.I. return from J119-3. Both readings are
  kept literally; the pin numbers differ, the wire colours and sources agree.
- Printed pin spellings that differ from the other wiring lists are kept as printed (`sol 17` without a period
  on J126-1, `sol.4` and `sol.7` without a space on J130-5 and J130-8, `D.C Motor Control brd` on J126-3).
