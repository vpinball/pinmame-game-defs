## Notes on the printed content

- Two balloons are printed `41` and the parts list has only one item 41 (`Elevator 2 Flasher`); the parts list has no
  second entry for either. Kept as drawn; the page does not say which of the two is the misprint.
- Balloons 17, 18, 41 and 42 are drawn above the top edge of the playfield outline, not on the playfield artwork; 41 and 42
  carry the `C-13337` assembly number shared with item 17, and 18 is the elevator motor.
- Item 07 (Knocker) is listed here with part `AE-23-800` and assembly `B-10686-1` but has no balloon; the solenoid/flasher
  table on PDF page 102 puts its voltage and drive connections in the Backbox columns.
- The second line `Insert Flasher #906` under items 17 and 21-28 is a separate lamp assembly (`24-8802`, no assembly number)
  listed beneath each flasher; the page does not say where it is mounted. The solenoid/flasher table on PDF page 102 lists
  `#906 (1)` in the Backbox part column of those rows.
- Item 18 `Elevator Motor` is listed with part `14-7993`, items 19 and 20 with `14-7992`; items 19 and 20 share assembly
  `A-16989`.
- Item 23 has no `A-` assembly number and carries `24-8802` (`#906`) as its main entry, unlike items 21, 22 and 24-28 whose
  main entry is `24-8704`; this agrees with the `#906 (1)` Playfield part printed for Sol. 23 on PDF page 102.
