# Demolition Man — Lamp Locations

Transcribed from `Williams_1994_Demolition_Man_Operations_Manual_English_OCR_searchable.pdf`, PDF page 99,
printed page `DEMOLITION MAN 2-43`, the `LAMPS LOCATIONS` parts list with the playfield drawing. Read from the
rendered page (308 dpi 1-bit scan), not from the OCR text. The OCR layer of this page scrambles the columns and
drops cells, so every cell below was read from the image.

The page heading is `LAMPS LOCATIONS`. The list columns are headed `Item`, `Bulb No.`, `Lamp Assy No.` (printed over
two lines, `Lamp Assy` above `No.`) and `Description`. The main list covers items 11 to 81 down the left of the
page; items 82 to 88 are a second block with its own repeated column headings at the lower right, under the
drawing. Right of the main list is the playfield drawing with numbered black balloons; below it the cabinet front
is drawn, with the note `Cabinet shown for reference only.` There are no other footnotes, asterisks or colour
columns. Blank cells in the table are printed blank (no dash is printed anywhere in the table); they are written
as empty cells below. The bulb type is printed as a `#555` or `#44` suffix in the Description column and is kept
as printed.

Every row prints exactly one bulb number and one description; no item has two bulb entries or two description
lines in the list. Items 86, 87 and 88 print no `Bulb No.` and instead print a longer part number in the `Lamp
Assy No.` column.

| 68 | Lower Rollover | "L" Rollover #555 |

Other differences are only the added bulb-type suffix. The parts list gives items 74 and 75 as `Not Used`, like the
matrix. The list carries no row or column information.

### Items not found on the drawing

- Balloon `13` (Museum Multiball) is not drawn anywhere on the page. The centre stack of rectangular inserts has
  an unlabelled rectangle between the one pointed to by 15 and the one pointed to by 12, which lies where a 13
  balloon would be expected; no balloon or tail is printed there. This is an observation about the drawing, not
  a statement that this rectangle is lamp 13.
- Items 74 and 75 are `Not Used` and have no balloon.
- Every other item from 11 to 88 has at least one balloon on the page. Items 11, 82 and 83 have two balloons
  each; 81 has one.
- Nothing is drawn for the backbox, backpanel or the claw mechanism as separate items. The balloons 61 to 65
  (claw items) sit in the upper-left area of the playfield drawing. The page carries no drawing of a backbox.
