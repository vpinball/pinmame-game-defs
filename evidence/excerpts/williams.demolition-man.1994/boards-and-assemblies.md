# Demolition Man — Parts Section: Boards and Assemblies

Transcribed from `Williams_1994_Demolition_Man_Operations_Manual_English_OCR_searchable.pdf`, PDF pages 58 to 97
(printed folios `DEMOLITION MAN 2-2` to `2-41`; the folio number is the PDF page number minus 56). The text was taken from the OCR
layer and corrected against the rendered pages (308 dpi 1-bit scans). Part numbers, spelling, capitalization,
punctuation, typos and the printed unit symbols are kept literal (for example `Asssembly`, `Contoller` where
printed, `Plfd.`, `Hair Pin Clip`). Drawings are not reproduced; only the existence of numbered callouts is
noted. Where a page prints a left-hand variant on its own line under a right-hand item, the variant is listed
as a row with an empty item cell and the text `(variant line, bold italic)`. Blank cells are printed blank.

Assembly pages that were requested in full are transcribed row by row. Page 59 is given in full. Pages 58, 60, 61, 63 to 66, 68,
79, 89, 91, 94 and 97 have title, part number and a short summary only, at the end of this file. Nothing
was marked illegible.

## Part-number disagreements inside the parts section (read before using the lists)

- The Elevator Assembly list (PDF page 80) prints `A-16909` as `Opto LED Assembly RTV` and `A-16908` as
  `Opto Photo Transistor, RTV`. The Ball Popper rear (page 86) and Chute (page 88) lists print the same two part
  numbers the other way round: `A-16908` is `Opto LED Assembly, RTV` and `A-16909` is `Opto Photo Transistor
  Assy., RTV`.
- Outhole Ball Trough items 15 and 16 (page 75) print the same description, `7 IR LED PCB Assembly`, for
  `A-17982` and `A-17981`. Page 67 titles `A-17982` `7 Ball Trough LED PCB Assembly` and `A-17981` `7 Ball Trough
  Photo Transistor Assy.`.
- The Ramp Diverter is `A-17241` on page 87, page 92 (`Unique Parts`) and page 95 (item 19) but `A-17421` on page
  96 (item 16).
- The flipper coil for the upper-left flipper is `FL-11630` (Red) on page 72 and page 95 (item 33b). The lower
  flipper coil is `FL-11629` (Blue) on page 72 but `FL-17629` on page 95 (item 44b).
- Control Handle assemblies are `A-18016-1` (Right) and `A-18016-2` (Left) on pages 59, 84, 95 and 96 but
  `A-18106-1` and `A-18106-2` on page 92 (Unique Cabinet Parts). The Control Handle Plate assemblies are
  `A-18142-1` (Right) and `A-18142-2` (Left) on both page 59 and page 92.
- Outhole Ball Trough is `A-16765-1` on page 75 and page 92 and `A-16765` on pages 95 and 96; the Ball Popper
  Trough on page 96 item 20 is `A-17823 Ball Popper Trough Assembly`, while page 92 prints `A-17823 Ball Popper
  Assembly`.
- Ball counts: page 59 lists `Steel Ball, 1-1/16" (6)` as `20-6500` and a `6-Ball Cashbox Assembly` `A-17445-1`;
  page 92 lists a `5-Ball Cashbox Assembly, Multiple` `A-17792-1`; the assembly page (PDF page 12) says
  `DEMOLITION MAN IS A 5 BALL GAME.` and the Error Messages page says `This game normally uses six balls`.
- Target assemblies on page 90 are `A-17795-6`, `A-17799-6` and `A-18018-4`; the Upper Playfield Parts Locations
  list (page 95) calls the standup targets `A-18017-6` (items 8, 20, 36), `A-18019-6` (item 11) and
  `A-18018-4` (items 29, 35).

Flipper Notes (printed in italic under a square bullet):

> 1. Each Flipper Assembly is mounted beneath the playfield, in conjuction with the Plastic Flipper & Shaft, and Flipper Rubber on the upper side of the playfield.
> 2. With the flipper, in the non-activated position, the E.O.S. Switch contacts must have a gap of .062 (±.015) inch. When flipper is activated switch must close.
> 3. Any adjustment of the E.O.S. Switch must be made at a minimum distance of 0.25 inch from the switch body.
> 4. Longer blade of E.O.S. Switch must be straight. Gap adjustment is done by adjusting shorter blade.
> 5. All moving elements of the assembly must operate freely without any evidence of binding.
> 6. Apply Loctite™ 245 when reattaching screws to the Flipper Stop Assembly, the Solenoid Bracket, and the Flipper Bushing.
