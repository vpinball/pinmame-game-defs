Notes (excerpt): "Variations between playfields can be seen in the three round orange inserts near the car crash feature and also
in the larger white rectangular insert to the left of them. The game in the flyer shows all four inserts are unlabeled. Other
playfields pictured in this listing show the orange inserts are labeled 3, 6, and 10 Million and the white insert is labeled
"Retina Scan Ready". One example shows the white insert is labeled while the orange ones are not." and "This game was part of
Williams/Midway's "SuperPin" line of widebody games."

Documentation listed: "Operations Manual (English, OCR searchable) [Williams Electronic Games, Inc.]" and "Parts List". The page
also lists game and sound ROM downloads (L-1, L-4, LX-3, P-2 prototype, P4 sound, L-1 and L-2 sound) and two home ROM sets (H-5,
H-6) marked "Removed by request of Planetary Pinball".
