# Bally The Shadow (1994) — Lamp Location Drawing, Ramp Rings

Source: *The Shadow* Operations Manual 16-50032-101, November 1994 (`Manual_Bally_1994_The_Shadow.pdf`,
IPDB 2528), PDF page 134, printed page 2-39, the drawing above the **LAMP LOCATIONS (continued)** list.
Read from the native 300 dpi render (2550x3300 px). Pixel coordinates below are on that full-page render.

The page carries two drawings: the main playfield drawing (right) and an inset (left) of the Battlefield
mini-playfield with the two wire ramps. The locations list on the same page names the rings: 81 Left Ramp
Left Ring, 82 Left Ramp Right Ring, 83 Right Ramp Left Ring, 84 Right Ramp Right Ring (bulb 24-8855,
assemblies A-19545-1 and A-19545-2).
