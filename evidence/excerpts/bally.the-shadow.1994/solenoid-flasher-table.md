The coil part and coil colour cells of each pair are printed once, spanning both rows, and are repeated
here. `NOT USED` is printed across the two cells of the upper left pair.

Printing notes, preserved literally: the main table prints solenoid 35 (MAGNET) on drive transistor
`Q2` and 36 (SINGLE DROP DOWN) on `Q7`, while the flipper block prints the same connectors J902-3 and
J902-1 and the same wire colours on `Q1` and `Q5`, and gives Q2 and Q7 to the upper right flipper.
