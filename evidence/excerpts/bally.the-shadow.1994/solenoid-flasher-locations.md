# Bally The Shadow (1994) — Solenoid/Flasher Locations

Source: *The Shadow* Operations Manual 16-50032-101, November 1994 (`Manual_Bally_1994_The_Shadow.pdf`,
IPDB 2528), PDF pages 137-138, printed pages 2-42 and 2-43, lists titled **SOLENOID/FLASHER
LOCATIONS** and **SOLENOID/FLASHER LOCATIONS (continued)**, with the **General Illumination Circuits**
and **Flipper Coils** lists on 2-43. Read from the native 300 dpi render. Bulb legend as printed on
2-43: `24-6549 = #44 bulb`, `24-8704 = #89 bulb`, `24-8768 = #555 bulb`, `24-8802 = #906 bulb`. Where
one item prints two rows (a bulb and a second bulb or socket assembly), both rows are kept.

Printing notes, preserved literally: item 08 prints coil `AL-23-800` where the solenoid table prints
`AE-23-800`; item 23's second assembly prints `A-12156` where 21 and 22 print `B-12156`; items 26-28
print `22-8802` where the legend decodes `24-8802` (#906). Item 09 (Left Slingshot) prints assembly
`B-9362-R-3` and item 10 (Right Slingshot) `B-9362-L-2`. The 2-43 drawing places callouts 24, 17,
19/20 and 15 on the mini-playfield inset.
