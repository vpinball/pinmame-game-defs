Printing notes: item 17 is printed `Left Lane` here while the matrix prints `LEFT RETURN LANE`. The
list and the matrix both leave position 83 unused, so there is no `Mini Right Standup 2` switch, and the
list numbers the mini right standups 4, 3 and 1 at 81, 82 and 84.

Drawing note: the mini-playfield inset of the SWITCH LOCATIONS (continued) drawing on printed page 2-41 labels the three right
standups 84, 83 and 81 from the rear and draws no 82, although the list above prints 82 and leaves 83
unused.
