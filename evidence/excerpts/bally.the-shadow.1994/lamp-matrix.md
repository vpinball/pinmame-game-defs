Printing notes, preserved literally: cell 62 prints `MINI LEFT STANDUP 23` and its number box prints
only `2`; cell 63 prints `MINI LEFT STANDUP 12`. The lamp locations list (printed 2-39) names 62 `Mini
Left Standup 3` and 63 `Mini Left Standup 2`, which continues the 61-64 = 4-1 order, so the grid
cells are misprints of `3` and `2`. Cell 36 prints `DUAL OF WILLS`; the game rules (PDF page 16) spell
the scene `Duel of Wills`.
