Printing notes: item 1's description prints "Right  Bracket, Divertor Mech." with a double space between
"Right" and "Bracket", preserved as printed (the mirror-image Left Side page prints a single space:
"Left Bracket, Divertor Mech."). The wire-colour label reads **RED/BRW** on this page (both occurrences),
not "RED/BRN" as printed on the mirror-image Left Side page (A-18954) — this is a genuine difference
between the two pages, not a transcription choice; both were checked closely at high zoom and read
unambiguously "BRW" here and "BRN" there. The "E"-Ring associated-part row prints its inch mark as a
proper `"` here, differing from the Left Side page's apostrophe-style `'` on the same row; both are
preserved as printed on their respective pages.
