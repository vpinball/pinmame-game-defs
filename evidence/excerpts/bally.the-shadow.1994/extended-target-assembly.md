Two harnesses are labelled with wire colours directly on the exploded-view drawing (not in the parts
table):

- Near items 23/24/25 (left-hand exploded view): **GRN** and **WHT**.

  inside the dashed detail circle showing the two-pin connector, **VIO** and **RED/BRN**.

Printing notes: no typos observed in the parts table. Item 4 in the table ("SM-30-1100-DC") is a
different, more elaborate part number than the plain "SM1-26-600"-style coil part numbers seen
elsewhere in this manual; transcribed exactly as printed.
