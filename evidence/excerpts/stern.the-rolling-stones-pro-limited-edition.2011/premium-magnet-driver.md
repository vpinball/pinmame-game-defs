# The Rolling Stones Premium magnet driver

Source: `Rolling-Stones-Manual.pdf`, PDF page 95, printed sheet Y19.

The `ROLLING STONES PREMIUM Magnet driver Board` schematic identifies the Q5 and Q7 input lines, 68 VDC supply, driver transistors and diodes, and the separate left- and right-magnet outputs on board 520-5316-00. This is Premium/LE hardware evidence and is not transferred to the Standard edition.
