# The Rolling Stones coil chart

Source: `Rolling-Stones-Manual.pdf`, PDF page 55, printed page 18.

The complete `COILS DETAILED CHART TABLE` lists Q1-Q32, physical functions, drive groups, wiring colors, connectors, voltages, and coil or bulb types. It identifies the trough kicker, launcher, lock, Premium magnets, left gate, shaker, pops, slings, flippers, Mick motor relays, flashers, posts, and optional coin meter; the page notes which outputs are Premium-only.
