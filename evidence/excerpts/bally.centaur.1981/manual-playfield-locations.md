# Centaur playfield switch and solenoid locations (factory manual)

Figure V in the Bally Centaur manual maps the numbered physical switch assemblies and solenoids onto the playfield. Circles identify switch assemblies and squares identify solenoids; the legend separately identifies cabinet, door, and backbox locations. This drawing is the placement evidence for the playfield devices, while the adjacent identification table supplies their printed names.
