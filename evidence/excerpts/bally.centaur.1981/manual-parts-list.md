# Centaur assembly coils and fitted modules (factory manual)

The Bally Centaur parts list identifies the fitted assembly coils and modules: coin lockout, two flippers, knocker, outhole kicker, two thumper bumpers, two slingshots, drop-target reset, individual drop targets, kick to playfield, magnet, ball release, and the lamp, display, solenoid-driver, MPU, speech, auxiliary-lamp, solenoid-expander, and GI-flasher modules. This is component and hardware-family evidence rather than a controller-address map.
