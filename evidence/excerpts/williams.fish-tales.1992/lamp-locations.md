## Boat Unit lamp cross-check

The Boat Unit Assembly parts page (`A-15109`, printed 2-31; see `boards-and-assemblies.md`) lists
a "5-Lamp PCB Assembly" (`A-15338`) and a "Socket & Bulb Assembly (4)" (`A-11271`). Those assembly
numbers match, exactly and completely, the five lamps assigned `A-15338` above (11-15, Hold Bonus /
Video Mode / Rock the Boat / Light Ex. Ball / Instant Multi-Ball) and the four lamps assigned
`A-11271` above (35-38, Right Boat Entry / Rt. Boat Feed Frenzy / Fish Finder(#44) / Lt. Boat Feed
Frenzy) — confirming all nine addresses are physically mounted on the Boat Unit assembly rather than
scattered playfield inserts.
