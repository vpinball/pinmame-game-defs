## PDF page 11 (printed 6)

- Maximum credits limits games accumulated through coining or replays. The match feature displays a random number in the Match/Ball in Play window and awards a free game if it matches the tens digit. High-score feature awards an extra ball or free game at each of two or three score levels, subject to settings. A new high score to date or score over 1,000,000 may award free games; the winning score becomes the new high score and appears on all four displays at game end.

## PDF page 12 (printed 7)

- SOUND OPTION: Self test #18 selects sound: “00” most switches associated chimes without feature background; “01” playfield switches associated chimes with background; “02” most scoring noise effect without background; “03” most all scoring noise effect with background. The printed note instructs turning both controls all the way up and back ¼ turn, then turning the front-door remote volume all the way up and back ¼ turn.
