# Switch 17: series contacts

The game's W-1192-29c playfield wiring, also retained as original `switch.tif`, shows
the normally-open single-drop target-down contact in series with the normally-closed
back-target contact. With the drop up, the composite circuit rests open. With the drop
down, a back-target hit opens the normally-closed contact briefly. Both contacts belong
to public switch 17; they do not create another public matrix input.
