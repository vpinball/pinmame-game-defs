# Embryon manual rules, PDF pages 6, 8 and 9

This is a factual paraphrase of the retained manual. The primary visually checked
pages 6, 8 and 9 against their native scans.

- Page 6: Power-up resets the drops. Credits start up to four players, and the outhole
  kicker serves the next ball. Tilt loses the ball and bonus and disables flippers and
  bumpers; slam ends the game. Operator options control game length and awards.
- Page 8: Completing A and B enables Flipsave; the right outlane starts the five-second
  outlane flipper. The top three-bank collects whichever feature is lit; drop and lane
  features advance bonus, multipliers, captive-ball values and memory.
- Page 9: Spelling EMBRYON lights the saucer for a lock. The single-drop back target
  can release the lock; a following player can release a held ball with the right button.
  The central captive-ball targets advance letter features, and the right captive target
  advances its values. Self-test positions 16–17 select replay, extra-ball or novelty awards.

The center feature distinguishes inside and outside contacts: completing all four
outside targets enables the spots-letter feature. Each subsequent inside-target hit
adds a letter; simultaneous inside hits add two letters and a bright flash. S21 changes
whether an inside hit lights one or two outside-target lamps; S22 controls their memory.
