Source: Bally game #1198 Viking manual, PDF page 21, printed page 17, Figure V tables.
Method: manually transcribed by the primary curator, visually checked against the native-resolution grayscale page.
Capitalization and printed wording are retained. `[blank]` means a printed number with no description, not a printed NOT USED claim. These are self-test numbers; the solenoid column is not the PinMAME public-address column.

The printed note states that the slingshot and thumper-bumper coils energize when their switch is made.

PDF page 22 (printed page 18), Figure V, separately locates the playfield switches and coils. The legend assigns switches 07 and 16 to the cabinet and 06, 09, 10, 11, 16 to the door; solenoid self-test 16 is in the door, 17 in the backbox, and 02 in the cabinet. The three tilt contacts share 07 and the two slam contacts share 16; their cabinet positions are not playfield coordinates.

PDF page 23 (printed page 19), ASSEMBLY ADJUSTMENTS, identifies leaf-spring contact assemblies and specifies a general open gap of 1/16 inch and closed wipe of .010 inch. PDF page 20 (printed page 16), section 6, describes closed/stuck contacts appearing in the switch test and reopening them to the same gap.
