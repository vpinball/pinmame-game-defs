# Viking rules and service behavior

Source: manual PDF pp. 7-8 / printed 4-4A; pp. 19-20 / printed 15-16; p. 36 vector drawing.
Method: transcribed by the primary curator, visually checked against the rendered page; Windows OCR only locates cells.

The four left inline targets advance bonus multipliers to 2X, 3X, 4X, 5X in order and expose a rear standup, which awards Special then 50,000. Completing them lights both 25,000 outlanes and changes the rollover-button Extra Ball/Special arrows. The right 3-bank advances values 2000, 4000, 6000, 8000, 10000; knocking down 1 then 2 then 3 pays tenfold. A and B light the two-way saucer collect-bonus feature, then specials. An unlit two-way saucer kicks down; a lit collect-bonus saucer kicks up.

The spinner starts at 10 points per revolution; after 10 revolutions it pays 100, after 20 it pays 1000. A warning sound precedes value reset and another spin during the warning retains the value. Bonus ranges 1000..59,000, with 20K/40K memory and multiplication; tilt cancels bonus.

The added VECTOR DRAWING FOR VIKING EJECT SAUCERS (PDF p. 36) directs the center saucer through the spinner to the left flipper. The two-way saucer kicks upward against the arch, downward toward the left bumper, whose return should feed it back into the saucer. The drawing states directional paths, not VPX velocities.

Diagnostics: one Self Test press flashes all feature lamps, two cycles display digits, three cycles coils while showing their self-test number, four tests sound, five identifies closed switches. The lamp test and coil test orders are separate numbering systems. Five physical six-digit display modules are fitted; the credit/ball-in-play module supplies two two-digit public callbacks.
