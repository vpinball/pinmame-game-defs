## Location drawing

The playfield drawing on the same page places every address from 11 to 68 and 86-88 on the
playfield; 71-85, the fifteen rows marked "(on insert)", are not drawn on the playfield. Notable
placements: 86, 87, and 88 are printed left to right above the three-bulb bracket at the rear of
the mini-playfield (upper right); 46 and 47 are the two eye circles and 48 the mouth circle on the
head face; 41-45 are the five skill-shot callouts stacked top (45) to bottom (41) along the
right-hand skill-shot lane; 64 is a leader into the left-centre wire-ball-lock area; 28 a leader to
the far-left small-wheel entrance; 11/12 lower left and 13/14 lower right; 18 the shoot-again
insert between the flippers.
