# The Machine: Bride of Pinbot — Solenoid Table (printed page 2-42 and front-matter copy)

Source: `the_machine_operations_manual.pdf`. The table is printed twice: on PDF page 110 (printed
"2-42", Section 2) and on the unnumbered front-matter PDF page 2, beneath the Jumper Chart. Both
were read from 300 dpi renders. They agree on every function, type, wire colour, first connection,
driver transistor, and part number, but the second connection column is printed one row lower on
page 2-42 than on the front-matter copy, and the part numbers for 27/28 sit one row higher on page
2-42. The front-matter alignment is the one reproduced in the main table below; the page 2-42
cells that differ are listed after it.

## Page 2-42 cells that differ from the front-matter copy

- Second connection column: 17 `J125-1 J125-2`; 18 `J126-2 J125-3`; 19 `J126-3` (no second
  connection); 20 `J126-4 J125-6`; 21 `J126-6 J125-7`; 22 `J126-7 J125-8`; 23 `J126-8 J125-9`;
  24 `J126-9` (no second connection). This is the front-matter column shifted down one row: on page
  2-42 the #89-only flashers 17 and 20 would carry insert connections while the #906-insert
  flashers 19 and 24 would not.
- Part-number column: `A-14423-1` is printed on the 26 row and `A-14119` on the 27 row, leaving
  28 blank. The front-matter copy prints them on 27 and 28, which the solenoid-locations page
  (printed 2-43) and the Relay Assembly A-14423-1 schematic on printed page 3-3 (coil driven by
  the BLU/ORG driver line) both confirm.
