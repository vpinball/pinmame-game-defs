## Location drawing: jet-bumper cluster

In the upper-right mini-playfield region the drawing prints `54` inside the upper-left bumper
circle, `53` inside the upper-right bumper circle, and `55` inside the lower bumper circle,
matching the list's Upper Left / Upper Right / Lower descriptions. `56` is printed at the jet-bumper
sling kicker below the cluster, `74` and `75` at the two mini-playfield exits, `73` beside the
mini-playfield entry at the top, `67` above the head box, `47` at the head entrance, `63`/`64` at
the two eye openings, and `65` at the mouth opening of the head face.
