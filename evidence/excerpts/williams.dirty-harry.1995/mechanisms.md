# Factory mechanisms and construction

Primary curator read service manual PDF41-42 (printed1-17/1-18),72 (1-48),98-104 (2-26..2-32),110-111 (2-38..2-39),126-127 (3-6/3-7),131-132 (3-11/3-12). Factual summary; exact small assembly tables are retained externally in the native page renders. This is a summary, not a transcription of all prose.

- T.16 tests right-loop switch and magnet: rolling a ball up the right loop closes R. MAG OPTO.; the magnet catches and releases it.
- T.17: Up starts continuous gun rotation; Down stops it. The position switch closes when the gun points almost at the backbox and opens before counter-clockwise travel. A ball in the chamber is gently kicked out. Automatic motor stop is about20seconds.
- T.18: trigger requests a ball, trapdoor opens, autoplunger feeds the mini-trough, both GUN POPPER and GUN LOCKUP close, the ball is popped into the gun and gently kicked out. These are two ball sensors, not interchangeable motor-position sensors.
- T.19 ejects under-playfield balls; trough balls continually re-eject if returned to the outhole during the test.
- Maintenance1-48 explicitly describes Fliptronic II EOS contacts as normally open, closing with the energized flipper. Failure does not damage the winding and is reported while play continues. Playfield blade contacts have a nominal1/16inch gap; leaf construction is established separately from emulator normalization.
