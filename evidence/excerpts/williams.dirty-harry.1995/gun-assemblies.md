service-manual PDF98-101 (2-26..2-29). Line wrapping and table delimiters are
normalized. Inch quotation marks are spelled out as `inch`; typographic quote
marks around E Ring are omitted. Associated/non-shown items remain distinguished
from drawn items. No part number is completed from a repeated pattern.
