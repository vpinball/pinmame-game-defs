# Jack*Bot — Power Driver Board Connectors

Transcribed from `Williams_1995_Jack_Bot_English_Manual.pdf` (SHA-256
`8295268601bbd4379917de2003b44ab56abc260b334f83c345d75ed50fe2ff94`): the `Power Driver Board Assembly A-12697-3`
connector list, which runs over three printed pages: `3-28` (PDF page 146; the board layout drawing and connectors
J101-J113), `3-29` (PDF page 147, headed `Power Driver Board Continued...`; J114-J132) and `3-30` (PDF page 148, headed
`Power Driver Board Continued...`; J133-J138). The pages print the list as text lines `connector-pin  wire colour,
function`; each line is given here as one table row, the printed text after the pin kept literally in the `Printed
text` column. Blank lines between connectors on the page are not rows. Read from the rendered pages (300 dpi scan) by
the curator, not from the OCR text. Every line was read from 1.5x-1.7x crops.

The board layout drawing on `3-28` prints the connector designators (J101-J138 with pin-1 / last-pin markers on some)
in their physical positions around the board; it carries no wire or function labels. On `3-28` the list is in two
columns (left: J101-J105; right: J106-J113). On `3-29` the left column holds J114-J121 and the right column
J122-J132; `3-30` is a single column.

The wire-colour names are printed in full words (Red, Blue-White, White-Red, Black-Yellow, Gray-Green, Violet-Brown,
and so on), not the `RED-GRN` abbreviations of the Solenoid/Flashlamp Table. `Key` is a keyed (absent) position.

## Observations (typos and anomalies as printed)

- `J101-2` is printed `from transformer secondary`; every other secondary line says `xformer secondary`.
- `J104-1` / `J104-2`: the two White-Blue 50Vac pins go to Fliptronic II Board `J901-3` and `J901-1`, in that order.
- `J112-4` prints `Keys` (plural); `J112-5` prints `9.8VAC` with capital `AC`, the others `9.8Vac`.
- `J114-7` has a stray `;.` before `J904-4, 5` (`J3-4, 5;. J904-4, 5`).
- `J114-1` and `J114-2` print the identical text (as do `J114-3`/`J114-4`, and J115-2/J115-3, J115-5/J115-6, J115-7/J115-8,
  J115-11/J115-12). They are listed as printed; the page does not say the pairs are paralleled.
- `J119-1` prints `Coin Door BrdJ2-5` without a space.
- `J121-1` prints `Brown, return, G.I. to` with no destination (the line ends after `to`).
- `J121-5` prints `G.I. to insert panel` although the other `J121` return and 6.8Vac pins print `G.I. to playfield`; the
  other `J120` pins all print `G.I. to insert panel`. The Solenoid/Flashlamp Table calls the `J120` connector the
  `PLAYFIELD` voltage column and `J121` the `BACKBOX` column of the same G.I. strings, which this list does not
  reproduce (it labels `J120` as insert panel and `J121` as playfield).
- `J122-4` prints `Blue-Yellow, solenoid 28 drive to flashlamp`; the Solenoid/Flashlamp Table lists solenoid 28 as
  `VISOR MOTOR` (`Gen. Purpose`, part `14-8023`), not a flashlamp. Likewise `J122-1` to `J122-3` (solenoids 25-27)
  print `to flashlamp`, consistent with the table's `#906` bulb cells.
- `J127-8` and `J127-9` (solenoids 15 and 16) print `drive to flashlamps` (plural) and sit on the coil connector `J127`;
  the table types 15 and 16 as `Low Power` with `#906` bulbs.
- `J130-2` prints `N/C`, but the Solenoid/Flashlamp Table lists solenoid 02 (`NOT USED`) with drive connection `J130-2`
  and wire `VIO-RED`; the connector list gives no wire colour or solenoid number for that pin.
- `J130-8` `Violet-Black, solenoid 7 drive to coil` agrees with the table's backbox knocker drive `J130-8`.
- `J107-2` is `+50V to coils` on `Red-Brown` and `J107-3` `+50V to coils` on `Red-Black`; the table uses `J107-2` for
  high-power solenoids (and the backbox knocker) and `J107-3` for low-power solenoids; `J107-6` (`+20V to flashlamps`)
  for flashlamps and the `Gen. Purpose` rows 25-27.
- The page prints the lamp matrix on `J134`/`J138` (playfield) and `J135`/`J136` (cabinet: rows 6-8 and column 8 only).
- `J112` (5 pins, `White-Green 9.8Vac`) has no stated destination.

## Uncertain readings

None. Every line was clearly legible.
