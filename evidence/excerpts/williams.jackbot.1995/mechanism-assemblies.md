Printed as shown: item 18 `(1 0)` with a gap, item 28 `114` (for `1-1/4`), item 29 `B-32`, item 32 `1 N4004`. The
facing page `2-22` (`Visor, Targets & Motor`) draws the three assemblies together: the visor on its pivot brackets,
the five-target moving bank, and the motor bracket with its cam and two micro-switches.

The two `Mini Micro Switch` parts (item 34, `5647-12694-06`) are the visor position switches; the Switch Locations
list prints `5647-12693-06` for F5 `Visor Is Closed` and F7 `Visor Is Open`.
