## Observations on the printed text

- Bulb numbers: `24-8768` (#555 bulb) is printed on the arrow, numbered-row (1-5), card (61-65), mini-playfield
  (71-76) and 81-84 lamps; `24-6549` (#44 bulb) is printed on 17, 18, 27, 28, 37, 38, 47, 48, 57, 58, 66, 67, 68,
  77, 78, 85, 86. Items 87 and 88 (the buy-in and start button lamps) print `-----` as bulb and an assembly number
  of the `20-9663-` type, the same part-number family the switch list prints for the start/buy-extra-ball buttons
  (`20-9663-1` for 13 `Start Button`, `20-9663-18` for 23 `Buy Extra Ball`).
- Lamp assembly numbers: `A-20079` on all the colour-row and arrow lamps; `A-17835` on 17, 18, 27, 28, 38, 47, 48, 57,
  58, 66, 67, 86; `A-17807` on 37, 68, 77, 78, 85; `A-20125` on 61-65; `A-19035` on 71-74; `A-17272` on 75, 76;
  `A-20174` on 81-84; `20-9663-18`, `20-9663-1` on 87, 88.
- The mini-playfield lamp descriptions (71-74) differ from the matrix (`lamp-matrix.md`): the list prints 71 `Light Ex.
  Ball (Mini Plfd)`, 73 `Cashier (Mini Plfd)` and 74 `25 Million (Mini Plfd)`, where the matrix prints 71 `CASHIER
  MINI-PLFD`, 73 `LIGHT EX. BALL MINI-PLFD` and 74 `JACK•BOT MINI-PLFD`; 72 `Mega Ramp (Mini Plfd)` agrees. All other
  descriptions agree with the matrix apart from capitalisation and punctuation (`Jack•Bot (Target)`, `Jack•Bot
  (Ramp)`, `Hit Me`, `Casino Run`, `Buy-In Button`).
- The word `Plfd` is printed as `Plfd` (not `PLFD`) in this list; `Ex.` carries a full stop.
- Possible typographical errors: none found that change a reading. The assembly `A-17807` (37, 68, 77, 78, 85) and
  `A-17835` differ by only the last two digits; both are printed as shown.

## Drawing (printed page 2-35)

The drawing, right of the list, has two parts: a large circular inset at the top (an enlarged view of the lamps in
the central insert area) and the playfield plan view below it, joined by a heavy vertical line from the bottom of
the inset down the centre of the playfield to the lamp array. All numbers were read on enlargements.

Inset (the circle, top right of the page): 30 numbers printed inside lamp outlines in a 5-column grid. Top row, five
tapered (arrow-shaped) outlines labelled `11`, `21`, `31`, `41`, `51` (left to right); beneath them five rows of
five rounded-square outlines labelled, row by row, `12 22 32 42 52`, `13 23 33 43 53`, `14 24 34 44 54`, `15 25 35
45 55`, `16 26 36 46 56`. That is lamps 11-16, 21-26, 31-36, 41-46 and 51-56, one column per colour (yellow, blue,
amber, green, red), arrows at the top, `1 (High)` to `5 (Low)` going down.

Playfield, circled balloons with leader lines:

- Left of the playfield, top to bottom: `81`, `82`, `83` (three balloons at the upper left), `75`, `76` (further
  left and slightly lower), `84`, `85`, `66`, `86`, `77`, `78`, `68` (a column down the left side, `77`, `78` and `68`
  with leader lines to the three square lamp outlines beside the left lane), then `17`, `18`, `27`, `28`, `37` (lower
  left; leader lines running right to round lamp outlines in the lower playfield).
- Right of the playfield, top to bottom: `67`, `48`, `57`, `58`, `47`, `38` (circled, leader lines running left into
  the lower right part of the playfield).
- Outside the right edge, a vertical stack of four circled numbers `71`, `72`, `73`, `74`; only `71` has a leader
  line (running left into the right-hand part of the playfield); `72`, `73` and `74` have no leader line.
- Below the playfield outline, circled and without leader lines: `87` (lower left) and `88` (lower right).

Uncircled labels, small type with short pointer ticks: `61`, `62`, `63`, `64`, `65` (printed above each of the five
slanted lamp outlines in an arc at the lower centre of the playfield, in the order 61 left to 65 right; `63` is
printed below its outline).

That is 29 circled balloons (`81, 82, 83, 75, 76, 84, 85, 66, 86, 77, 78, 68, 17, 18, 27, 28, 37, 67, 48, 57, 58, 47,
38, 71, 72, 73, 74, 87, 88`), the 30 numbers in the inset and the five labels `61-65`: all 64 list items appear
somewhere on the drawing, each once.
