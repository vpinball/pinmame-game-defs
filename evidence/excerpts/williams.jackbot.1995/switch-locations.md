## Observations on the printed text

- The matrix (`switch-matrix.md`) calls F2, F4, F6 and F8 `... Opto` (`Lower Right Opto`, `Lower Left Opto`, `Upper
  Right Opto`, `Upper Left Opto`; shaded cells) and F1, F3 `E.O.S.`; this list calls F2 and F4 `Lwr Right Flipper
  Cab.` and `Lwr Left Flipper Cab.` (part `A-17316`) and lists F6 and F8 as `NOT USED` with no part number. The matrix
  prints a wire, connector pin and label for F6 and F8 (`Black-Yellow J905-3 Upper Right Opto`, `Black-Blue J905-5
  Upper Left Opto`).
- The matrix prints F5 `Visor Closed` and F7 `Visor Open`; this list prints `Visor Is Closed` and `Visor Is Open`
  (same part `5647-12693-06`).
- The matrix prints `TROUGH 4 LEFT` for cell 35 and `TROUGH 1 (RIGHT)` for cell 32; the list prints `Trough 4` and
  `Trough 1 (Right)`.
- Items 36 and 37 share `5647-12693-11`; items 46, 47, 48 share `5647-12693-43`; 25-28 and 58 share
  `5647-12693-19`; 56 and 57 share `5647-12133-08`; 61-63 share `A-12030-3`; 11, 12, 66 and the `(score)` lines of
  64 and 65 share `SW-1A-120`; 16-18 share `A-13609`; 38 and 55 share `A-18605-4`; 54 and 67 share `A-18605-2`.
- Visor switches 42-45 print `SW-1A-163-1`, `-4`, `-2`, `-3` in the order 42, 43, 44, 45 (the suffixes are not
  in numerical order); 41 prints `SW-1A-161`.
- The `A-17316` in F2 has a smudge over its first characters on the scan (`A-` is partly obscured); the reading
  `A-17316` matches F4's identical text.
- The list prints `20-9663-1` for 13 and `20-9663-18` for 23; the lamp list (`lamp-locations.md`) prints
  `20-9663-1` for lamp 88 `Start Button` and `20-9663-18` for lamp 87 `Buy-In Button`.
- Possible typographical points: none were found that change a reading. The description `Lwr` is an abbreviation as
  printed.
