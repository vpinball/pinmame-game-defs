# Jack*Bot — Solenoid/Flashlamp Table

Transcribed from `Williams_1995_Jack_Bot_English_Manual.pdf` (SHA-256
`8295268601bbd4379917de2003b44ab56abc260b334f83c345d75ed50fe2ff94`), PDF page 116, printed page `2-38`: the
`Solenoid/Flashlamp Table` with its main table (solenoids 01-28 and the unused 37-44), the General Illumination
block (strings 01-05), the Flipper Circuits block (29-36), the legend line, and the printed folio `2-38`. The page
prints no motor or visor block of its own: the visor motor is main-table row 28. Read from the rendered page
(300 dpi scan) by the curator, not from the OCR text. Method: every cell read from 1.6x crops of the rendered page.

Main table columns, in print order: `SOL. NO.`, `FUNCTION`, `SOLENOID TYPE`, `VOLTAGE CONNECTIONS` (sub-columns
`PLAYFIELD`, `BACKBOX`, `CABINET`), `DRIVE XISTOR`, `DRIVE CONNECTIONS` (sub-columns `PLAYFIELD`, `BACKBOX`,
`CABINET`), `DRIVE WIRE`, `SOLENOID PART NUMBER / FLASHLAMP TYPE` (sub-columns `PLAYFIELD`, `BACKBOX`).
`(blank)` is a cell with nothing printed. The `CABINET` columns print nothing in any main-table row (they carry
entries only in G.I. row 05 below).

Notes on this block:

- Row 02 prints `NOT USED` as the function but carries a full set of voltage connection (`J107-2`), drive transistor
  (`Q80`), drive connection (`J130-2`) and wire colour (`VIO-RED`); only its part-number cell is blank.
- Row 07 `KNOCKER` is the only main row whose connections sit in the `BACKBOX` columns (voltage `J107-2`, drive
  `J130-8`, part `AE-23-800`); its `PLAYFIELD` cells are blank.
- Row 14 `DROP RAMP` prints `SM1-26-600` in the part cell (the only `SM1-` part).
- Rows 15 and 16 are typed `Low Power` but carry a `#906` flashlamp type and a `FLSHR(2)` function; rows 25-27 are
  typed `Gen. Purpose` but also carry `#906`; the printed `(2)` after `FLSHR` in rows 15-16 is part of the function
  text (the two parenthesised digits are not explained anywhere on this page).
- The `#` of `#906` prints as a slashed `#` glyph in the scan, in every row.
- The row `29-36` / `SEE FLIPPER CKTS` has a type cell and every other cell blank; the flippers are in the Flipper
  Circuits block below.
- Rows 37-44 print a type (`Low Power`), a drive transistor and a wire colour only; all voltage, connection and
  part cells are blank.
- Function names are printed exactly as shown, including the abbreviations `FLSHR`, `PNL`, `LT`, `RT`.
- In the right half of the page a dashed horizontal line runs across the part-number columns at the boundary between
  rows 23 and 24 (in the 2-38 scan only; it is absent on the `3-5` and inside-cover reprints). It is a scan/print
  artefact, not a cell entry.
- The Drive Xistor numbers do not run in solenoid order: `Q28`, `Q30`, `Q34`, `Q32` for 21-24, and `Q26`, `Q24`, `Q22`,
  `Q20` for 25-28. They are transcribed as printed.

Layout notes:

- `LOWER RIGHT FLIPPER` and `LOWER LEFT FLIPPER` are printed once on the lower (Hold) line of their pair and the
  upper (Power) line's Function cell is blank. For rows 33-36 the Function cells are two separate lines: `NOT USED`
  on the Power line (33, 35) and `UPPER RIGHT FLIPPER` / `UPPER LEFT FLIPPER` on the Hold line (34, 36). So the Power
  line of each upper-flipper pair is printed `NOT USED` while its Hold line carries the flipper name. Read as one
  two-line label per pair, as the lower pairs are, it says `NOT USED` / `UPPER RIGHT FLIPPER` and `NOT USED` /
  `UPPER LEFT FLIPPER`: the coil column's merged `NOT USED` and the Flipper Circuit Diagram's note (`THE UPPER RIGHT
  AND UPPER LEFT FLIPPERS ARE NOT USED`) agree that neither upper flipper is fitted.
- `FL-11630` / `RED` are each one merged cell spanning a Power+Hold pair (rows 29-30 and rows 31-32), printed once
  per pair. `NOT USED` in the `COIL PART NUMBER` column is likewise one cell spanning rows 33-34 and one spanning
  rows 35-36; the `COIL COLOR` cells of those pairs are blank.
- Power wires print left-aligned (`YEL-GRN`) on the Power line, Hold wires right-aligned (`ORG-GRN`) on the Hold line.
- The voltage connection cells print the connector pin followed by the wire colour in parentheses
  (`J907-1 (RED-GRN)`), repeated on both lines of the pair.
- Flipper connectors are `J907` (voltage, Fliptronic II board) and `J902` (drive); the legend names `J9XX-X` as the
  Fliptronic II board.
- The printed hold transistors are `Q11`, `Q9`, `Q7`, `Q5` and the power transistors `Q4`, `Q3`, `Q2`, `Q1`.
- Wire-colour/pin pairs as printed: lower right `J902-13` / `J902-11`, lower left `J902-9` / `J902-7`, upper right

## Comparison with the other two printings

The same table is printed three times in the manual: PDF page 116 (`2-38`, the reading above), PDF page 123 (`3-5`, the
Section 3 reprint) and PDF page 002 (the inside-cover sheet, headed `DIP SWITCH SETTINGS AND JUMPERS` and, below it,
`SOLENOID/FLASHER TABLE`). All three were read from rendered 1.6x-1.7x crops of every block, left half and right half,
and compared row by row and cell by cell.

### PDF page 123 (printed `3-5`)

Heading `SOLENOID/FLASHLAMP TABLE` in capitals (`2-38` prints `Solenoid/Flashlamp Table` in mixed case). Every cell of the
main table (01-44), the General Illumination block, the Flipper Circuits block, the headers and the legend line agrees
with `2-38`. The only differences:

- the page heading's capitalisation (above);
- the printed folio (`3-5` instead of `2-38`);
- the dashed line that crosses the right half of `2-38` between rows 23 and 24 is absent from the `3-5` reprint
  (artefact in `2-38` only).

### PDF page 002 (inside-cover sheet)

Heading `SOLENOID/FLASHER TABLE` (`FLASHER`, not `FLASHLAMP`; the column header still reads `SOLENOID PART NUMBER
FLASHLAMP TYPE`). The sheet is printed smaller than `2-38` but every cell was legible at 1.7x; no cell was illegible.
Every cell of the main table, the General Illumination block, the Flipper Circuits block, the headers and the legend
line agrees with `2-38`. The only differences are the page heading (`SOLENOID/FLASHER TABLE`) and the absence of a
folio (the sheet prints none under the table; the page also carries the DIP switch settings table above it). It
does not carry the dashed artefact line.

### Net result

No cell differs between the three printings. Differences are limited to the page heading text (`Solenoid/Flashlamp
Table` / `SOLENOID/FLASHLAMP TABLE` / `SOLENOID/FLASHER TABLE`), the folio (`2-38` / `3-5` / none), and the dashed
scan artefact on `2-38`.

## Uncertain readings

None in this table. The slashed `#` glyph is consistent across every row of every printing. The duplicated
transistor designators (G.I. block, flipper Hold column and unused solenoids 37-44, listed under the G.I. notes) are
printed on all three printings and are therefore not a scan defect; the interpretation of them (misprint or shared driver) is not settled by this page.
