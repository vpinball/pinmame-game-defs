## Notes on the lists

- `*Not Shown.` is printed once, directly under item 28, but no item in the main list carries an asterisk, so the page
  does not say which item(s) it refers to. Reading the drawing, the balloons present are 01, 03-06, 08-28 (26 balloons);
  items `02` (NOT USED) and `07` (Knocker) have no balloon, and the flippers `29-36` have none either. The likely
  referent (`07`, Knocker) is an inference only; it is not printed.
- Item 02 prints `NOT USED` in the Description column and nothing in the coil and assembly columns; flipper rows
  33-34 and 35-36 do the same.
- The `Coil/Flasher No.` column holds the flashlamp bulb part `24-8802` (`#906`, per the legend) for items 15-27, and
  `14-8023` for the visor motor (item 28). Item 14 prints `SM1-26-600`.
- Items 15-17 (the visor flashers) and 23-27 (the back-panel flashers) share assembly `A-20158`; items 18-19 share
  `A-17802`; items 20-22 share `04-10091.1`; items 03, 05 and 08 (the three eject holes) share `A-20453`; items 06, 09
  and 10 share `B-9362-L-2`; items 11-13 share `A-9415-2`.
- The table on `2-38` prints `AE-23-800` as a backbox part for item 07 (Knocker) and `14-8023` for item 28; both
  agree with this list. This list prints the knocker assembly as `B-10686-1`.
- Item 03 (`Far Left Eject`) and item 08 (`Left Eject Hole`) are separate entries; item 05 is `Right Eject Hole`.
- The column header `Coil/ Flasher No.` is printed over two lines (`Coil/`, `Flasher No.`).
