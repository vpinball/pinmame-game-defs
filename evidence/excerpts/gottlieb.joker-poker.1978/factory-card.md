The lower-left factory instruction card B-18450 describes the three-ball game.
The first ball spots the Ten bonus, the second the Queen bonus, and the third the
Ace bonus. Completing the spotted sequence lights 5X bonus. Completing a group
of drops lights its bonus; completing the Ten/J sequence or another group scores
5,000 and resets the targets. A target scores 500, increased to 5,000 when its
bonus is lit. Completing the A group or A-B-C rollovers lights Extra Ball and
Special. Pops score 1,000. Bonus is collected and features reset after each ball.
The card says tilt does not disqualify the player. These are the card's rules;
operator option descriptions require the missing factory manual.
