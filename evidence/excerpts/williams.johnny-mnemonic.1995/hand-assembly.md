The two 5647-12693-06 mini micro switches are the part the switch-locations list gives for switches
12 ("X" Hand Home) and 37 ("Y" Hand Home); the two A-20533.1 boards are the part it gives for 74-77.
The #906 bulb (item 33) in the red mini dome (item 12) and socket (item 35) sits on the assembly
that also carries the screened back panel (item 18).
