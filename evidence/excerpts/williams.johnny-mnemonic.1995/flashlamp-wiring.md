# Williams Johnny Mnemonic (1995) — Flashlamp Wiring

Source: Williams *Johnny Mnemonic* operations manual (September 1995), IPDB machine 3683 file
`Williams_1995_Johnny_Mnemonic_English_Manual.pdf`, PDF page 115, printed page 3-7. Read from the
native 300 dpi render. Two wiring drawings, each drawn from the power driver board to a row of
flashlamp symbols sharing one +20V feed.

`PLAYFIELD FLASHLAMPS`: `J107-6 RED-WHT, +20V` feeds every lamp.

| Connector | Wire | Solenoid | Printed name |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| J126-1 | BLK-BRN | SOLENOID 17 | JET FLASHER |
| J126-2 | BLK-RED | SOLENOID 18 | CRAZY BOB'S FLASHER |
| J126-3 | BLK-ORG | SOLENOID 19 | LEFT SLINGSHOT FLASHER |
| J126-4 | BLK-YEL | SOLENOID 20 | RIGHT SLINGSHOT FLASHER |
| J122-1 | BLU-BRN | SOLENOID 25 | LEFT RAMP FLASHER |
| J122-2 | BLU-RED | SOLENOID 26 | RIGHT RAMP FLASHER |
| J122-3 | BLU-ORG | SOLENOID 27 | HAND POPPER FLASHER |
| J122-4 | BLU-YEL | SOLENOID 28 | RIGHT BACKPANEL FLASHER |

`BACKBOX FLASHLAMPS`: `J106-5 RED-WHT, +20V` feeds every lamp.

| Connector | Wire | Solenoid | Printed name |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| J125-2 | BLK-RED | SOLENOID 18 | CRAZY BOB'S FLASHER |
| J125-5 | BLK-ORG | SOLENOID 20 | RIGHT SLINGSHOT FLASHER |
| J124-1 | BLU-BRN | SOLENOID 25 | LEFT RAMP FLASHER |
| J124-5 | BLU-YEL | SOLENOID 28 | RIGHT BACKPANEL FLASHER |

The backbox J125-5 wire is printed `BLK-ORG`; the solenoid table and the connector list (printed
3-30) print it Black-Yellow, solenoid 20's colour.
