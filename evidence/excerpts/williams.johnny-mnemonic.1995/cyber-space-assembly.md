The exploded drawing puts the cover (18) and its ball guards (21) at the top, the nine red rollover
buttons (10) and their springs (11) above the wood panel (6), the base (9) below them with a nylined
bearing (19) at its edge, and below the base the pivot arm (8), the link and roll pin (1a, 1b), the
plunger (1c) with its spring (3) and the coil (2e) in its bracket (2a-2d) with its cable (2f).

The coil AE-25-1000 and bracket assembly A-20446 are the part and assembly the solenoid table and
the solenoid-locations list give for solenoid 05 Clear Matrix. The PC board 04-10277 is the assembly
the lamp-locations list gives for the nine cyber-matrix lamps.
