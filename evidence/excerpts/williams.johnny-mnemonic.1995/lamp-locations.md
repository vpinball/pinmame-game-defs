Item 87's assembly is printed `20-663-21`; the Switch Locations list (printed 2-35) prints the Buy-in
Button as `20-9663-21`. The 04-10277 part numbered for the nine cyber-matrix lamps is the PC board
of the A-20447 Cyber Space Assembly (printed 2-25).

The drawing puts callouts 86 at the front right of the cabinet and 88 and 87 at the front left, below
the playfield outline; every other callout reaches a playfield insert or the matrix.
