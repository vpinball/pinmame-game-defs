the right; this transcription covers both and is cited by two crops, `switch-locations` (the list)

## Drawing

The drawing is the playfield outline with circled callouts and leader lines. It is used here only to
check which physical feature each switch number belongs to:

- An inset labelled `Cyber Space Assy.` draws the nine matrix holes as three rows: `51 52 53` at the
  rear, `61 62 63` in the middle and `71 72 73` at the front, left to right.
- Callouts 44, 46 and 45 point at the upper-left jet bumper, the right jet bumper and the lower jet
  bumper of the three-bumper group at the upper left.
- Callouts 64, 65 and 66 point at the three jet lanes above the bumpers; 12, 37 and 74-77 point into
  the hand drive assembly at the rear left; 36 points at the popper and 43 at the drop target beside
  it at the rear centre; 58 points at the inner loop entry at the left edge of the matrix.
- 27 and 28 point at the left and right standup targets on the side rails; 38 at the right rubber;
  56 at the left loop and 57 at the upper right loop; 41 and 42 along the left ramp, 54 and 55 along
  the right ramp.
- 15/16 and 17/18 point at the left and right outlane/return-lane pairs, 25 and 26 at the slingshots,
  F1 and F3 at the flipper assemblies, and 31 to 35 along the trough under the apron, with 31 at the
  right-hand (shooter) end and 35 at the left.
- Cabinet callouts: 11 at the front right, 13 and 23 at the front left, 67 and F2 on the right cabinet
  side, 68 and F4 on the left side, 78 at the shooter lane.
