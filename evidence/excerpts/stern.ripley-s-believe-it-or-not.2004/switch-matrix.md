# Ripley's Believe It or Not! switch matrix

Source: `Ripleys_Manual.pdf`, PDF page 190 (printed drawing `DR. 4`), upper half of the page.

The complete `SWITCH MATRIX GRID & DEDICATED SWITCHES` visibly enumerates matrix switches 1-64 across eight driven columns and eight returns. It identifies the UK-only cabinet buttons, coin slots, Head, tombstones, four-ball trough and stacking optos, shooter lane, spinners, scoop/VUK, ramps, orbit, mini-playfield, vari-target optos 41-43, three lock seats 44-46, jackpot loop, pops, start/tournament controls, plumb bob, lanes, and slingshots. The grid marks 3 and 7 as future-use coin positions and 10, 37, 63, and 64 as not used; the transcription in the owner script preserves those machine-specific marks rather than inferring activity from runtime.

The same page prints the dedicated input block: DS-1/2 are the left flipper button/EOS, DS-3/4 the right flipper button/EOS, DS-5 the upper-right flipper button, and DS-6/7/8 the volume, service-credit, and begin-test cabinet buttons. The memory-protect input is the separate CN6-P12 signal documented elsewhere in the manual.
