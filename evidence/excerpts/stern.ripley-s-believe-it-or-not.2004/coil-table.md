# Ripley's Believe It or Not! coil and flasher chart

Source: `Ripleys_Manual.pdf`, PDF page 8 (printed drawing `DR. 6`).

The complete `COILS DETAILED CHART TABLE` visibly lists Q1-Q32 and the optional UK-only AUX1-AUX3 outputs. Q1-Q16 are the high-current group: trough up-kicker, autolaunch, vari-VUK, left/right ramp diverters, six pops, scoop VUK, lock kicker, upper-right flipper, and the two lower flippers. Q17-Q24 are the low-current group containing both slingshots, Idol and Head magnets, vari reset, Idol opto LED, top post, and an optional 5 V output. Q25-Q32 are the flash channels for the lower pops/left lower playfield, left spinner, upper left, Head, vari-target, upper pops/right ramp, right spinner, and right-ramp lower. The chart also prints each Q number, drive-transistor/control connector, power feed, voltage, and coil or bulb type; AUX1-AUX3 are the left, center, and right UK up/down posts on the separate 3X transistor board.
