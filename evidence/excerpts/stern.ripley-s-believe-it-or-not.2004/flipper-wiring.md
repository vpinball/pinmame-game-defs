# Ripley's Believe It or Not! three-flipper wiring

Source: `Ripleys_Manual.pdf`, PDF page 123 (printed page 106), `3-Flipper Circuit Wiring Diagram`.

The manual explicitly identifies the three-flipper game: lower left, lower right, and upper right. DS-1 and DS-3 are the lower-left and lower-right cabinet flipper buttons; DS-5 is the upper-right button. DS-2 and DS-4 are the normally closed lower-left and lower-right EOS contacts. The drawing connects the physical coils to Q15 (lower left), Q16 (lower right), and Q14 (upper right), all on the 50 V driver system, and describes the CPU's hold/pulse behavior for the flipper switches and EOS contacts.
