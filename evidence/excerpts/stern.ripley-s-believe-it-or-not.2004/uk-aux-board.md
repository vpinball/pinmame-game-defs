# Ripley's Believe It or Not! optional UK auxiliary board

Source: `Ripleys_Manual.pdf`, PDF page 166 (printed page 149), `UK & Special App. 3X Transistor Driver Board`.

The 520-5068-01 auxiliary driver board schematic visibly provides three transistor channels on the J2 connector. Together with the coil chart, the channels are AUX1 left up/down post, AUX2 center up/down post, and AUX3 right up/down post. This is an optional UK/special-application assembly; the standard physical machine may omit it, so the owner definition keeps these outputs optional instead of treating them as universal stock hardware.
