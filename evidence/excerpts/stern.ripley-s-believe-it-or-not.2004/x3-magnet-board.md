# Ripley's Believe It or Not! X3 magnet driver board

Source: `Ripleys_Manual.pdf`, PDF page 162 (printed page 145), `Playfield X3 (Triple) Driver Board Component Layout, Parts & Wiring`.

The 520-5235-03 X3 driver board drawing visibly maps Q19 to the lower Idol magnet, Q20 to the upper Shrunken Head magnet, and Q21 to the vari-target trip release. The board receives the +50 V feed and the three PinMAME-facing control lines, and its harness labels identify the three physical loads. This is the manual proof for treating the two magnets and vari-target reset as distinct physical actuators rather than decorative or software-only objects.
