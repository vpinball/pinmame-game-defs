# Ripley's Believe It or Not! lamp matrix

Source: `Ripleys_Manual.pdf`, PDF page 190 (printed drawing `DR. 4`), lower half of the page.

The complete `LAMP MATRIX GRID` visibly enumerates lamps 1-80 as eight column drives by ten row returns. The chart names the six Super Jackpot lamps, Shoot Again, specials, Idol counters, Tombstone 1, scoop arrow, the seven RIPLEY'S letter lamps, the three-by-three grid, pop and skill-shot lamps, tombstones, jackpot loops, all seven vari-target regions, Penguin, Head, locks, spinners, Multiball, Bigfoot, Odditorium, Bozo, inlanes, back-panel lamps, tournament, and Start. The lamp type is printed at each cell (`#555` or `#44`); the owner script preserves the machine-specific clear-bulb assignments and exact matrix wiring.
