# Ripley's Believe It or Not! three mini-DMD board

Source: `Ripleys_Manual.pdf`, PDF page 163 (printed page 146), `Playfield Dot Display (5x7) x3 LED Board (ABC Left Ramp/Upr. P/F Side) Schematic`.

The schematic visibly contains three independent 5x7 dot-display blocks, with separate DOTBLOCK1/2/3 signal groups and the shared board connector. It is the physical-manual evidence for three small display surfaces in addition to the main DMD; it does not support collapsing them into one virtual 15x7 display.
