# Ripley's Believe It or Not! vari-target opto board

Source: `Ripleys_Manual.pdf`, PDF page 165 (printed page 148), `3-Position OPTO PC Board (Vari-Target) Theory of Operation & Schematic`.

The 520-5234-00 board is a three-position photo-interrupter assembly. The manual names the three optos as switch 41, switch 42, and switch 43 and shows their switch-drive and return wiring to the CPU/sound board. The board note identifies it as the playfield detection switch for the `Vari-Target` assembly, so these three inputs are sensors for one moving mechanism, not three unrelated playfield switches.
