Printing notes, preserved literally:

- The table jumps from `28` to `35`; there is no row 29-34 in this block. Printed circuits 29-36
  also appear in the Flipper Circuits block, where 33/34 are `Upper Right Flipper` and 35/36 are
  `Upper Left Flipper` with coil `SEE ABOVE`.
- Row 17 prints `14-8021.1`; the Solenoid/Flasher Locations list prints `14-8021` for the same item.
- Row 04 prints `AE-28-1500` (both here and in the locations list).
- Footnote line below the Flipper Circuits block:
  `J1xx=Power Driver Board; J9xx=Fliptronic II Board; 24-6549=#44 bulb; 24-8704=#89 bulb; 24-8768=#555 bulb; 24-8802=#906 bulb`.
