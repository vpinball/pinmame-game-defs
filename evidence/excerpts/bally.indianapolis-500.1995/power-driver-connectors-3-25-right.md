Printing notes: this list labels J121 "to insert". The handbook's G.I. table prints J121-1/-2/-3 and
J121-7/-8/-9 in its Playfield columns for strings 01 Upper Left Playfield, 02 Upper Right Playfield
and 03 Lower Playfield, and the race-track reflector sockets on the playfield are wired `ORG` and
`ORG/WHT`, the J121-2 (Orange return) and J121-8 (White-Orange) colours.
