# Bally Indianapolis 500 (1995) — A-20169 Race Track Assembly, front view with reflector wiring

Source: Midway Manufacturing Company *Indianapolis 500* operations manual (152-page image-only scan,
IPDB machine 2853 file `indy500manualfull.pdf`; the untouched download is retained as
`indy500manualfull.original-scan.pdf`), PDF page 103, printed page 2-29, the right-hand (front) view
of the race-track drawing. Read from the native 300 dpi render. This is a drawing, so the crop is the
evidence; the transcription lists what it labels.

Labels visible in the crop:

- The arched race track weldment, with callout `18` (Decal - Racetrack) on its face and the small car
  on its hub near the top of the arch.
- Two reflector fixtures at the top corners of the arch. Callout `3` (04-10094 Reflector Socket
  Assembly) points at the left fixture; callout `8` (8-32 x 1/2" machine screw) points at the right
  fixture's mounting screw. Each fixture is drawn with two reflector cones on one socket bracket.
- At **each** fixture, two leads are labelled `ORG` and `ORG/WHT`, and callout `16`
  (17-1116-2 Wire, Yellow Jumper Cut 2") marks the jumper at each fixture.
- The drawing and its parts table print no quantity for item 3 and no bulb count.
- Callout `7` (set screw) on the side view at the left edge of the crop.

No lead on this view carries a solenoid or lamp-matrix wire colour (the flasher drive wires are
Black-/Blue-/Brown- colours and the lamp matrix uses Yellow-/Red- colours; see
`solenoid-flasher-table.md` and `lamp-matrix.md`). The G.I. strings use White-<colour> 6.8VAC feeds
and <colour> returns (`power-driver-connectors-3-25-left.md`, `power-driver-connectors-3-25-right.md`).
