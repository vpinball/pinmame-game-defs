# Bally Indianapolis 500 (1995) — Turbo Multi-Ball rules paragraph

Source: Midway Manufacturing Company *Indianapolis 500* operations manual (152-page image-only scan,
IPDB machine 2853 file `indy500manualfull.pdf`; the untouched download is retained as
`indy500manualfull.original-scan.pdf`), PDF page 17, the paragraph under **TURBO MULTI-BALL™** in the
game rules. Read from the native 300 dpi render; the crop carries the full printed paragraph.

What the paragraph states, in order:

- The left loop lights the lock when "Light Lock" is lit; balls shot into the Turbo while "Lock" is
  lit are locked.
- Locking two balls in the Turbo starts a 3-ball multiball; jackpots are on the two ramps.
- During the multiball, re-locking one ball and then a second in the Turbo boosts jackpot values,
  and re-locking the third ball awards a Super Jackpot.
- Locking all balls on the playfield in the Turbo also serves an additional ball onto the
  playfield, "for a maximum of four balls".

The paragraph gives no physical capacity for the turbo.
