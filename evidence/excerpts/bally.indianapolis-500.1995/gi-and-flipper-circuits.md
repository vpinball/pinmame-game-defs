Printing notes, preserved literally:

- The coil part/colour cells for the `Upper Left Flipper` pair read `SEE` and `ABOVE`: circuits
  35/36 are the `Diverter Power` / `Diverter Hold` rows of the solenoid block above (same Q1/Q5,

- The GI block's `Backbox-Coindoor` row prints only backbox connections.
