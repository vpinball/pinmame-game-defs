# Avatar Pro/LE flipper wiring

Source: Avatar-Manual.pdf, PDF page 43, Flipper Circuit Wiring Diagram.

The diagram identifies the left, right, upper-left and upper-right flipper buttons as normally open dedicated switches D-9, D-11, D-13 and D-15, with the matching normally closed end-of-stroke switches D-10, D-12, D-14 and D-16. It explains the double-stacked cabinet buttons and shows Q13-Q16 as the four flipper coil outputs.
