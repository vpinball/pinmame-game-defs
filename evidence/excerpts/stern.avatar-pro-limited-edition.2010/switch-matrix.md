# Avatar Pro/LE switch matrix

Source: Avatar-Manual.pdf, PDF page 40, Playfield Switch Wiring Diagram.

The matrix drawing labels switch drives 1-4 and returns 1-16, covering switch numbers 1-64. It explicitly labels the tournament/start dedicated switches as switches 15 and 16 and marks optos in the matrix. The page is the electrical address cross-check for the switch inventory.
