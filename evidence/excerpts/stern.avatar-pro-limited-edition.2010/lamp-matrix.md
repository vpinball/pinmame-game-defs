# Avatar Pro/LE lamp matrix

Source: Avatar-Manual.pdf, PDF page 41, Playfield Lamp Wiring Diagram.

The drawing labels eight lamp columns at J13 and ten lamp returns at J12, forming the 80-lamp matrix. Lamps 1-80 are laid out in eight columns by ten returns, with opto positions marked in the later rows and the manual note that switch, lamp and coil assemblies may use diodes on the playfield or terminal strips.
