# Avatar Pro/LE GI wiring

Source: Avatar-Manual.pdf, PDF page 39, General Illumination Circuit Detailed Wiring Diagram.

The playfield wiring drawing shows the underside playfield and the four fused GI circuits. Circuit 1 is the brown/white group at the upper-left/middle playfield, circuit 2 is the yellow/white group at the lower-left/middle, circuit 3 is the green/white back-panel and coin-door group, and circuit 4 is the violet/white right-playfield group. The manual labels the rear-panel and coin-door quantities separately from the playfield groups.
