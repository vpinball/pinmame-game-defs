# Avatar Pro/LE cabinet wiring

Source: Avatar-Manual.pdf, PDF page 42, Cabinet and Coin Door Wiring.

The cabinet diagram identifies the flipper buttons and end-of-stroke switches, tournament/start buttons, tilt pendulum, ticket notch, optional shaker motor Q8 and optional coin/ticket-meter coil Q24. It also shows the cabinet speaker wiring and the dedicated switch and lamp connectors. These controls are cabinet hardware, not playfield coordinates.
