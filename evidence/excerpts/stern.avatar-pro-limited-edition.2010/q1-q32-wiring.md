# Avatar Pro/LE Q1-Q32 board wiring

Source: Avatar-Manual.pdf, PDF page 37, Backbox I/O Power Driver Board Detailed Wiring Diagram.

The diagram groups the outputs by board connector: J8 carries Q1-Q8 high-current solenoids, J9 carries Q9-Q16 high-current solenoids, J7 carries Q17-Q24 low-current solenoids/flash lamps, and J6 carries Q25-Q32 flash lamps or low-current solenoids. It also shows the connector pin numbering and the optional Q33-Q35 ticket-dispenser note.
