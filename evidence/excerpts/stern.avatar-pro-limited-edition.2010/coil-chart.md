# Avatar Pro/LE coil chart

Source: Avatar-Manual.pdf, PDF page 36, COILS DETAILED CHART TABLE.

The chart enumerates Q1-Q32. High-current group 1 identifies the trough up-kicker, auto launch, AMP suit magnet, 3-bank relay, Link lockup and latch, and optional shaker; high-current group 2 identifies the pop bumpers, AMP suit relay and flippers. Low-current groups identify slingshots, AMP suit motor, Link flashes, back-panel flash, and the remaining flashes. The chart also records driver-board connectors, voltages and coil/bulb types.
