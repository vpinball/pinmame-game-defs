# Mustang Pro — switch matrix excerpt

Source: `Mustang-Manual.pdf`, PDF page 12, crop box 0.05,0.08,0.96,0.88, rotated 90 degrees counter-clockwise after rendering. The scanned crop transcribes the complete visible switch matrix, dedicated switches D-1–D-32, and the CPU/sound-board DIP-switch row.

The four drive rows are GRN-BRN J1-P1, GRN-RED J1-P3, GRN-ORG J1-P4, and GRN-YEL J1-P5; the matrix labels the Mustang Pro switch numbers and locations for targets, troughs, ramps, orbit switches, bowl, and cabinet controls. Dedicated cells include left/center/right/fourth/fifth coin slots, home/away, flipper buttons and EOS, tilt/slam/ticket, back-green, minus/red, plus/red, and select/black service buttons; D-25 onward are DIP switch positions. The printed table distinguishes above-playfield, below-playfield, and unused cells.
