# Theatre of Magic — Solenoid/Flasher Table

Transcribed from `Theatre_of_Magic_OPS.pdf`, PDF page 2, a front-matter duplicate of the printed
2-46/2-47 Solenoid/Flasher Table (the same table also appears at PDF pages 120 and 127, inside
Section 2's Locations chapter and Section 3's wiring chapter respectively). Page 2's scan is sharper
than 120/127, so it is the primary transcription source. The retained PDF carries a Paper Capture OCR
text layer, but per project policy every table was verified against a 300 dpi render of the page
regardless of the text layer's presence. The General Illumination table printed as a continuation of
the same page is transcribed separately as `general-illumination.md`.

**Repurposing finding.** Printed circuits 33-36 ("Upr. Rt./Lt. Power/Hold") are the same physical
Fliptronic-II-board driver transistors (Q2, Q7, Q1, Q5) as main-table rows 33 (Cube Magnet, Q2), 34
(Sub Ball Release, Q7), and 35 (Left Drain Magnet, Q1) — the transistor numbers match exactly across
the two tables. Row 36 (Upper Left Flipper Hold, Q5) has no corresponding main-table entry and prints
`NOT USED` for its coil part and color. This is the "printed circuits reserved for upper flippers
repurposed with no address translation" pattern (`tomGameData.hw.flippers` declares `FLIP_SOL(FLIP_L)`
only, no `FLIP_UR`/`FLIP_UL` bit).
