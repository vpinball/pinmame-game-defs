# Theatre of Magic — Lamp Locations (parts list)

Transcribed from `Theatre_of_Magic_OPS.pdf`, PDF page 117, printed page 2-41, the Lamp Locations
parts list. The retained PDF carries a Paper Capture OCR text layer, but per project policy every
table was verified against a 300 dpi render of the page regardless of the text layer's presence.

Bulb legend: `24-8768 = #555`, `24-6549 = #44`. Quantity `(2)` items: 26 Jacket Award (assemblies
A-19983 and A-19982), 45 Safe Award (A-19983 and A-19984), 54 Lock Ball (A-19983 and A-19984), 63
Grand Finale (A-19981, single assembly, two bulbs), 81 Special (A-17835, single assembly, two
bulbs). Unused: 82, 83, 84 (blank bulb and assembly). Bulb 85 "Lamp In Cube" (#555, A-17826) is the
light that rides inside the rotating trunk. 86 "Shoot Again" (#44, A-17807). 87/88 are the
illuminated Buy-In/Start pushbutton assemblies (`20-9663-18` / `20-9663-1`), cabinet hardware with no
separate lamp assembly number.
