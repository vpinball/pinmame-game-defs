# Mustang Premium / LE / Boss — coil wiring excerpt

Source: `Mustang_LE_web.pdf`, PDF page 16, crop box 0.61,0.45,0.98,0.92. The crop transcribes the typical single-coil diagram and the beginning of the Mustang dual-winding coil wiring panel.

The typical Q1 trough up-kicker diagram shows the I/O PCB drive transistor, a 1N4004 diode located on the I/O power-driver PCB, J8 control connection BRN-BLK, coil connection at J10-P9/10, and +50VDC high power on YEL-VIO; the coil is identified as #1, 26-1200 gauge-turn. The dual-winding panel identifies replacement coil 090-5083-00-ND and labels the upper-ramp and mid-ramp assemblies with BRN-BLU, BRN-GRN, and YEL-VIO leads.
