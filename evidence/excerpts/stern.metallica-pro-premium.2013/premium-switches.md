# Metallica Premium / LE — switch wiring and location excerpt

Source: `MTLAB1-compressed.pdf`, PDF page 104, crop box 0.05,0.06,0.95,0.70. The crop transcribes the Premium switch-location drawing and the adjacent switch, dedicated-switch, and terminal-strip schematics.

The drawing labels the Premium playfield switch groups for the electric chair, grave lanes, pops, captive-ball region, loops, ramps, eject, and drop-target area. The typical switch schematic shows the switch circuit on GRN-XXX, a blocking diode, and switch return WHT-XXX; the dedicated schematic shows PNK-XXX or GRY-XXX to the CPU/sound-board grounded input, black ground at J2-P11/J3-P10, and normally-open/common terminals. The terminal-strip variant shows a diode on the strip and the continuing row/column wire color convention.
