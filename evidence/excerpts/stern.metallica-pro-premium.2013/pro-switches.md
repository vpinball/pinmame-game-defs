# Metallica Pro — switch-menu and wiring excerpt

Source: `MTLAB1-compressed.pdf`, PDF page 147, crop box 0.05,0.06,0.95,0.70. The crop transcribes the Pro manual's switch-menu description and the adjacent standard and dedicated-switch schematics.

The service text states that playfield switches use a 4×16 drive/return matrix with up to 64 addresses, while dedicated inputs use a 2×16 arrangement with up to 32 addresses. It also documents active-switch testing and the normally closed trough and flipper-EOS examples. The standard schematic identifies the driven column, blocking diode, and switch-return row; the dedicated schematic uses PNK-XXX or GRY-XXX to the grounded input and BLACK to J3-P10.
