# Star Trek Premium / LE — switch matrix excerpt

Source: `Star-Trek-LE-Manual.pdf`, PDF page 68, crop box 0.05,0.06,0.95,0.92. The crop transcribes the complete visible four-row switch matrix, dedicated switches D-1–D-24, and CPU/sound-board DIP positions.

The drive rows are GRN-BRN J1-P1, GRN-RED J1-P3, GRN-ORG J1-P4, and GRN-YEL J1-P5. The matrix identifies the four-ball trough 18–21, jam opto 22, shooter lane 23, center target and ramp/warp switches, orbit/loop switches, slings, pops, banks, and cabinet/service switches; the dedicated row identifies coin slots, fire, flipper buttons/EOS, and cabinet switches. The crop retains the printed return-wire colors, SPI part numbers, above/below-playfield labels, and DIP-switch row.
