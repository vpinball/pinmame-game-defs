# Star Trek Premium / LE — opto amplifier board excerpt

Source: `Star-Trek-LE-Manual.pdf`, PDF page 153, crop box 0.05,0.06,0.95,0.92. The crop transcribes the Star Trek Pro/LE Opto Amplifier Board 1 and 2 wiring configuration.

Board 1 carries switch 33 and board 2 carries switch 13; each has transceiver boards 500-6775-01 connected by two-conductor silver-anode/copper-cathode cables to transmitter and receiver inputs J1–J4 on amplifier boards 520-5239-01. Board 1 returns WHT-BRN, WHT-RED, GRN-ORG, BLK ground, and RED +5VDC to the CPU/I/O boards; board 2 returns TAN-GRN, TAN-BLU, GRN-RED, BLK ground, and RED +5VDC. The drawing is physical wiring evidence, not a claim that the Pro machine has every LE opto channel.
