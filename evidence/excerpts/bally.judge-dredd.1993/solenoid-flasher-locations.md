# Judge Dredd — Solenoid/Flasher Locations

Transcribed from `Bally_1993_Judge_Dredd_Manual.pdf`, PDF page 113, printed page 2-45, the
SOLENOID/FLASHER LOCATIONS parts list, its General Illumination Circuits and Flipper Coils
sub-tables, and its playfield location diagram. Produced by rendering the retained PDF at 300 dpi and
600 dpi with `pdftoppm` and reading the page directly.

Footnotes printed on the page: `*Not Shown`, `†Located Under Playfield`. Diagram legend: a filled
**square** indicates a coil, P.C.B. or magnet; a filled **circle** indicates a flasher.
