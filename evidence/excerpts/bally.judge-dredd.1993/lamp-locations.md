# Judge Dredd — Lamp Locations

Transcribed from `Bally_1993_Judge_Dredd_Manual.pdf`, PDF page 109, printed page 2-41, the LAMP
LOCATIONS parts list and its playfield location diagram. Produced by rendering the retained PDF at 300
dpi and 600 dpi with `pdftoppm` and reading the pages directly.

Bulb legend printed on the page: `24-8768 = #555 Bulb`, `24-6549 = #44 Bulb`. Light bulb covers:
`03-8063-2 green`, `03-8063-4 Red`, `03-8063-5 White`, `03-8063-6 Yellow`. Footnotes: `* Not Shown`,
`† Located Under Playfield` (neither footnote is actually used by any row of this list).

This list is the authoritative label source for the lamps; where it differs from the Lamp Matrix page
on 2-40 the difference is noted at the end.

## Quantities

The list was swept row by row for a printed quantity annotation. Exactly two rows carry one, and they
put it in different columns:

- **61 `Extra Ball (2)`** — the `(2)` is in the Description column. The location diagram confirms it by
  drawing the 61 callout twice, once at the far left of the playfield below the crane assembly and once
  at the upper right beside 65 Air Raid.
- **83 `A-16929 (2)`** — the `(2)` is in the Lamp Assy. No. column. The location diagram likewise draws
  83 twice, as a symmetric pair flanking the drain at the bottom of the playfield.

Every other row is a single bulb. Three rows are cabinet button lamps rather than playfield inserts,
identified by a `---` bulb number and a `20-9663-*` button assembly: 38 Buy-In, 87 Super Game, 88
Start Button. The diagram draws all three outside the playfield outline at the bottom of the page.

## Label difference against the Lamp Matrix page (2-40)

Lamp 61 is printed `Extra Ball (2)` here and `Right Extra Ball` on the matrix page. This list is
preferred as the label source, and the `(2)` explains the matrix page's qualifier: there are two
Extra Ball inserts and the matrix page named only the right-hand one.
