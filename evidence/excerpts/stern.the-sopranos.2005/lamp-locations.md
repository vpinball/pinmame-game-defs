# The Sopranos — lamp location drawing and back-panel inset

Read from `Stern_2005_The_Sopranos_Service_Manual.pdf`, PDF page 7 (printed `DR. 5`), lower half: the playfield lamp
location drawing under the lamp matrix grid. The drawing is an embedded 1-bit scan (2698 x 1212 px at 380 ppi); the
crop `lamp-locations.webp` renders it at that native resolution. As on the switch page, the playfield's rear is at the
left of the image and the flippers at the right.

Each lamp is marked by its number in a black box (lamps below the playfield, i.e. inserts) drawn on the insert itself.
Numbers on the playfield: 1-61 once each and 73-78 once each. 1-6 sit in one black strip over six round inserts on the
centre line, 7 inside the round centre insert with 8-16 around it. 73, 74, 75, 76 and 77 are white boxes whose leaders
run into the black character art left of the bumpers; 24 is a white box with a short black stub pointing at the boat
(The Stugots) insert. 79 is a grey box outside the outline labelled `Optional Tournament Kit Required`; 80 (the start
button) is a grey box at the lower right corner of the page. No 62, 63 or 64 is drawn.

Below the drawing, a separate inset labelled `Lamps 65 thru 72 are located on the rear of the Back Panel.` shows the
back panel with the `BADA BING!` artwork and eight portrait windows in two rows: 65, 66, 67, 68 left to right in the
top row and 69, 70, 71, 72 left to right in the bottom row. The legend reads `Playfield ▲` / `◀ Backpanel`.

The coordinates of every callout, as read independently on this crop, and the curator's corrections and drawing
measurements are in `tools/seeds/stern/the-sopranos-2005-callouts.json` (page `pdf-7`).
