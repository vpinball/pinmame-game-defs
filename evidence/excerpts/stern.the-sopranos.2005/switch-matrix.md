# The Sopranos — Switch Matrix Grid and Dedicated Switches

Transcribed from `Stern_2005_The_Sopranos_Service_Manual.pdf`, PDF page 6, the front-matter drawing printed `DR. 4`
("SWITCH MATRIX GRID, DEDICATED SWITCHES & LOCATIONS"), upper half: the 8 x 8 switch matrix grid and the
dedicated-switch column to its right. The page is born-digital: the grid's cell text was taken from the PDF text
layer and every cell was checked against a 220 dpi render. The location drawing in the lower half of the page is
transcribed separately in `switch-locations.md`.

Each matrix cell prints, on its top line, the switch number in a small box, then the mounting class (`Above P/F`,
`Below P/F`, `Cabinet Side`, `Coin Door` or `In Cabinet`); then the switch name over two or three lines; and on its
bottom line, after the row's `Sw. Part Number:` header, the part number. Names are written here on one line,
joined by single spaces. Where a printed cell is blank or reads `NOT USED` this is stated. The legend below the
drawing reads: white box `= Switches above Playfield.`, black box `= Switches below Playfield.`, grey box
`= Switches not on Playfield.` The `NOT USED` cells are printed as black blocks with a grey number box.

## Notes printed below the drawing

- `Switch Part Notes: ¥ Yen Coin Switch is 180-5091-00. Part Numbers which start with 515- or 500- include the bracket, target, and/or housing.`
- `Switches 14 & 15 Part Note: Transmitter & Receiver OPTO PC Boards are used as Switches: Transmitter: 515-0173-00 (Sw. 14 & 15); Receiver: 515-0174-00 (Sw. 14 & 15);`
- `Sw. 56 Part Note: The Switch is comprised of a Hanger Bracket (535-5319-00) and Contact Wire (535-7563-01) located in the Cabinet.`
- `Some Switch Diodes may be located under the playfield, in the Cabinet or Backbox on Terminal Strips or Diode Boards and not on the assemblies. DOTS: Diode On Terminal Strip, see Sec. 5, Chp.2, Playfield Wiring.`
- Beside the drawing: `55 Optional Tournament Kit Required` and `53 Optional Slam Tilt Kit Required`.
