# The Sopranos — Coils Detailed Chart Table

Transcribed from `Stern_2005_The_Sopranos_Service_Manual.pdf`, PDF page 8, the front-matter drawing printed `DR. 6`
("COILS DETAILED CHART TABLE"). The page is born-digital but its text layer scrambles this table's columns, so every
cell was read from a 220 dpi render. The last column prints the coil gauge-turns (with a winding-direction arrow,
omitted here) over the coil part number, or the bulb type over its part number. The `Driver Ouput Board` column is a
merged cell reading `I/O Power Driver` for each group of eight rows (`UK 3X Trans. Driver Board` for the auxiliary
rows); it is repeated on each row below.

The groups are headed `High Current Coils Group 1` (#1-#8), `High Current Coils Group 2` (#9-#16), `Low Current Coils
Group 1` (#17-#24), `Low Current Coils Group 2` (#25-#32) and `Auxiliary (UK ONLY)`. Row #24 and the bulb cells of
#25-#32 and the AUX rows are shaded grey. Under row #24: `Diode On Terminal Strip (if noted)`.

Notes printed on the page:

- Below #32: `Note: In Test Flash Lamps Menu ("Flash" Icon), Flashers tested are all Flash Lamps located between Q1-Q32 (This Game: Q19-Q20, Q25-Q29, Q31-Q32)`
- At the foot: `Coil Note: Either -00B or -00T can be used for coil replacements. B/T listed is preferable for easier diode access & may differ on game.`
