# The Sopranos — playfield switch parts and major assemblies

Transcribed from `Stern_2005_The_Sopranos_Service_Manual.pdf`. Section 4, Chapter 1 ("The Pink Pages") PDF page 83
(printed 66, "Playfield Top & Bottom - Switches") and Chapter 2 ("The Blue Pages") PDF pages 99-121 (printed 82-104).
These pages are born-digital; lines were taken from the PDF text layer and checked against the rendered pages. Quoted
lines keep the manual's spelling; only the items a definition cites are listed.
