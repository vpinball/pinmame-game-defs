# Monster Bash — Boards and Assemblies

Transcribed from `Williams_1998_Monster_Bash_English_Manual.pdf`, PDF pages 78-84, printed pages
2-10 through 2-16, the board and assembly parts pages that fix device construction (which switches
and outputs are opto interrupters, proximity sensors, or motor-driven rather than plain leaf
switches/coils). Read from the rendered pages; the retained scan is image-only. The "Bears on" column
is this curator's cross-reference to the affected addresses, added while transcribing; the part
number, name, and printed component descriptions are transcribed verbatim.
