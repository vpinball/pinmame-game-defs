## Wiring (printed 2-53)

Footnote under the general-illumination block: "*These general illumination strings do not brighten
and dim, they are always on." The asterisk is printed on the description of GI 04 (`*TOP INSERT
PANEL`) and GI 05 (`*BOTTOM INSERT PANEL`) only, so the claim covers the two backbox insert-panel
strings and not the three playfield strings 01-03, which do dim.

String 05 is the only entry on the page with a cabinet connection: besides the bottom insert panel it
feeds cabinet bulbs through J104-3 / J104-1. So the five printed GI strings split three ways — 01-03
playfield, 04 backbox insert panel, 05 backbox insert panel plus cabinet — and a definition must not
treat all five as playfield strings.
