# Monster Bash — Switch Locations

Transcribed from `Williams_1998_Monster_Bash_English_Manual.pdf`, PDF pages 116-117, printed pages
2-48/2-49, the switch-locations parts list. Read from the rendered pages; the retained scan is
image-only. `*` marks "NOT SHOWN" on the printed playfield map. `**` marks "SCORE SWITCHES HAVE
DIODES ATTACHED".
