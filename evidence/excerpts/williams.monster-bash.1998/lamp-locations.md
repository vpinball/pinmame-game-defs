Only lamp 78 is unused. Lamps 87 and 88 are the cabinet launch-button and start-button lamps and
share their assembly part numbers with switches 11 and 13 (the illuminated button assemblies), so
they are cabinet devices with no playfield position. Every other address drives a playfield or
insert-panel bulb. `(2)` marks the four two-bulb moon inserts: 12, 24, 31, 43.
