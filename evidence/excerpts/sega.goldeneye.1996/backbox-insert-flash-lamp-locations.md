# Sega GoldenEye (1996) — Backbox Insert Flash Lamp Locations

Source: Sega Pinball *GoldenEye* operations manual (`Sega_1996_Goldeneye_Manual.pdf`), PDF page 34,
printed page 22 (Section 3, Chapter 2), drawing and tables **Backbox Insert Flash Lamp Locations**.
Read from the native 300 dpi render. This is a drawing, so the crop is the evidence; the transcription
lists what it labels.

Printed introduction: the drawing shows the flash lamp locations in the backbox insert, with the
general illumination (GI) lamps also shown for reference, and the flash lamp numbers correspond to the
coil numbers seen in the Coil Test (FLAMP 1-8, the Coil Test's #25-#32).

Labels visible in the drawing (wiring side of the insert):

- Flash lamp sockets drawn as numbered black circles, each labelled with its power and drive wire
  colours: 1 `ORG BLK/BRN`; 2 `ORG BLK/RED` (two sockets); 3 `ORG BLK/ORG`; 4 `ORG BLK/YEL` (two
  sockets); 5 `ORG BLK/GRN` (two sockets); 6 `ORG BLK/BLU`; 7 `ORG BLK/VIO`; 8 `ORG BLK/GRY`.
- `GI` sockets joined by dashed wiring runs; one run is labelled `WHT/BRN BRN` at the left, another
  `GRN WHT/GRN` at the right.
- Legend: `GI = General Illumination`, `#44 Bulb (Bayonet) 165-5000-44`, `#44 Bulb uses 2-Lug Socket
  (077-5000-00)`; `# = Flash Lamp (FLAMP)`, `#89 Bulb (Bayonet) 165-5000-89`, `#89 Bulb uses a Stand-Up
  Short Socket (077-5101-00)`.
