# Sega GoldenEye (1996) — Playfield Bulbs and Sockets

Source: Sega Pinball *GoldenEye* operations manual (`Sega_1996_Goldeneye_Manual.pdf`), PDF pages 59, 60
and 61, printed pages 65, 66 and 67 (Section 4, Chapter 1, Parts Identification & Location), the parts
tables of **Playfield - Wedge Base Type Bulbs and Sockets**, **Playfield - Bayonet Type Bulbs and
Sockets** and **Playfield - Large Bayonet Type Bulbs and Sockets**. Read from the native 300 dpi render.
Every page carries the side note `† Items with Ø Qty. are not used in this game.` and the footer
`Note: Size and/or quantities may change during production.` Shaded rows are the zero-quantity ones.

## Wedge base (PDF page 59, printed 65)

Captions printed with the drawings above the table, in page order:

- Socket 1: "This socket is normally used on Lamp Boards to position bulbs vertically. (Use w/#555 &
  #906 Bulbs.)"
- Socket 2: "This socket is normally used in Pop Bumpers. (Use #555 Bulbs only.)", with the drawing
  note "2 Wires attached are approx. 12" ea."
- Socket 3: "This socket is normally used on Lamp Boards to position bulbs horizontally. (Use w/#555 &
  #906 Bulbs.)", with "Replacement Note: Socket color may be either black or white".
- Socket 4: "This socket is normally used with Reflectors.", with the drawing note "Note the notch."
- Socket 5: no caption.
- Socket 6: no caption; its drawing shows one socket "replaced with :" another.
- Bulb B (#906): no caption.
- Socket 7: "This socket is sometimes used in conjunction with Mini-Mars or special Butyrate
  assemblies."
- Socket 8: "This socket was used with an alternate insert using Insulation Displacement Connection
  vs. a Soldered-On Connection."
- Bulb C (Neon): "This Neon Bulb is used with Motor Assemblies for voltage spike suppression."

Crops: `-1` shows the captions of A and 1-3, `-7` those of 4-6, `-5` those of B, 7, 8 and C, and `-2`
the table.
