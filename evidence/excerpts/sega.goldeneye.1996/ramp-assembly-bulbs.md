# Sega GoldenEye (1996) — Ramp Assembly Signs and Lights

Source: Sega Pinball *GoldenEye* operations manual (`Sega_1996_Goldeneye_Manual.pdf`), PDF pages 76,
77 and 78, printed pages 82, 83 and 84 (Section 4, Chapter 2, Assembly Drawings), parts tables of the
**Right Plastic Ramp Assembly 500-5998-00-42** (continued on printed 83) and the **Center Plastic Ramp
Assembly 500-5999-00-42**. Read from the native 300 dpi render. The drawings above the tables are
blank in this scan. Only the sub-assembly blocks that carry a bulb are transcribed; each is complete
from its item row to the next item row.
