# Sega GoldenEye (1996) — Backbox General Parts: Speaker Panel Assembly

Source: Sega Pinball *GoldenEye* operations manual (`Sega_1996_Goldeneye_Manual.pdf`), PDF page 48,
printed page 54 (Section 4, Chapter 1, Parts Identification & Location), table **Backbox - General
Parts**. Read from the native 300 dpi render. The drawing shows the backbox with the CPU/Sound, I/O
Power Driver, Display Power Supply and 2-Flipper SSFB boards and, below them, the speaker panel (18)
holding the 128 X 32 Dot Matrix Display (18A) between two speaker grills (18G/18H).
