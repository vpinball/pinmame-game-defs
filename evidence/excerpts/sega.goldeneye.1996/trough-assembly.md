# Sega GoldenEye (1996) — 5-Ball Trough (OPTO) Assembly and Lock Ball Assembly

Source: Sega Pinball *GoldenEye* operations manual (`Sega_1996_Goldeneye_Manual.pdf`), PDF page 65,
printed page 71 (Section 4, Chapter 2, Assembly Drawings), **5-Ball Trough (OPTO) Assembly
500-5989-05-42**. Read from the native 300 dpi render. Three committed crops cover the two parts
tables (the trough table in two halves, the lock ball table in one); the drawing labels below were read
from the full-page render.

Drawing labels: five subminiature roller switches (items 3-6) in a row along the outhole mounting
bracket; at the right end an up-kicker coil labelled `23-800` (items 8-15) with the opto receiver
board `REC` (item 18) and, per the note, the opto transmitter board `TRANS` (item 17) "mounted on the
other side of the assembly, in line with the Receiver (REC) and with the same hardware"; above the
trough a lock-ball coil labelled `25-1240` (items 23-39) with its link, cam and plunger; and the ball
trough enter/exit scoop (item 40). Printed notes: "Items 23-39, Lock Ball Assembly 500-5684-01, is not
included with the 5-Ball Trough Assembly." and "Item 40, Ball Trough Enter/Exit Scoop 535-7329-00, is
not included with the 5-Ball Trough or Lock Ball Assemblies."
