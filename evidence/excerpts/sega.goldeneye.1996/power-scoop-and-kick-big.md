# Sega GoldenEye (1996) — Power Scoop Assembly and Kick Big Assembly

Source: Sega Pinball *GoldenEye* operations manual (`Sega_1996_Goldeneye_Manual.pdf`), PDF page 70,
printed page 76 (Section 4, Chapter 2, Assembly Drawings). Read from the native 300 dpi render.

Printed note: "The Power Scoop & Kick Big Asemblies work in conjunction with each other but are
separate assemblies." Headings: **Power Scoop Assembly (Operates with Kick Big Assembly below)
500-5809-00-42** and **Kick Big Assembly (Operates with Power Scoop Assembly above) 500-5862-00-42**.
The Power Scoop drawing shows the scoop weldment (1) with a micro switch (2) and diode (3) on its side;
the Kick Big drawing shows a vertical coil labelled `23-800` with plunger, spring and frame.
