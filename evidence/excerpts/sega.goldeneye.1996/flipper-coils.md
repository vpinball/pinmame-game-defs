Source: Sega Pinball *GoldenEye* operations manual (`Sega_1996_Goldeneye_Manual.pdf`), PDF page 36,
printed page 24 (Section 3, Chapter 2), table **FLIPPER COILS** at the foot of the page. Read from the
native 300 dpi render. The Find-It-In-Front page iv (PDF page 6) reprints it.
