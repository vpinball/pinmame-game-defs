# Sega GoldenEye (1996) — Satellite Assembly and Satellite Motor Base Assembly

Source: Sega Pinball *GoldenEye* operations manual (`Sega_1996_Goldeneye_Manual.pdf`), PDF pages 72
and 73, printed pages 78 and 79 (Section 4, Chapter 2, Assembly Drawings). Read from the native 300 dpi
render. Both drawings are blank in this scan; only the parts tables survive. The committed crop shows
the page 73 table (the motor base); the page 72 table is transcribed from its own render.
