"SOLENOID / FLASHER LOCATIONS" table. Read from the rendered page; the retained scan is image-only. Both are top
views with the backbox end at the top. The page legend reads "■ Square indicates coil, P.C.B. or Magnet" and
"● Circle indicates flasher". Callout numbers are the table's item numbers, so 37-44 are the auxiliary board's
printed numbers (public solenoids 51-58).
