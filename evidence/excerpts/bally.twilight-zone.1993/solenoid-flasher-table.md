Notes on the transcription:

- Rows 37-44 are the auxiliary (8-Driver) board's own numbers; the public PinMAME addresses are 51-58.
- Row 07 (Knocker) is the only solenoid row whose connections and part number sit in the Backbox columns.
- Rows 17, 18 and 19 print "(2)" in the Function cell; row 20 does not, although page 2-53 prints "Upper Left Ramp
  (2)" with two bulb rows for item 20.
- Row 27 prints the part number `AR-26-1500`; page 2-53 prints `AE-26-1500` for item 27.
- No row uses J130-3; the power driver board's connector list (page 3-33) lists J130-3 as the key.
- In every fitted coil row 01-28 (row 22 prints "----") the pin under Voltage Connections is the power driver
  board drive connection (a J130, J127, J125 or J124 pin; the connector list on page 3-33 names each one as that
  solenoid's drive) and the pin under Drive Connections its J107 or J109 supply pin, so the two column headings do
  not match the cells on any fitted solenoid row, the Knocker's included.
- In the General Illumination rows the pin under Voltage Connections is the string's return and the pin under
  Drive Connections its 6.8VAC feed: the connector list on page 3-33 names J120/J121 pins 1-6 "Return G.I." and pins
  7-11 "6.8VAC".
