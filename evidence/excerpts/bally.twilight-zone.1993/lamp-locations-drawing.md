# Twilight Zone — Lamp Locations drawing

Transcribed from `Twilight_Zone_OPS.pdf`, PDF page 63, printed page 2-55, the playfield drawing to the right of the
"LAMP LOCATIONS" table. Read from the rendered page; the retained scan is image-only. Top view with the backbox end
at the top. Only the door-panel group and the cabinet buttons are transcribed here; the other callouts sit on
the inserts named in the table.

Door panel, the insert group in the middle of the playfield between the jet bumpers and the slingshots:

| Position in the door panel | Callout printed |
| --- | --- |
| Top row, left to right | 17, 18, 27 |
| Left column, top to bottom | 16, 15, 14, 13 |
| Right column, top to bottom | 26, 25, 24, 23 |
| Bottom row, left to right | 12, 11, 22 |
| Inner upper pair, left to right | 36, 28 |
| Round insert between the pairs | 34 |
| Inner lower pair, left to right | 32, 31 |

The inner lower-right insert is printed "31". Callout 31 is printed a second time at the left edge of the
playfield, and no callout 21 appears anywhere on the drawing; the facing table lists 21 as Door Panel "Lock 2" and
31 as Left Extra Ball. The inner "31" is therefore read as a misprint for 21, which is where the retained table
places Light l21.

Below the playfield outline, at the cabinet front: 88 at the left and 87 at the right (Credit Button and Buy-In
Button in the table). Items 76-78, 81 and 83-86 are marked "*" (not shown) in the table.
