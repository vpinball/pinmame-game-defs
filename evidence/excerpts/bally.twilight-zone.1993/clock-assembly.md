Socket detail of the 2-33 drawing (the committed crop): the clock mounting bracket (callout 24) carries two lamp
sockets side by side. Callout 25 has two leader lines, one to each socket. Callout 27 points to the bulb sleeve of one
socket and callout 29 to the bulb sleeve of the other, so one bulb wears the red sleeve (item 27) and the other the
yellow sleeve (item 29). Callouts 31 and 32 point to the diode and its tubing on the socket leads, and callout 19 to a
screw at the bracket's edge.

Manual Amendment 16-50020-AMD-1 changes this page's assembly number to A-16124-1 and item 13 to A-17047 (see
`manual-amendment-clock-assembly.md`); it changes none of items 24-29.
