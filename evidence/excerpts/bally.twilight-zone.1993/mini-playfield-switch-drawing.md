# Twilight Zone — Mini-playfield, top and bottom ramp switch locations (printed 2-50)

Transcribed from `Bally_1993_Twilight_Zone_Operations_Manual_OCR_searchable.pdf` (the complete IPDB copy of
operations manual 16-50020-101), PDF page 118, printed page 2-50, the drawing captioned "MINI-PLAYFIELD, TOP AND
BOTTOM RAMP SWITCH LOCATIONS" at the lower right of the page (beside the Switch Locations table for items F1-F8 and
11-33). This page is missing from the retained Internet Archive scan. Read from the 300 dpi render.

The drawing is a perspective detail of the rear left of the playfield: the mini-playfield at the lower left, the
ramps above and to the right. Each switch is marked by a filled black balloon carrying its number, with a short
pointer tail towards the switch; the balloons are remote callouts, not the switch positions themselves.

The Switch Locations table on page 2-51 prints items 45 "Mini-playfield Left (2)" and 46 "Mini-playfield Right (2)"
(see `switch-locations-continued.md`); this drawing agrees on the count (two balloons each), the side (45 left, 46
right) and the order (one on the side rail, one on the bottom rail).
