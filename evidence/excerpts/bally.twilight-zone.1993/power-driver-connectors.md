Notes on the transcription:

- The G.I. returns split by connector: J121 pins 1, 2, 3 and 6 (Brown, Orange, Yellow, Violet) go "to playfield",
  J120 pins 2, 3 and 5 (Orange, Yellow, Green) "to insert". The Orange (string 02) and Yellow (string 03) returns
  appear on both connectors; Brown (01) and Violet (05) only on J121; Green (04) only on J120. This matches the
  Playfield and Backbox columns of the page 2-52 G.I. rows.
- J130-8 (solenoid 7, the knocker) carries the same "to playfield coil" wording as every other J130 pin, while page
  2-52 prints the knocker's J130-8 under its Backbox column and page 2-4 lists the knocker assembly in the backbox.
