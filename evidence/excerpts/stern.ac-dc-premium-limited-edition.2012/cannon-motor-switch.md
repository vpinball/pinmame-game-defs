# AC/DC Premium cannon motor and switch assembly

Source: ACDC_Prem_web.pdf, PDF page 46, Cannon motor and switch / AC/DC cannon assemblies.

The exploded drawings and parts tables identify the cannon motor-and-switch bracket, opto interrupter board, micro-switches, plunger assembly, motor, coil, shaft, spring, and cable harness. This is the retained mechanical evidence for the rotating cannon and its position contacts.
