# The Walking Dead Premium / LE — coil locations excerpt

Source: `Walking Dead pinball Manual.pdf`, PDF page 14, crop box 0.06,0.08,0.95,0.90. The crop transcribes the playfield coil-location drawing and the typical coil-wiring schematic.

The drawing labels the trough/launcher region, pop bumpers 10–12, magnets 6–7, left pop 9, flashers 19, 20, 25–29, 31–32, upper/mid ramp power and hold 3–4 and 5–6, diverter 5, drop-target bank 52–53 and 55–56, cannon 51, slings 13–16, and cabinet/coin-side outputs 1–2. The typical Q1 trough up-kicker schematic shows +50VDC YEL-VIO at J10-P9/10, BRN-BLK at J8, a 1N4004 diode on the I/O power-driver PCB, and coil 26-1200.
