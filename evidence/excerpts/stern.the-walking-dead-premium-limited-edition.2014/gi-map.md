# The Walking Dead Premium / LE — GI map excerpt

Source: `Walking Dead pinball Manual.pdf`, PDF page 135, crop box 0.06,0.08,0.95,0.90, rotated 90 degrees counter-clockwise after rendering. The crop transcribes the physical GI map, the five-socket rear-panel inset, and the printed LED part counts.

The printed inventory is 23 bayonet 112-5034-08 LED (white), 16 bayonet 112-5034-02 LED (red), and 3 bayonet 112-5034-04 LED (green) on the playfield map. The rear inset shows two red and three green bayonet sockets on the staple-bayonet socket panel; the circuit legend identifies GI-0-WHT, GI-1-BACK PANEL, GI-2-GRN, and GI-3-RED with their wire colors and board connector pins. The map marks the physical sockets around the Premium/LE playfield rather than the broad render-light pools used by a VPX table.
