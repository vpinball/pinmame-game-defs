Method: mixed; primary GPT Sol checked retained factory supplement renders and the independent root physical reads. Reviewed: true. The literal wire/color roles below are retained independently of public-address association. SS41 (21April1987), both PDF pages, and the undated diverter binding bulletin are authoritative for physical updates. An owner's2019 retrofit is a lead, not a factory release chart.

## Service Bulletin SS41

Upper flippers change to FL11722 green-label coils to improve target life/playability. Figure1 names four loop-plastic domes RED1 lower-left, BLUE upper-center, CLEAR upper-right, RED2 lower-right. BEFORE wiring in Figure2: RED1 and CLEAR orange plus white/violet; BLUE red plus yellow; RED2 yellow jumper plus white/black. The instructions remove both lower red bulb/socket/bracket assemblies. CLEAR is disconnected from orange/white-violet and connected to the former RED2 yellow/white-black connection; BLUE remains. Figure3 moves white/black on the nearby resistor board from left LAMP terminal to the right LAMP terminal with the white lead, so the complete update changes circuit membership as well as bulb count. Do not apply only a quantity decrement to an old callback.

The retained factory solenoid table is an as-printed population:01C-07C each2 playfield bulbs,08C3 playfield bulbs,09 and 15 each2 backbox+1 playfield. The older/new VPX ancestor models the earlier four-dome configuration:31 drives a white and a red dome,15 a red dome,9 a blue dome. Public31's original pair is RED1+CLEAR, not a silently inferred pair of reds. The post-update two-dome state and restoration wiring must be disclosed separately. No photograph or document associates a unique physical population with p3,p4,p5 firmware; field ROM/dome replacements make such an inference unreliable.

## Diverter gate binding correction

The undated bulletin addresses insufficient plunger inside the coil at rest and gates binding at the launch-trough ports. GateE presses to the right until flush with its port. StopD is bent until gateF is flush. Replace steel washerL with plastic03-8042, use E-ring20-8712-18, and adjust the rest geometry so the plungers enter the coils sufficiently. These are physical home/stop, spring-return and failure-mode facts. Table-owned rotation angles, speeds and ball-kick strengths are not factory dimensions.
