# F-14 Tomcat factory updates and physical population

Method: mixed; primary GPT Sol checked retained factory supplement renders and the independent root physical reads. Reviewed: true. The literal wire labels and drawn connections below are retained independently of public-address association. SS41 (21 April 1987), both PDF pages, and the undated diverter binding bulletin are authoritative for physical updates. An owner's 2019 retrofit is a lead, not a factory release chart.

## Service Bulletin SS41

Upper flippers change to FL11722 green-label coils to improve target life/playability. Figure 1 names four loop-plastic domes: RED1 at lower left, BLUE at upper center, CLEAR at upper right and RED2 at lower right.

Figure 2 retains these literal labels and connections:

| Dome | BEFORE | AFTER |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RED1 | ORANGE at one socket connection; WHT/VIO at the other | Bulb/socket/bracket removed; orange wires joined and insulated, white/violet lead individually insulated |
| BLUE | RED and YEL label the left-hand connection; WHT labels the right-hand connection | The same RED, YEL and WHT annotations and drawn connections remain |
| CLEAR, renamed CHGD after the update | WHT/VIO at the left-hand connection; ORANGE at the right-hand connection | YEL at the left-hand connection; WHT/BLK at the right-hand connection |
| RED2 | YEL JUMPER at the left-hand connection; WHT/BLK at the right-hand connection | Bulb/socket/bracket removed; its connections are transferred as directed to the remaining CLEAR socket |

The BLUE annotations are transcribed as printed, without reinterpreting them as a striped wire or inferring an additional wire. The instructions remove both lower red bulb/socket/bracket assemblies. CLEAR is disconnected from its former orange and white/violet circuit; the remaining BLUE and CHGD sockets share the YEL connection shown in the AFTER diagram. Figure 3 moves WHT/BLK on the nearby resistor board from the left LAMP terminal to the right LAMP terminal with WHT. The complete update therefore changes circuit membership as well as bulb count. Do not apply only a quantity decrement to an old callback.

The retained factory solenoid table shows the as-printed population: 01C-07C each have 2 playfield bulbs; 08C has 3 playfield bulbs; 09 and 15 each have 2 backbox bulbs and 1 playfield bulb. The older and newer VPX builds share an ancestor that models the earlier four-dome configuration: public 31 drives a white and a red dome, 15 a red dome, and 9 a blue dome. Public 31's original pair is RED1 plus CLEAR. The post-update two-dome state and restoration wiring must be disclosed separately. No photograph or document associates a unique physical population with P-3, P-4 or P-5 firmware; field ROM/dome replacements make such an inference unreliable.

## Diverter gate binding correction

The undated bulletin addresses insufficient plunger insertion inside the coil at rest and gates binding at the launch-trough ports. Gate E presses to the right until flush with its port. Stop D is bent until gate F is flush. Replace steel washer L with plastic washer 03-8042, use E-ring 20-8712-18, and adjust the rest geometry so the plungers enter the coils sufficiently. These are physical home/stop, spring-return and failure-mode facts. Table-owned rotation angles, speeds and ball-kick strengths are not factory dimensions.
