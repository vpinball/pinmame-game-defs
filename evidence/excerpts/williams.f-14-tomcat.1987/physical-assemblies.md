## Location and construction facts (PDF 45/printed 40; PDF 52-53/printed 47-48)

Flipper assembly notes distinguish the normally closed power-winding EOS contact from the lane-change Switch & Diode Assembly B-9951, part SW-1A-150. Upper flipper assembly variants omit the lane-change switch. PinMAME does not simulate those physical EOS contacts for this game and supplies matrix 15/63 from cabinet buttons instead.

The lamp location drawing labels every matrix address 1-64. Only 24 and 39 each have two labeled locations, hence 66 controlled bulbs. Lamp 24 serves the two drain lanes; 39 serves both flipper return lanes. Three launch-ramp signs each have a pair: top 60/57, middle 61/58, bottom 62/59, with blue landing above red lock. They are playfield ramp bulbs, not backbox lights. The drawing does not enumerate G.I. bulbs; unnumbered circles also depict posts.

The switch parts/location list explicitly fits switch 9 (SW-1A-117 Playfield Tilt), marks 17/18/19/27/29/39/40/64 Not Used, and prints 15/63 as right/left flipper EOS. Its cabinet flipper-button row is SW-1010A-13. Physical contacts are mechanical, with ramp/trough switches explicitly microswitches, and target/spinner/rollover/bumper/sling contacts leaf assemblies. Do not infer an opto from a VPX trigger.
