## Interpretation

The insert panel lists seven A-8798 socket/#89 bulb assemblies and no #906 flasher. The seven #89 backbox bulbs match the manual circuits 09, 04C, 05C, 06C (three) and 07C (public 9, 28, 29, 30 x 3, 31). Both playfield ramp assemblies #3 and #1 carry H-13745 flash cables with black/gray control wires; the handbook assigns black/gray to 08C (public 32). Both ramps fit flashlamp assemblies. Together with two physical right-side domes and the working table binding both to 32, this settles the table row 08C location marks 1p1b as a printing error: two playfield lamps, no extra backbox lamp. The top-center label on PDF 84 is blurred (6C/8C) and remote from the second output-32 dome; the drawing is not used to establish two 08C locations. Quantities and literal marks remain preserved in handbook-tables.md.

The 35 #44 lamps on the insert panel do not establish a complete playfield GI count; no circuit quantity is inferred from a missing child component.
