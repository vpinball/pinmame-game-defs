Author Soren identifies aa9e in post45 (November18,2018). Posts17/45 describe software
fixes for diverter, kickback, GI, lock accounting, ball search, multiball and tail-slider
behavior. John_I's post20 reports a physical tournament installation of an earlier
iteration. Compatibility of aa9e is an inference from that installation, the later
software-only update and pinned unchanged routing; exact aa9e was not replayed here.
Post26 says the lock releases all balls together, including with unmodified firmware.
The table's two kicker objects therefore do not imply independently controlled coils.
