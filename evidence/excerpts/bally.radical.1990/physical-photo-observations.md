# Radical physical-photo review

The originals remain outside Git under the retained Radical session. Exact resource and
Wayback capture URLs, acquisition times, photographers and file hashes are recorded on each
source and in `photos-ipdb/retained-photos-manifest.json`. Photographers retain copyright;
no photo is redistributed here. Method: visual observation by primary curator, 2026-11-06.

- Michael Sciulli's production upper playfield, IPDB photo13522 (`image-2.jpg`, SHA256
  `76475890cdda7187dedf466e504eebbf3046c62430321dba01b3831f24d4ff89`),
  shows the four bumper region, bowtie paddle, upper flippers, ramps and raised green/purple
  flasher domes. The prototype's additional sling does not appear in that assembly.
- His middle playfield, photo13523 (`image-3.jpg`, SHA256
  `7daafd595c433ee7251de1fa318c8e5f22a16dfc0c53fa07251afd34e749f75b`),
  shows the lower lock-lane dome, lower right-side green dome and the clear left curl ramp.
  Together with the upper photo, there are two distinct right-side green dome locations.
  A photograph alone does not prove which electrical circuit powers them.
- Neo Skywalker's prototype upper playfield, photo31926 (`image-10.jpg`, SHA256
  `3d0cb2342df9cf98efb83771d9a9ff7a064deea17526541c2c1a8df62915bf22`),
  shows the additional kicker/sling arm and rubber beside the upper-right flipper region.
  The listing explicitly identifies this as prototype-only hardware.
- His prototype VUK detail, photo31927 (`image-13.jpg`, SHA256
  `4285bb8c5be494a677081ca3a04a9923e596d2cd0d888195e4b40afdf17fb486`),
  shows the ball hole/receiver and switch assembly that the listing says production omits.

The views corroborate construction, side/order and prototype differences. No pixel in an
oblique photograph is used as a normalized coordinate, and no electrical binding or lamp
quantity is inferred solely from a lit photograph. The manual, working script and own-machine
parts bill must establish those roles separately. The production/prototype split is also
supported by the ROMs' different names at switches16/49 and outputs3/15.
