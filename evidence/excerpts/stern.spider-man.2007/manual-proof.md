# Spider-Man service-manual proof

Source: `Spider-Man_Manual.pdf`, Stern part 780-5094-00, SHA-256 `63462d261c7558739b198e96db47a32d7edd4e01bb1e89cd3483736c51043cbe`.

## Switch matrix

PDF page 6 prints the complete 1-64 switch matrix and the D-1 through D-32 dedicated-switch block. The dedicated block identifies D-15 as the upper-right flipper button and D-16 as its EOS switch; D-17 through D-24 cover tilt, slam tilt, ticket notch, service Back/Minus/Plus/Select, and D-25 through D-32 are the eight CPU DIP positions.

PDF page 7 plots the matrix switches on the playfield and states that no switches are located on the back panel. Green markers identify opto switch pairs, including the Goblin ramp, trough, ramp, Sandman, and Doc Ock sensors.

## Lamp matrix

PDF page 8 prints the complete 1-80 lamp matrix. It identifies lamp 1 as the Start button, lamp 2 as the Tournament Start button, the six rear-panel lamps at 66-71, and unused positions including 73, 79, and 80.

PDF page 9 plots every fitted controlled lamp and the six 66-71 controlled rear-panel lamps. Its caption distinguishes controlled lamps from the top ten clear rear-panel GI bulbs.

## Coils and flashers

PDF page 10 prints the detailed Q1-Q32 output chart plus auxiliary outputs Q33-Q35. Among the game-specific assignments are Q3 Doc Ock magnet, Q4 Doc Ock VUK, Q5 Doc Ock motor/relay, Q12 Sandman VUK, Q13 Sandman motor/relay, Q19 Green Goblin, Q20 Sandman three-bank motor/relay, Q21 Doc Ock flasher, Q23 two Sandman flashers, Q25 two Venom flashers, Q28 two Green Goblin flashers, Q29/Q30 rear-panel flashers, and Q31 three pop-bumper flashers; Q32 is not used.

PDF page 11 plots the playfield, underside, and rear-panel locations for those coils and flashers. It shows both Q23 Sandman bulbs, both Q25 Venom bulbs, both Q28 Green Goblin bulbs, the three Q31 pop-bumper bulbs, and the separate Q29/Q30 rear-panel emitters.

## Four-ball trough

PDF page 85 identifies the 500-6318-24-ND four-ball trough. The drawing shows four steel balls, Q1 with coil 26-1200, three trough microswitches 18-20, and the two dual-opto boards carrying switches 21 and 22.

## Green Goblin

PDF page 91 identifies Green Goblin assembly 511-5058-00 and its separate coil-actuator assembly. The actuator is Q19, coil 28-900, explicitly marked `NO DIODE`.

## Doc Ock

PDF page 95 identifies the Doc Ock Webslinger assembly. It includes the Q21 flasher and the switch-63 opto pair on the moving ball guide.

## Sandman three-bank

PDF page 100 identifies the Sandman three-bank up/down motor assembly. Q20 is the 24 VAC synchronous motor/relay, switches 49 and 50 are the down and up limits, and the target-bank drawing labels switches 9, 10, and 11.

## Driver-board wiring

PDF page 119 prints the game-specific I/O power-driver wiring. Connector J6 carries Q25-Q32, J7 carries Q17-Q24, J8 carries Q1-Q8, and J9 carries Q9-Q16, with the same game-specific output names as the detailed chart.

## General illumination

PDF page 121 prints the four fused GI circuits and their physical bulb diagram: brown/white on the left side, yellow/white on the right side, green/white for ten rear-panel bulbs plus two US coin-door bulbs (three Euro), and violet/white on the upper-right playfield. The diagram separately plots the playfield and rear-panel emitters.
