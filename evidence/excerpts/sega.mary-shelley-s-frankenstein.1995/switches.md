## Switch-relevant text on PDF page 34 (printed page 30, "SWITCH TESTS")

> "Switches are configured in an 8 x 8 matrix of columns (switch drives) and rows (switch returns) with up to 64 switches possible. The switch tests include three parts; switch test, active switches, and bad switches. Column and Row wire colors are indicated in each test using corresponding resistor color code numbers."

> "To initiate, push the STEP push-button switch, until the display indicates SWITCH TEST. Close each switch and observe the display. The display will indicate the switch name, column wire colors, row wire colors and the switch number. When a switch is released, the name and number disappear until another switch is closed or the test is exited."

> "Push the STEP push-button switch from the Switch Test. The display will indicate ACTIVE SWITCHES. If any switches are stuck closed (or made from the presence of a ball), the display sequences through switch names, and indicates the row and column wire colors, and the switch numbers are indicated in the display. This cycle continues until all switches are cleared or until the STEP push-button switch is depressed."

The page's switch schematic is labelled: "(Column: Switch Drive Wire)" GRN-XXX, "Blocking Diode", "(Row: Switch Return Wire)" WHT -XXX, terminals "N.O.*", "COM.*", "N.C.*" with "* Switch Terminals"; the switch drawing shows "N.C. N.O. COM." with leads "WHT" and "GRN" and a diode labelled "Diode 1N4001". The page makes no statement about opto switches.
