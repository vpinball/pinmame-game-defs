layer, which misreads digits and glyphs badly on this two-column board layout. Header: `Yellow (B+)`

Lamp 41 and lamp 76 are both printed `Not Used`. Lamp 88 (Credit Button) is a cabinet/apron lamp
sharing the button assembly, not a playfield insert. Lamps 14, 17, 43, 44, 48 spell `G-R-A-V-E`
across four different matrix columns (not a contiguous run), independent of the jet-bumper
`G-R-E-E-D` word at column 3.
