## Drawing callouts, page 40

Read from the drawing enlarged in two crops. The lower playfield is drawn with its two flippers (item 22 on page 39)
near the top centre, below a long diagonal guide rail, because it slopes away from the player. Where the callouts sit:

- SW43 is at the upper end of the ball tube along the right edge; SW53 on the track under the top diagonal rail.
- L5 and L6 are two round inserts at the top centre between the flippers' level and the drop banks; L4 is a round insert
  in the centre.
- SW70, SW60, SW50 and SW40 run down the left drop bank from top to bottom; L47, L46, L45 and L44 are a column of round
  inserts beside it, from top to bottom.
- SW41, SW51 and SW61 run along the right (white) drop bank from its right end to its left end.
- L19 is printed three times, at three arrow inserts on the left; L20 three times, at three arrow inserts on the right.
- SW62 is in the left lane; SW72 is printed three times, at the kicking target on the left lane wall, at a rubber on the
  right below L6, and at the lower right kicking rubber; SW71 twice, at the pop bumper at the bottom centre and the one
  on the right.
- SW42 is the hole at the lower left; SW52 the gate at the lower right edge.
