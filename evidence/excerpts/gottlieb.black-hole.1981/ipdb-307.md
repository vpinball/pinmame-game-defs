Source: the Internet Pinball Database page `https://www.ipdb.org/machine.cgi?id=307`, retrieved through the Wayback

"Manufacturer: D. Gottlieb & Company, a Columbia Pictures Industries Company (1977-1983) [Trade Name: Gottlieb]"
"Date Of Manufacture: October, 1981" "Model Number: 668" "Common Abbreviations: BH" "MPU: Gottlieb System 80" "Type:
Solid State Electronic (SS)" "Production: 8,774 units (confirmed)" "Specialty: Widebody Mechanical Backbox Animation"

"Notable Features: Flippers (6), Pop bumpers (6), Slingshots (3), 5-bank drop targets (1), 4-bank drop targets (2),
3-bank drop targets (1), Kick-out holes (2), Kick target (1), Horseshoe lane (1), Spinning target (1), Rollunder (1),
Cellar hole (1), Right outlane ball detour gate. Score display in playfield. Backglass animation (rotating disc,
non-export only). First machine to offer a lower playfield sloped away from the player with the flippers at the backbox
end. Multiball and speech (non-export only)."

"Design by: John Buras, Shing Lam" "Art by: Terry Doerzaph" "Notes: ... Speech generated by a Votrax SC-01."

Documentation listed: "English Manual (Availability limited by copyright) [Gottlieb Amusement Games]"; no file is
linked. ROMs listed: "PinMAME Romset", "PinMAME Romset (Rev. 2)", "PinMAME Romset (sound only)".
