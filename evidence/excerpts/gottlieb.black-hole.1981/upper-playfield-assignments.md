## Drawing callouts

Read from the drawing enlarged in four crops. Each switch and lamp callout appears once, except SW06 (pop bumpers),
which is printed at each of the four pop bumpers (three in the upper centre and right, one on the left side above the
left kicking rubber), and SW34 (10 point switches), which is printed at five places: beside the left (B-L-A-C-K) drop
bank, beside the right (H-O-L-E) drop bank, on the left rail below SW05, above the triangular left kicking rubber, and
beside a rubber-ringed post left of SW35 on the right. The lower-playfield lamps (L4-L6, L19, L20, L44-L47) do not
appear. Where the drawing places the other devices:

- SW00, SW10 and SW20 are the three top rollover lanes under L41, L42 and L43 at the top centre.
- SW24 is at the top of the left arc of lanes, with L36, L35 and L34 below it in that arc; SW33 is in the U-shaped lane
  further left.
- SW03, SW13, SW23, SW04 and SW14 are along the left drop bank from its lower end upwards; SW02, SW12, SW22 and SW32
  along the right drop bank from its upper end downwards, with L40 and SW30 in the right-hand lane beside it.
- L21-L25 and L26-L29 are the two diagonal rows of arrow inserts in front of the left and right drop banks, and
  L30-L33 the row of four round inserts above the window.
- SW16 is at the left spinning target in the left lane, with L7 below it; SW05 is the hole at the left edge further
  down, with L37 and L38 below it.
- SW01, SW11, SW21 and SW31 run down the right edge of the window beside L48, L49, L50 and L51; L39 and SW35 are below
  them at the right return lane.
- L3 is between the lower flippers; SW26 is at the lower left on the apron line, SW15 below the flipper gap and SW25 at
  the lower right.
- A six-digit "888 888" display is drawn inside the lower edge of the window, and a four-digit "88 88" display at the
  lower left of the outline, above the apron's left card frame.
