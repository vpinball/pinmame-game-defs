The drawing shows the trough from the left entry end to the right kicker end with these
printed labels: the three roller microswitches (item 9, "Qty. 3", with item 10 protect plates
"Qty. 3") are labelled SW. 18, SW. 19 and a third unlabelled-but-shown switch of the same item
under the guide plate; the Dual OPTO boards are labelled SW. 22 (on board 12, transmitter) and

"Enter / Exit Scoop" and item AP-B as four steel balls "Qty. 4".
