# Terminator 2: Judgment Day - solenoid, flashlamp, GI, and lower-flipper table

Source: `Terminator 2 Judgement Day Operations Manual.pdf` (ARC ARC retained source SHA-256 `8540d654b39c58ad3b19ece0f42eb1dfdb8460d249e9480f8906385c8ecdb16b`), PDF page 48, printed page 1-28, "Solenoid Table". The full visible table is retained in the crops and was read manually from the image-only scan. `PL` and `BB` are the manual's playfield and backbox bulb-count abbreviations.
