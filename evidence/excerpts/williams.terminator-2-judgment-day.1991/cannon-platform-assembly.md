# Terminator 2: Judgment Day - cannon platform assembly

Source: `Terminator 2 Judgement Day Operations Manual.pdf` (ARC ARC retained source SHA-256 `8540d654b39c58ad3b19ece0f42eb1dfdb8460d249e9480f8906385c8ecdb16b`), PDF page 90, printed page 2-24, A-14504 Platform Gun Assembly. The source scan is sideways; the retained crop is rotated 90 degrees clockwise so the plan and parts list are readable. The fact extraction was performed manually from the drawing and list.

The non-fastener parts list identifies A-14505 Platform/Kicker Assembly (with a page 2-25 cross-reference), motor pitch 14-7963, motor-mounting bracket 01-10061, Magnalube Teflon grease EI-204, bushing-staked assembly A-14459, switch 5647-12693-31, plate bushing 01-10060, subminiature switch 5647-12693-06, insulator switch 01-8600, nut plate 01-8240, cam-gun A-14706, cannon cable H-14576, diode 5070-09054-00/1N4004, two E-ring retainers, motor cable H-14943, and switch bracket 01-10228. This is physical evidence for a rotating motor-driven gun platform with two mounted switches and a wired cannon; it does not establish the logical home/mark windows or motor position scale, which remain VPX-controller semantics.
