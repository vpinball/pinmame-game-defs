## Observations on the printed text

- Item 84's bulb part number prints `24-6768`; every other bulb number in the list is `24-8768` or `24-6549`, and
  the legend defines only `24-6549 = #44 Bulb` and `24-8768 = #555`. `24-6768` is therefore probably a typographical
  error for `24-6549` or `24-8768` (items 85 and 86, the other two `A-17835*` lamps of the `(3)PT` group, print
  `24-8768`), but it is transcribed as printed.
- Items 38, 48 and 57-58 (the lane lamps) use `24-6549` (`#44` bulb); the `A-17835*` assembly of 38 and 48 has no
  socket number, while 57 and 58 use assembly `A-17807` with socket `A-17806`.
- Items 84-86 print the descriptions `(3) PT.`, `3 (P)T.` and `3 P(T)` with spaces and (for the first two) a trailing
  period, where the matrix prints `(3)PT`, `3(P)T` and `3P(T)`.
- The list's `IN THE PAINT` numbering (67 = 4, 68 = 3, 77 = 1, 78 = 2) matches the matrix (`lamp-matrix.md`).

## Balloon numbers visible on the drawing (printed page 2-45)

The drawing is a playfield plan view. The numbers are printed as circled, boxed or bare numerals at the lamp positions (arrowheads for 41-47), and many in the lower playfield are printed very faintly in light grey. Numbers read, in groups:

- Clearly legible: 11, 12, 13, 34 (boxed), 35, 36, 37, 38, 41, 42, 43, 44, 45, 46, 47, 48 (boxed), 51, 52, 53, 54, 55, 56, 57, 58, 62, 63, 64, 65, 66, 67, 68, 72, 73, 74, 75, 76, 77, 78, 81, 82, 83, 84, 85, 86, 87, 88.
- Light grey, read with difficulty: 14, 15, 16, 18, 31 (boxed), 32 (boxed), 33 (boxed), 61 (boxed), 71, and the pair printed side by side near the lower centre that reads `24` and `25`.
- Not read: 17, 21, 22, 23, 26, 27, 28 (the faint grey region in the lower-centre playfield holds several numerals too faint to read).

Every number read belongs to an item of the list (11-18, 21-28, 31-38, 41-48, 51-58, 61-68, 71-78, 81-88); no number outside the list was seen. Balloons 77, 78 and 84 stand left of the playfield and 67, 68, 85 and 86 stand right of it, each on a leader line; `88` and `87` are drawn below the apron, outside the playfield outline, without leader lines.
