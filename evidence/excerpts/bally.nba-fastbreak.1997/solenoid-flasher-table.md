# NBA Fastbreak — Solenoid/Flasher Table

Transcribed from `Bally_1997_NBA_Fastbreak_Operations_Manual_May_1997_Final_with_schematics.pdf` (document
16-50053.1-101, May 1997), PDF page 68, left half of the 2-up spread, printed page `2-50`: the `SOLENOID/FLASHER
TABLE` with its main table (solenoids 01-28), the General Illumination block (strings 01-05), the Flipper Circuits
block (29-36), the Motor & Shot Clock Circuits block (37-40), the legend line and the footnotes. The right half of
the PDF page is blank apart from the printed folio `2-51`. The same table in the March 1997 edition
(`Bally_1997_NBA_Fastbreak_Operations_Manual_Final_no_schematics.pdf`, PDF page 124, printed page `2-46`) was
compared cell by cell. Read from the rendered page (300 dpi scan), not from the OCR text.

Main table columns, in print order: `Sol. No.`, `Function`, `Solenoid Type`, `Voltage Connections` (sub-columns
`Playfield`, `Backbox`, `Cabinet`), `Drive Xistor`, `Drive Connections` (sub-columns `Playfield`, `Backbox`,
`Cabinet`), `Drive Wire Color`, `Solenoid Part Number / Flashlamp Type` (sub-columns `Playfield`, `Backbox`).
`(blank)` is a cell with nothing printed. `Cabinet` columns print nothing in any main-table row except G.I. string
5 (below).

Notes on this block:

- Rows 02, 21 and 23 print `NOT USED` as the function. Row 02 prints its drive transistor (`Q68`) and wire colour
  (`VIO-RED`) but no connections or part number; rows 21 and 23 print their transistors (`Q26`, `Q25`) and wire
  colours (`BLU-GRN`, `BLU-VIO`) with no voltage connection, drive connection or flashlamp type.
- Row 20's playfield flashlamp type prints with a space after the `#` (`# 906 (1)`); row 19's does not. Both
  editions print it so.
- Main-table rows with a Backbox voltage connection: 07 (`J133-2`), 19 and 20 (`J134-5` each). Rows with a Backbox
  drive connection: 07 (`J117-3`), 19 (`J112-3`) and 20 (`J112-5`). In row 07 the Playfield voltage and Playfield
  drive cells are blank and only the Backbox cells carry a connection.
- Function names are printed exactly as shown, including the abbreviations `FLSHR` and `FLSH` (row 24 reads
  `LOWER RIGHT/LEFT FLSH`, filling its cell). `NOT USED` in rows 02 and 23 is indented by one space in the cell.
- The Solenoid Type column prints `High Power` (01-08), `Low Power` (09-16), `Flasher` (17-24) and `Gen. Purpose`
  (25-28).

Layout notes for this block:

- The `Function` cell `LOWER RIGHT FLIPPER` is one merged cell spanning rows 29-30 (it is printed on the line of row
  30, with the row-29 half blank); `LOWER LEFT FLIPPER` is likewise one merged cell spanning rows 31-32. They are
  repeated above on the lower row only, with the upper row marked blank.
- The `Coil Part No.` cell `FL-11630` and the `Coil Colors` cell `RED` are each one merged cell spanning rows 29-30,
  and again one merged cell spanning rows 31-32 (printed once per pair, level with the Power row). They are
  not repeated on the Hold rows.
- Rows 33-36 print `AE-23-800` once per row in a single cell that spans both the `Coil Part No.` and the `Coil Colors`
  columns (no rule separates the two columns on those rows); the table above shows it in `Coil Part No.` and `(blank)`
  in `Coil Colors`, which is therefore a spanned cell, not an empty one.
- The `Drive Wire Colors` sub-columns: Power wires are printed left-aligned in the column (`YEL-GRN`), Hold wires
  right-aligned (`ORG-GRN`); the page prints the Power wire of every pair on the Power row and the Hold wire on the
  Hold row.
- Voltage connection cells print the connector pin and the wire colour in parentheses with two spaces between them
  (`J119-1  (RED-GRN)`); the left edge of the leading `J` is clipped by the cell border in the scan.
- The Flipper block prints no `Backbox` or `Cabinet` columns.

## The Section 3 reprint (May edition, PDF page 71, right half, printed page `3-5`)

The May edition prints this table a second time on PDF page 71, right half, printed `3-5`. It was compared with the
`2-50` reading above in every block (solenoids 01-28, General Illumination, Flipper Circuits, Motor & Shot Clock
Circuits, legend and footnotes, and the closing `MOTOR WIRING DIAGRAM IS SHOWN ON PAGE 3-26.` / `SHOT CLOCK WIRING
DIAGRAM IS SHOWN ON PAGE 3-25.` lines) and agrees cell for cell (the reprint also prints `# 906 (1)` with the space in
row 20 and `NOT USED` for rows 02, 21 and 23). Its footnote text reads `**THESE GENERAL ILLUMINATION STRINGS DO NOT
BRIGHTEN AND DIM, THEY ARE ALWAYS ON.` (with `GENERAL ILLUMINATION` spelled out where the `2-50` page prints `G.I.`).

## Differences between the March (PDF page 124, printed `2-46`) and May (PDF page 68, printed `2-50`) printings

No difference in any cell of any block (every row of the main table, the G.I. block, the Flipper Circuits block, the
Motor & Shot Clock block, the headers, the legend line and the footnotes were compared on rendered crops). The two
pages are the same table; both print the stray space in `# 906 (1)` of row 20, both print the `NOT USED` rows 02, 21
and 23 with the same blank cells, and both print the same two page references (`3-26`, `3-25`). The only difference
is the printed folio: `2-46` in March, `2-50` in May.
