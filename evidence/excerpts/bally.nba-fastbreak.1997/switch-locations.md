## Footnote (verbatim, bold)

```
*NOT SHOWN.   **SCORE SWITCHES HAVE DIODES ATTACHED.
```

No item in the list carries a single leading `*`; the `**` is printed in items 57 and 58 (`A-17794 (**SCORE)`).

## Observations on the printed text

- Items F2, F4 and F6, F8 describe the flipper opto boards as `... FLIPPER CABINET` (not `OPTO`); the matrix page
  (`switch-matrix.md`) calls F2, F4, F6 and F8 `... Flipper Opto`. Their assembly cell is `A-17316` for F2 and F4 and
  `NOT USED` for F6 and F8; F5 and F7 are the basket switches (`BASKET MADE`, `BASKET HOLD`).
- Item 31's description prints `TROUGH ELECT` (probably a typographical error for `TROUGH EJECT`, which is what the
  matrix prints for cell 31).
- Items 65-68 print the switch part number `5467-12693-66`; item 25 prints `5647-12693-66` (the two prefixes
  `5467` and `5647` are both printed as shown).
- The list prints `JET BALL DRAIN` for item 56 where the matrix prints `JETS BALL DRAIN`.
- Item 12 `BACKBOX BASKET` carries both an assembly (`A-21710`) and a switch part (`5647-12693-19`).
- The switch part number `20-10293` is printed on items 44, 47, 63 and 64 and `20-10448` on 45.
- Items 11-12, 14, 21-22, 24 and the flipper items F1-F4, F6, F8 have no balloon on the 2-43 drawing (see below).

drawn faintly in the centre of the playfield without a visible leader line.
