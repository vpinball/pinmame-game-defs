## Page 1

```
Service Bulletin: SB 99
DATE: May 14, 1997
GAME: NBA Fastbreak
SUBJECT: For a ball trap on the right ramp
If your NBA Fastbreak traps a ball at the plunger lane entrance to the right ramp, implement the
following change.

Note: If your game is missing the metal plate with the plastic pin as shown above, you may order the
Right Ramp Ball Hang-up kit #A-22014.
Editor's Note: Image text reads:
Note: ESNA nut replaces plastic pin and is mounted on the topside of the ramp.
1) Remove clear plastic pin and screw and discard.
2) Replace with 6-32 ESNA lock nut (p/n 4408-01118-00) and machine screw (p/n 4006-01113-04) as
shown.
```
