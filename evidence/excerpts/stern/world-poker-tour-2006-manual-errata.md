Ripley's matrix row 56 identifies the cabinet plumb-bob tilt and points to its SW56 note. That note identifies hanger bracket 535-5319-00 and contact wire 535-7563-01 in the cabinet. Batman's location page assigns the same bracket/contact-wire note to dedicated D17.

WPT page 7 repeats Ripley's SW56 note, although WPT page 6 assigns its pendulum tilt to dedicated D17 and describes SW56 as an optical pair above the left VUK, part 500-6775-00. The WPT assembly drawing on page 116 labels both sides of the SW56 optical beam on the left VUK tube; page 117 identifies that item as transceiver OPTO PCB 500-6775-00. These specific WPT drawings and the two tilt-note references resolve the footnote as stale template text, rather than an incompatible claim about WPT's installed SW56 construction.

The WPT page 7 backpanel view and page 116 heading put this tube on the backpanel. Its flattened VPX Trigger.sw56 point is a runtime proxy, so SW56 receives cabinet/service spatial exclusion rather than a playfield coordinate. The two PCBs form one optical beam, not two independently addressed sensors.
