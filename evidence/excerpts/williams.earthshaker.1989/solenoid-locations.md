## Location drawing callouts read at 300 dpi

Jet-bumper cluster, upper left of the playfield: callout 21 leads to the uppermost bumper, 19 to
the bumper to its lower right, and 17 to the leftmost bumper nearest the left wall. 03C leads to
the spinner area above the top bumper and 03A to the 3-bank drop targets; 04C leads to the round
flasher below the left bumper and 05A to the eject hole below the right bumper. Callout 9 sits at
the top of the drawing with a vertical leader down into the Institute building. Callout 22 has a
dashed leader, the drawing's convention for a part hidden under the playfield, running into the
left-center of the playfield. 13 is at the top right (top ball popper), 08C has two leaders to the
upper two round flashers along the right ramp, 07C and 06C lead to the next two, 01C to the
captive-ball flasher, 06A to the bottom ball popper's lift tube, 14 has two leaders to the two
center arrow inserts, 16 leads to the upper left, 05C to the lower left, 18 and 20 to the
slingshots, 02A to the shooter-lane feeder, and 01A below the apron (outhole).
