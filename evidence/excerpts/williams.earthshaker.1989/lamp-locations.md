## Location drawing callouts read at 300 dpi

The drawing places the bracket callout "17-25" on the Institute building in the upper left of
the playfield. In the jet-bumper cluster callout 41 leads to the uppermost bumper, 43 to the
bumper to its lower right, and 42 to the leftmost bumper nearest the left wall. Callout 7 leads to
the spinner above the top bumper. On the right-hand callout column, 49 (Left Road Sign) leads to
the sign at the entrance of the center ramp and 57 (Right Road Sign) to the sign at the entrance
of the right ramp. Lamps 58-64 have no callout on the drawing: they are the speaker-panel jackpot
values.
