# Earthshaker — Lamp-Matrix Table and wiring diagram (printed pages 67-68)

Source: `earthshaker.pdf`. The Lamp-Matrix Table is printed at the bottom of PDF page 71 (printed
"EARTHSHAKER 67"); the lamp-matrix wiring diagram is at the top of PDF page 72 (printed
"EARTHSHAKER 68", above the switch-matrix table). Read from 200 dpi renders of the native 300 ppi
scan. Every cell is transcribed; a circled digit in a cell is the table's "Zone" marker, written
"(zone n)".

Legend printed under the table: "TL = Top Left   TR = Top Right   BL = Bottom Left   BR = Bottom
Right   (7) = "Zone"   SP = Speaker Panel   ** = 2 Lamps". No cell of this scan carries a visible
"**" marker.

The circled zone-8 marker is printed in the Left Outlane cell (16), not in the L Return Lane cell
(15). The lamps list (printed 69) puts "Zone 8 (Left Return Lane)" on 15, switch 18 is the left
return lane (zone 8), and the game rules (printed 8) make zones 7 and 8 the right and left return
lanes, so the marker's cell is a printing slip.
