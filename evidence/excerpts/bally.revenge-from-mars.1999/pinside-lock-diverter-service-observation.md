# Revenge From Mars lock-diverter service observation

Source: Pinside technical thread *RFM diverter closes but doesn't open fully*, opened at `https://pinside.com/pinball/forum/topic/rfm-diverter-closes-but-doesnt-open-fully` on 2026-08-15.

The original poster reports direct observation of a physical Revenge from Mars machine: the diverter is pulled fully to its open position by the factory Power solenoid test, but a fault prevents it from returning fully to the closed position. The same post says the diverter should normally rest fully at either end rather than midway, and notes that manually moving the plunger does not make it return fully closed. Follow-up replies identify the diverter rod or return spring as the likely obstruction or failure.

This source establishes only the physical direction of the two controller states: the power winding opens the diverter and the springs close it after release. The official assembly drawing remains authoritative for the A-22993 parts and spring topology, and the pinned PinMAME service trace remains authoritative for public outputs 51 and 52.
