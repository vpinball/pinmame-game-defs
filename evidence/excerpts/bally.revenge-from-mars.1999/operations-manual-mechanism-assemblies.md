# Revenge from Mars mechanism assemblies

Source: Bally/Williams *Revenge From Mars* Operations Manual, February 1999, model 50070.

The following facts were manually summarized from the assembly drawings and parts lists on PDF pages 52-68 (printed pages 2-12 through 2-28). The retained page images are the authoritative visual excerpts; this table uses the manual's exact assembly and component names where quoted and records only the controller-facing topology needed by a digital recreation.

| Assembly | Printed evidence | Recreation fact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `A-22429-4` Auto-Fire Assembly | Plunger, coil `AE1-23-800`, and return spring | The coil drives a linear plunger and the spring returns it. Together with the shooter-lane switch and the manual's start/launch description, this is the auto-launch mechanism. |
| `A-19963-4` Ball Trough Assembly | Ball Trough Welded Assembly `A-16809-2`, Ball Trough Front `01-11587`, Bell Armature Assembly `A-6306-2`, coil `AE1-26-1500`, Spring `10-135`, Trough IRED LED PCB Assembly `A-18617-1`, and Trough IRED Transistor PCB Assembly `A-18618-1` | Four ball-position beams run along the stock trough. A bell-armature eject driven by one coil serves the leading ball, and the spring returns the eject mechanism. |
| `A-23004-R-2` and `A-23001-L-2` Flipper Assemblies | Right and left brackets, `SW-1A-194` Switch Assemblies, blue Flipper Coils `FL1-11629`, Spring `10-364`, Flipper Crank Link Assemblies `A-23000`, and flipper bats/shafts `20-10110-5` | The two lower flippers each use public power/hold windings, a cabinet button, and an end-of-stroke switch. The manual says the EOS contact closes in the activated position and the moving assembly must operate freely. |
| `A-17811` Kicker Assembly | Coil Plunger `02-2364`, Kicker Mounting Bracket Assembly `A-17810`, Crank Assembly `A-12664`, Armature Link `03-8085`, left Coil & Bracket Assembly `A-22207-6` with coil `AE1-26-1200`, and Kicker Spring `10-128` | The left and right slingshots use the same spring-returned kicker topology. Their playfield switches request the corresponding public coils; the retained evidence does not identify those switches' construction. Exact rubber geometry and impulse strength remain table-owned. |
| `A-22989` Skip Ramp Assembly | Ramp Bracket `04-12615`, Skip Ramp Armature Assembly `04-12616.2`, yellow Flipper Coil `FL1-11753`, Sub-Mini Micro Switch `5647-12693-07`, and associated Skip Ramp Flap Assembly `A-22988` | One power/hold coil assembly moves the center ramp, the micro switch reports the raised position, and the flap is the moving ball-path surface. |
| `A-23156` Popper Assembly | Popper bracket, cup, armature-and-extension assembly, plunger spring, coil `AE1-25-1000`, opto LED assembly, and opto phototransistor assembly | An opto reports a ball in the cup. The coil drives the armature/cup eject and the plunger spring returns it. |
| `A-15211-1` 1 Bank Drop Target Assembly | Flush target, bracket/post assembly, coil `AE1-26-1200`, coil `SM1-26-600`, plunger/reset plate, actuator, reset spring, frame/eyelet assembly, and Sub Mini Micro Switch `5647-12693-31` | Separate down and up coils command the single target. The mechanical actuator/reset linkage moves between the two positions, and the switch reports the down position. |
| `A-20680-1` 3 Ball Popper Assembly | Three-ball frame, ball popper cup, coil `AE1-23-800`, armature-and-extension assembly, plunger spring, opto LED assembly, opto phototransistor assembly, and associated popper hood | The lock weldment has three physical ball positions. An opto senses a populated position, while one spring-returned coil/cup assembly ejects the leading locked ball. The stock game populates only the first opto; the separate myPinballs expansion excerpt documents the two retained sensor positions. |
| `A-22993` Diverter Mechanism Assembly | Diverter Mech Bracket `04-12619`, Flipper Coil `FL1-22241`, Master Spring `10-303`, Diverter Plunger Assembly `A-16636` containing Diverter Plunger `02-4793` and Gate Spring `10-389`, Drive Arm Assembly `04-10408`, and associated Diverter Paddle Assembly `A-22970` | A power/hold winding pair moves and holds the lock-diverter paddle. The master and gate springs define the unpowered return path; no position sensor is fitted. |
| `A-17796` Ball Gate Actuator Assembly | Ball Gate Coil Bracket `01-12348`, Frame & Eyelet `A-6892`, Coil Assembly `A-14406`, Ball Gate Armature Assembly `A-11146`, Spring `10-120`, and Extension Spring `10-194` | Separate public outputs drive the right and left gates through matching spring-returned actuator assemblies. The gate's authored collision surface defines the routed ball path. |
| `A-22977` Up/Down Post Assembly | Post bracket, plunger spring, coil-centering bracket, coil tubing, coil `AE1-26-1500`, and armature assembly | A single coil moves the jet-exit post and the plunger spring defines its unpowered return. The nearby Jet Exit opto is a ball-path sensor, not a post-position sensor. |
| `A-22992` Ball Catcher Assembly | Ball Catcher Main Bracket `01-15193.2`, Backstop mounting Bracket `01-15194`, Ball Catcher Backstop `03-10008`, Ball Catcher Shaft `02-5476`, Sub Mini Micro Switch `5647-12693-11`, Ball Catcher Spring `10-562.1`, and Ball Catcher Anti Ball Trap Bracket `01-15204.2` | A ball deflects the pivoting backstop and actuates public switch 75, which the switch matrix names Center Deflector Panel; the spring returns the backstop after the ball leaves. No controller-driven actuator is fitted. |
| `A-22960` Martian Mechanism Assembly | Martian Mechanism Main Bracket `01-15123`, Martian Plunger Assembly `04-12595.2`, Plunger Spring `10-135`, coil `AE1-26-1500`, Martian Retaining Bracket `01-15124`, and Martian Figurine `23-6768` | Two independent copies form the left and right Martian toys. Each public coil draws its plunger into the coil to raise the figurine; the plunger spring returns the figurine down when unpowered. No position sensor is fitted. |
| `A-22205-4`, `B-9414-6`, and `B-13123-2` Jet Bumper Assemblies | Coil Plunger `02-3406-1`, Jet Bumper Spring `10-326`, coil `AE1-26-1200`, Jet Bumper Leaf Switch Assembly `A-12030-3` containing Jet Bumper Switch and Diode Assembly `A-16443-1`, two `B-9414-6` bodies, and one `B-13123-2` body | The left, right, and bottom jet switches request three independent coils. Each coil pulls its plunger/linkage and the spring returns the bumper; playfield geometry supplies the ring and ball impulse. |

The factory solenoid diagnostic was also walked on `rfm_160` with a pinned release build of PinMAME. That trace proves transitions at the explicitly targeted public output addresses and records that output 5 did not transition when its Left Gate service entry was crossed; a separate production serve/launch trace does record public output 5, so the negative result is specific to the service-menu walk. Names and physical mappings remain grounded in the manual and pinned source rather than inferred from display text. The trace does not measure physical travel, force, timing, or launch vectors.
