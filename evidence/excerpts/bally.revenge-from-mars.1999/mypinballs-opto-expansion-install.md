# Revenge From Mars aftermarket opto-expansion installation

Source: *Revenge From Mars Opto Expansion Upgrade Install Instructions*, myPinballs Electronics, version 2.0. Visually reviewed from PDF pages 2, 3, 6, and 7. The two committed crops retain the installed trough and lock mechanism photographs; the wiring facts below were checked against the born-digital tables on pages 2 and 3.

## Six-ball trough

The page-2 table assigns receiver-board position J1-8 to Trough 5 / switch 53 (`WHT-ORG`) and J1-9 to Trough 6 / switch 54 (`WHT-YEL`). It also assigns transmitter-board positions J1-2 and J1-1 to the corresponding added emitters. The instructions say to install the two supplied transmitter/receiver pairs into the blank positions on the existing trough PCBs. Figure 6 on PDF page 6 shows the completed boards on the production trough.

The factory location drawing establishes the stock sequence at the common trough y-coordinate: Ball 1 through Ball 4 run from right to left at normalized x values `0.660`, `0.580`, `0.500`, and `0.420`. The two retrofit positions continue the same evenly spaced physical PCB sequence, so the definition records Trough Ball 5 at `(0.340, 0.970)` and Trough Ball 6 at `(0.260, 0.970)`. These are validated authoring projections combining the stock drawing with the installation photograph, not surveyed coordinates.

## Three-ball right lock

The page-3 table assigns Ball Lock 2 to switch 55 (`WHT-GRN`) and Ball Lock 3 to switch 56 (`WHT-BLU`). The instructions state that the production lock weldment retains the omitted mounting positions and that two standard WMS individual opto sets are installed there. Figure 7 on PDF page 7 shows the two populated retrofit optos and all three ball pockets on that same lock assembly.

The factory location drawing supplies the stock Right Lockup 1 assembly point at `(0.695, 0.205)`. Figure 7 establishes two additional consecutive ball positions toward the rear of that assembly. The definition therefore records Right Lockup 2 at `(0.695, 0.185)` and Right Lockup 3 at `(0.695, 0.165)`. The small uniform offsets are validated intra-assembly authoring projections, not a claim of measured hole spacing.
