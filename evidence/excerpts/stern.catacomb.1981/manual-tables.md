## Physical interpretation boundaries

The p18 note explicitly wires each drop-target contact in banks A-D in parallel with the corresponding backbox rollover contact. Backbox contacts have .05 capacitors; target contacts do not. Switches 1-3 lack diodes; all others have diodes. Asterisks identify the optional .05-capacitor symbol, not normally-closed construction. Contacts are drawn open; p7 says closing a switch displays its ID. The independent retained switch-test run displays every public matrix number 1-40 during its own closure.

P11 explicitly says the coin lockout coil was eliminated; generic p5 operating prose still mentions it. This machine-specific non-fitment agrees with p21 Q19 N/U and its unused public 18 sweep in the service test; the generic operating sentence does not establish another coil. No fitted coin-lockout device is claimed.

P9 mini-charts and p12 full tables preserve their own bit order above. For special awards use p12 columns 31,32 (extra ball OFF,ON; 100000 points ON,OFF), rather than reading p9 reversed 32,31 as the same order. No bit is silently swapped.
