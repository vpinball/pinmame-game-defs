# Physical photographs: scope and observations

Method: curator visual inspection of the retained originals below. Photographs corroborate physical topology and visible emitters. They do not establish hidden sockets, original replacement-bulb colors, factory dimensions or electrical connections concealed in a harness. Originals stay outside Git; no full photograph is redistributed here.

The photos support the manual's separate physical contacts on shared switch circuits. The underside shows lamp sockets behind the central inserts and separate GI hardware; a render collection cannot replace that physical inventory. The lower left-flipper/post region has visible illumination that the modern table's 21 GIBulbPrims members do not completely account for. The photographs do not show every concealed socket or trace every colored wire to its connector, so the GI inventory stays open. No photograph identifies the unnamed Q12/Q15 loads.
