# Catacomb memory-clear button locations

Source: Stern Electronics Catacomb manual, PDF4 / printed3, original SHA-256 `5219dc4d4791b0d4cff12ee0b8b48ca80c7afb9150bdb5a2b6c49a991f1202be`. Method: curator visual verification of the assembly drawing. Rights: NOASSERTION; Stern Electronics, Inc.; original retained locally.

The upper `S-33 (MEMORY CLEAR)` callout points to the MPU assembly on the back-rack. A second `S-33 (MEMORY CLEAR)` callout points to a button inside the front door, beside the separately labelled SELF TEST control. These are two physical locations. The names alone do not establish the same electrical route: the [MPU circuit](mpu-nmi.md) and [door circuit](door-service.md) identify their separate paths.

The crop preserves both callouts at the scan's native resolution. It supplies location evidence, not connector assignments or proof of a firmware memory-clearing outcome. Image derivation is recorded verbatim in the source record and can be reproduced with `tools/render_excerpt_image.py`.
