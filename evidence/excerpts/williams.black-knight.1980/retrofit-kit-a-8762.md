# Williams A-8762 Retrofit Kit, BLACK KNIGHT Playfield

Source: `bkmicroswitchkit.pdf` (IPDB 310, listed as "A-8762 Microswitch Retrofit Kit"), three pages:
two typed instruction pages and one page of mounting templates. Transcribed by hand from a native
246 dpi render. Only the first page's heading, general statement and kit complement, and the two
switch-identification steps, are transcribed; the remaining steps are mounting and soldering
instructions that assert nothing further about the machine.

Heading, verbatim: `A-8762 Retrofit Kit` / `BLACK KNIGHT Playfield`.

## GENERAL (verbatim)

```
This kit replaces locking mechanisms and ball ramp switches with microswitches.  The
microswitches should provide reliable, adjustment-free operation.
```

## The switches it replaces (verbatim)

Under `Upper Playfield (Refer to Template 1)`, step 2:

```
Locate the bottom lock-up switch (two Green-Blue, and two White-Brown wires are
connected to it).  Disconnect the four wires and remove the switch from the
playfield.
```

Step 3:

```
On the bracket, disconnect Yellow jumper, two Green-Blue, and two
White-Orange wires from one switch; disconnect two Green-Blue and White-Red
wires from the other switch.
```

Under `Lower Playfield (Refer to Template 2)`, step 2:

```
Locate the right ball ramp switch (one Yellow jumper wire, and two White-Brown
wires are connected to it).  Disconnect the three wires and remove the switch
from the playfield.
```

## How the wire colours tie the kit to the switch matrix

GRN-BLU is switch-matrix column 6 and WHT-BRN, WHT-RED and WHT-ORN are rows 1, 2 and 3
(`switch-matrix.md`), so the "bottom lock-up switch" on GRN-BLU/WHT-BRN is switch 41 and the two
bracket switches on GRN-BLU/WHT-ORN and GRN-BLU/WHT-RED are 43 and 42. The right ball ramp switch on
WHT-BRN with the column-3 (GRN-ORN) jumper is switch 17. The kit is a field retrofit: the lock-up
switches 41-43 sit on the upper playfield and the ball-ramp switches 17-19 on the lower playfield,
and after the kit all of them are microswitches.
