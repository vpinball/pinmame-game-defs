# Spider-Man Vault Edition trough opto LED1 test

Source: SpiderMan_VE_web.pdf, PDF page 65, OPTO TROUBLESHOOTING and TROUGh DUAL OPTO BOARDS ALIGNMENT/TEST FOR LED1.

The troubleshooting page defines the electrical states: an open opto (light falling on the LED) is switch open, while a blocked opto is switch closed. It also shows the LED1 beam test with the trough plunger in service position and identifies the receiver-board red-ring indication. This documents the semantic behavior of the opto-backed trough switches.
