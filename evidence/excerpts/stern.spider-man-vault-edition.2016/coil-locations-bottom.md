# Spider-Man Vault Edition lower coil locations

Source: SpiderMan_VE_web.pdf, PDF page 19, section 1.14 COILS & FLASH LAMP LOCATIONS.

The lower portion of the drawing is the underside view of the playfield. It places the trough, eject, flipper, ramp, target and other coil/flash-lamp assemblies beneath the playfield, with their numbered output references. The page distinguishes these underside devices from the upper-playfield and backbox devices shown in the companion drawing.
