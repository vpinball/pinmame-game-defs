# Spider-Man Vault Edition detailed coil chart

Source: SpiderMan_VE_web.pdf, PDF page 52, COILS DETAILED CHART TABLE.

The table lists Q1 through Q35 and records the coil or flasher name, connector/pin information, voltage and driver details. It identifies the game outputs as individual driver-board circuits rather than treating every address as an interchangeable playfield device. The chart is the electrical cross-check for the numbered coil and flasher locations on manual page 19.
