# Spider-Man Vault Edition trough opto LED2 alignment

Source: SpiderMan_VE_web.pdf, PDF page 66, TROUGH DUAL OPTO BOARDS ALIGNMENT/TEST FOR LED2.

The alignment drawing shows the LED2 light path through the trough and contrasts the correct and incorrect LED position. The page is retained to document the physical alignment dependency of the second trough opto channel and the service test used to confirm it.
