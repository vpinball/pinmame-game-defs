# Spider-Man Vault Edition trough opto boards

Source: SpiderMan_VE_web.pdf, PDF page 64, THROUGH UP-KICKER DUAL OPTO PCBS.

The page documents the dual opto transmitter/receiver boards used by the through up-kicker assembly, including the board views, connector/pin references and the LED transmitter/receiver arrangement. It is retained as mechanism evidence for the trough sensors rather than as a replacement for the numbered switch-location drawing.
