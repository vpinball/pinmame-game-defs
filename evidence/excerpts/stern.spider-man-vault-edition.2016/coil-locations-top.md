# Spider-Man Vault Edition upper coil locations

Source: SpiderMan_VE_web.pdf, PDF page 19, section 1.14 COILS & FLASH LAMP LOCATIONS.

The upper/right portion of the drawing identifies the upper-playfield and backbox coil and flasher locations. Numbered markers include the major toy, ramp, target, kicker and backbox assemblies. This companion view is used with the underside drawing when assigning output coordinates to the physical playfield.
