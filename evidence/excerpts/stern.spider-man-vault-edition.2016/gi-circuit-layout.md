# Spider-Man Vault Edition GI circuit layout

Source: SpiderMan_VE_web.pdf, PDF page 55, PLAYFIELD WIRING.

The underside drawing shows the four color-coded GI circuits across the playfield and the separately captioned rear-panel view. The rear-panel drawing explicitly marks ten GI bulbs (X10), while the playfield drawing shows the bulb markers distributed over the underside. The machine definition uses these physical markers for the normalized GI positions and keeps the rear-panel group at the backbox edge.
