# Spider-Man Vault Edition switch locations

Source: SpiderMan_VE_web.pdf, PDF page 15, section 1.12 SWITCH LOCATIONS.

The playfield drawing numbers the physical switches, including the trough, shooter lane, ramps, loops, targets, Sandman, Doc Ock, Green Goblin and cabinet/service switch areas. The drawing marks switch locations on the playfield and separates the dedicated switch-bank areas from the ordinary switch-matrix positions. This is the manual location reference for the normalized switch placements in the machine definition.
