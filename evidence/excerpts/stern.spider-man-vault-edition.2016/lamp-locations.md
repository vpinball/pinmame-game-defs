# Spider-Man Vault Edition lamp locations

Source: SpiderMan_VE_web.pdf, PDF page 17, section 1.13 LAMP LOCATIONS.

The drawing labels the playfield lamp positions 3 through 65 and the upper/back-panel positions 66 through 71. The legend distinguishes lamps mounted above and below the playfield and identifies the rear-panel group. These numbered markers are the manual evidence for the lamp map and the separate cabinet/back-panel projection used by the machine definition.
