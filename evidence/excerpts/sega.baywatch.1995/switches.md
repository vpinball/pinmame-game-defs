## All 64 matrix cells and all parts-list rows

The number is printed in each matrix cell. Column = `(number-1)//8+1`, row = `(number-1)%8+1`. Marker `*` belongs to the parts list and means “Location - In or On Cabinet”; `[blank]` is a genuinely unmarked row. The dagger in the parts heading says to specify Game N° (33) and decal description if applicable. Switch 30 is shaded and says “Not Shown” in the location list; that shading is not an opto legend. The location drawing puts 45 beneath 49 on the left rear orbit, contradicting its `*` cabinet marker; the device is on the playfield. The same drawing omits 30 explicitly. The generic switch diagram on PDF 38 shows GRN drive at N.O., WHT return through the diode to COM.; N.C. is labelled separately.

PDF 111, printed 107, Trough Up-Kicker OPTO Theory of Operation: illuminated transmitter/receiver leaves Q1 and Q2 off, acting as an OPEN SWITCH. Blocking the light turns Q1 and Q2 on, acting as a CLOSED SWITCH. Receiver 520-5125-00 has row/return WHT/XXX at pin 1 and column/drive GRN/XXX at pin 2; transmitter is 520-5124-00. Thus switch 15's matrix contact rests open, despite being optical.
