## Disagreements preserved for reconciliation

PDF 43's mini-table reads 1R “Flashes: Styrene X2, Ins. X2”. Its drawing places two 1R lamps on the rear panel and two on the backbox insert. PDF 44 explicitly separates X0 P/F, Insert X2, X2 B/P. PDF 45's circuit label instead says “1 DROP TARGET / 1 BACK PANEL / 2 INSERT”. Total four is consistent; the allocation of the two non-backbox bulbs differs. The exact retained table and physical photographs show the pair on the rear panel; no drop-target flasher placement is inferred from the schematic text.

PDF 45 labels circuit 3L UPPER BALL LAUNCH 24-940 and circuit 4L UPPER BALL LAUNCH 22-800. PDF 44 distinguishes AUTO BALL LAUNCH 23-800 and UPPER BALL LAUNCH 24-940. The assembly drawings on PDF 70 show Auto Ball Launch 500-5969-00-33 with a 23-800 coil, and Upper Ball Launch (Kick Big) 500-5862-06-33 with a 24-940 coil. Follow those assembly identities and retain the schematic's text as a misprint.

PDF 44's 6L description says 50V but its Power Description is 32v L and its power wire BRN. The wiring schematic and the Super VUK assembly establish the device and part; the structured record preserves the game's table power branch and discloses the conflicting voltage wording.
