# Retained physical photographs

Primary curator independently viewed IPDB photographs 51946 and 51949 by Mark Steinman, plus Pinside gallery image keys 35822 and 35797. The exact direct URLs, acquisition times, attribution and SHA-256 values are in `photo-receipts.md` beside this excerpt. The photos were acquired through the verified Baywatch machine pages (IPDB 2848; Pinside 550). All images remain external under `review-artifacts/sega.baywatch.1995/photos/`; no redistribution permission is asserted.

IPDB 51946 shows the full upper area: three front-facing tower indicator lenses, two red rear-panel flash domes, a bare flash-bulb socket beside the left elevated ramp, and the elevated Ironman and Tourist Season signs. The white bare socket is distinct from the surrounding clear plastic and reflections. IPDB 51949 is a close view of the three signs: Ironman with a yellow bulb cover, Tourist Season red, and Search and Rescue red; it also shows a separate raised red flash dome beside the center ramp. These views corroborate physical construction and layout, never controller addresses on their own.

Pinside 35822 shows three separate tower front indicator lenses and a red beacon on the front-right corner. The tower parts list assigns its three indicator sockets; the photograph alone does not assign the beacon to a runtime circuit. Pinside 35797 is taken from the reverse direction and clearly shows the miniature Shark Fin bat, its separate wire-form rollover lane and nearby stand-up target. Its perspective is not used as raw normalized coordinates.

No photograph read here is a complete backbox/G.I. socket survey. The views cannot establish the full wiring of every illumination branch or supply the retained table's missing flasher socket objects.
