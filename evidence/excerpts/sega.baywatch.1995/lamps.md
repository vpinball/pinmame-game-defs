The drawing labels 25 twice, one at each outlane. Items 7 (Ball Launch Button) and 8 (Extra Ball Buy-in/League Button) are on the front of the cabinet, left side; item 64 (Start Button) is on the front, right side. General illumination is explicitly not shown.

The bulb wiring diagram (PDF 91, printed 87) shows all matrix cells LB1-LB64 and the two playfield G.I. feeds at 4M/F6: pins 1 YEL, 2 WHT-YEL, 3 VIO, 4 WHT-VIO, going to the G.I. fuses. Its schematic bulb symbols do not count the physical sockets on those feeds. That schematic prints pin 6 for lamp drive 5, while the matrix heading above prints CN7-5 and the ROM's single-lamp screens also print CN7-P5 for Q67. Both readings are retained; the structured field follows the matrix and service screen. This is a connector-detail disagreement, not another physical lamp.
