The sample's drawing places one 1R callout over the drop-target bank and another on the rear panel; the production drawing instead puts both on the rear panel. This explains why the production coil schematic still carries older drop-target wording. Production photographs and the exact retained table agree with the production location drawing, so the older callout is not promoted to a current socket.

PDF 18 is the older complete coil/flipper wiring chart; it has different launch/kick power-stage cells from the production chart. Those cells are not copied into the curated production electrical data. A physical early/sample build would need its own reviewed wiring overlay; no pinned driver identifies such a hardware edition. The sample's final PDF page 19 is a generic colour-reference chart containing other games' labels, so it is not Baywatch fitment evidence.
