Notes:

- There is no column-8 (81-88) row on this page at all: the switch matrix's eighth column is entirely unpopulated (confirmed independently by the Switch Matrix page, evidence/excerpts/williams.congo.1995/switch-matrix.md).
- Item 52 prints "Com" on this page (three letters, no "r"); the Lamp Locations page (evidence/excerpts/williams.congo.1995/lamp-locations.md) independently transcribes the *lamp*-matrix address 72 sharing the same physical feature as "Corn" — a genuine manual self-disagreement, resolved in the curated definition in favor of "COM" because three of four printed sources (this page, the Switch Matrix page, and the Lamp Matrix page) agree, and because items 51/52 ("Travi"/"Com") spell "TRAVICOM", the name of Karen Ross's company in the 1995 Congo film.
- Items 31-35 (trough) and 41-43 (volcano lock) share one opto-pair part number (A-18617-1/A-18618-1 and A-16909/A-16909 respectively) with no separate switch part, matching the shaded "OPTO, TYPICALLY CLOSED" cells on the Switch Matrix page.
