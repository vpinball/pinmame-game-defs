Notes:

- Item 72 prints "Corn" on this page. The Lamp Matrix page (evidence/excerpts/williams.congo.1995/lamp-matrix.md, printed 2-38) and the Switch Matrix/Switch Locations pages for the co-located switch at address 52 (evidence/excerpts/williams.congo.1995/switch-matrix.md, switch-locations.md) all independently print "COM" / "Com" for the same physical feature. Three sources against one, plus items 51/52 ("Travi"/"Com") spelling "TRAVICOM" (Karen Ross's company in the 1995 Congo film), resolve this in favor of "COM"; "Corn" on this page is treated as the typographic slip.
- Bulb quantities: no item on this page carries a printed "(2)" or other multi-bulb marker; every fitted address is a single bulb.
