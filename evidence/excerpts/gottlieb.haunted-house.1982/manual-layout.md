# Haunted House: factory layout readings and projection policy

Source manual SHA-256 `168a1bc2e2198b03154835d45471eb5b3910f1619930c5c3fd48f9b86ad8c21f`. PDF 39/printed 35 has separate attic/cellar insets; PDF 40/printed 36 has the main board. Native page images are 2483×3496 pixels. Tight native-resolution crops accompany this excerpt; source pages remain external.

The independent reader read the drawing pixels without access to VPX coordinates. The primary curator then inspected the annotated overlays against the retained scans and physical photos. The original readings and meanings are retained in `tools/seeds/gottlieb/haunted-house-1982-layout.json`; corrections/exclusions are explicit. A label's centre is never silently promoted into a part or socket centre.

The main fit uses both bumper caps, all four main flipper pivots and the independently read hatched up-kicker assembly SW45. The original candidate 'main rear round opening' is ambiguous among neighbouring openings and is excluded. The SW45 control is a mechanism anchor, not an assertion about the contact's exact location. The attic fit uses its bumper, both broad flipper pivot ends, first/fourth drop faces and outer standing-target face. The cellar fit uses its bumper, two flipper pivots, lower hole and rear up-kicker. Attic flippers are outward-facing at rest; their broad pivot ends must not be swapped with their narrow tips.

The helper `haunted_house_layout.plan` uses the shared affine least-squares solver from `drawing_callouts.py` to map each inset independently into the same global player-view table coordinates. It records each source pixel, each table control, the affine coefficients, RMS, leave-one-out error, source image hash and convex-hull extrapolation flag. Main controls span more than half the full playfield height. The attic and cellar control spans do not; their local inset fits remain observed and are not used to claim the full-field validation gate. No frame corner is a control. Measurements are rounded to three decimals, reflecting the scan/read precision.

Only separately drawn empty lamp circles are measured: main L18/L19/L20 and L35–L39, plus the distinct circle above L46; attic L40/L41/L42. The companion JSON records all circled-L balloons and leaderless inscriptions as excluded, including L21, L44, cellar L23–L26/L45, and the other main labels. Those name lamps but do not identify a separate socket. Some accepted symbols fall outside the control convex hull; their explicit extrapolation flags keep them observed.

The primary curator separately read the main six-digit bonus-display rectangle centre at (910, 2390), approximate 25px, and retained the independent reader's SW67 outhole-bar read (907, 2757). The display is a located physical display supported by the lightbox schematic's explicit MAIN PLAYFIELD region. The outhole sensor and coil effect are projected onto their own drain bar; that bar is beyond the flipper controls and is explicitly extrapolated. Neither uses the script's hidden Kicker1 surrogate. The trap-door effect uses a separately retained world-mesh mechanism projection, described in runtime-bindings.md, rather than claiming the winding itself was measured on this drawing.

These are observed symbol/mechanism projections, not observed photographs of bulb filaments or coil centres. They cannot by themselves validate socket count, emission height or every shared emitter. The source's L8 balloon between the flippers is also a mislabel against the physical Shoot Again legend and the two runtime scripts; that balloon is excluded. No source geometry is altered to satisfy sidedness.
