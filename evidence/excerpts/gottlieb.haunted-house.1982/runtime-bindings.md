Later VPW2.1.1 script: lines 658–703 handle auxiliary MagnaSave keys separately. SolLFlipper/SolRFlipper at 1019–1140 drive two main flippers per side. The older embedded script instead selects main or cellar in the red callback when lamp 17 changes; that simplification does not overwrite the factory's four-button wiring or the later script's separate controls.

The old script's SetLamp100 and 111 are local animation channels; PinMAME publishes only 0–63. In particular NFadeL100,L2 drives a table GI/insert proxy named L2, not public lamp 2 or a proven first-target socket. Desktop Backdrop_Init moves cellar insert-render objects; those are not fixed physical bulb coordinates. Kicker TrapDoor outside the right table bounds and Kicker1..6 trough helpers are excluded from physical placements.

Retain the exact script digests and extraction manifests from the definition's source records. Animation frame counts, kick forces, plunger-class choices, hidden transport geometry and helper timers are table-owned implementation details.

The later official VPW2.1 table was also retained, extracted and considered as supplementary geometry. Its bounds are the same 1116×2186. A direct inventory found 368 Light records with no show_bulb_mesh flag enabled. Its exported Parts meshes contain numerous welded components whose physical roles remain unclassified; the lightmap positions and unclassified fragments are not accepted as socket observations. Its VPX, extraction manifest and world-space OBJ each have independent pinned hashes in the evidence seed. The known-working 2.1.1 corpus script is a separate artifact from this 2.1 VPX and retains its own byte identity.

One explicitly identified animated mechanical mesh is used: `BM_pTrapDoor`. The 2.1 script drives its BP_pTrapDoor group from the lamp 16 callback and TrapDoorTimer (lines 2389–2408); its RotX moves between 0 and -30. The closed world-space VPU OBJ has 1805 vertices for that object and bounds x792.9112–944.87256, y640.31116–778.25684, z-12.087792–5.986874. Its plan-view bounds centre (868.89188, 709.284) is an observed effect projection onto its own plate/linkage assembly, not a winding centre and not the stored primitive pivot (905.4074, 739.5926). Four exported bumper-skirt bounds centres are checked against the independently positioned collision bumpers to verify the export's coordinate frame; exact bounds and residuals are retained. The manual's hinge/lever assembly and physical trap-door photo establish that this is the mechanism being actuated. This accepts a named physical mechanism model while keeping unclassified Parts fragments and glow lights out of socket inventory.
