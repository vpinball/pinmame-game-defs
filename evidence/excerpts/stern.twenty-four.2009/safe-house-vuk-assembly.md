# 24 Safe House VUK assembly

Source: `24Manual.pdf`, PDF page 39, printed drawing B23.

The `24-SAFE HOUSE VUK ASSEMBLY` drawing identifies the coil assembly, plunger/link assembly, spring, retaining brackets, and rubber bumper with their part numbers. It is mechanical construction evidence for the Safe House's moving front and plunger linkage, not evidence for PinMAME output numbering.
