# 24 suitcase bottom assembly

Source: `24Manual.pdf`, PDF page 40, printed drawing B24.

The `24 SUITCASE BOTTOM ASSEMBLY` exploded view identifies the suitcase frame, plunger, hinge, release hardware, three ball cable, and the three light-actuation microswitches. The drawing shows how the visible suitcase and its release hardware are constructed around the addressed motor assembly.
