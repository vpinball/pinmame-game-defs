The notes call for unlisted jumper routes to be disconnected and
describe an extra E12-E7 and E14-E15 provision for older memory
tables on the modified -17. The intervening page explains that
F.O. 597 involves board changes, including foil cuts and jumpers.
The -35 adds A14 addressing. Its bare PCB is P-2948-330, while
the -17 is P-2948-277. These are Bally service routes for the
specified boards and factory ROMs. The retained material here
does not document the factory jumpers on an untouched -17 or a
complete Quench EPROM jumper map.
