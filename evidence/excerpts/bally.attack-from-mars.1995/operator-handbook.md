# Attack From Mars operator-handbook excerpts

Source: Bally/Midway Operator's Handbook 16-10206, January 1996.

- PDF page 4 (printed page 2) prints the complete 8-by-8 lamp matrix and lamp-location parts list for addresses 11-58. It names the insert families, feature arrows, saucer-related lamps and the player/cabinet indicators.
- PDF page 5 (printed page 3) continues the lamp-location list through address 88 and retains the complete numbered playfield lamp drawing.
- PDF page 6 (printed page 4) prints the complete switch matrix, flipper switches F1-F8 and the first half of the switch-location parts list. It identifies the trough, shooter, lanes, ramps, loops, targets, saucer, Martian targets, bumpers and cabinet controls.
- PDF page 7 (printed page 5) continues the switch-location list and retains the complete numbered playfield switch drawing.
- PDF page 8 (printed page 6) prints the complete solenoid/flasher table, all four flipper winding pairs and the first half of the solenoid-location parts list, including driver transistors, connectors, wire colors, voltages and coil or flasher parts.
- PDF page 9 (printed page 7) continues the solenoid/flashlamp locations, retains the numbered playfield drawing, and prints the five general-illumination strings and four flipper circuits.
- PDF page 10 (printed page 8) is the upper-playfield parts list. It identifies the saucer, motor target, Martian targets, bank reset and ramp, gate, bumper, eject and kicker assemblies.
- PDF page 11 (printed page 9) is the matching upper-playfield parts drawing and locates those assemblies on the physical machine.
