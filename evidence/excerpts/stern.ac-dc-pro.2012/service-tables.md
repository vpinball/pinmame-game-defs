# AC/DC Pro service-table excerpts

Source: Stern `ACDC_Pro_web.pdf`, manual 500-55C0-01.

## PDF page 16 — switch matrix and dedicated switches

The page prints the complete 8-by-8 switch matrix (`#1` through `#64`), the dedicated-switch range (`D-1` through `D-32`), row and column wire colors, CPU/Sound-board connector pins, switch parts, mounting notes, and unused positions. The populated playfield rows include the four-ball trough, trough jam and shooter lane; both outlanes, return lanes and slingshots; three bumpers; ramp, loop, eject and standup contacts; cannon home and mark; and the cabinet FIRE button. Dedicated rows identify the coin-door and service contacts, left/right flipper buttons and EOS contacts, tilt contacts and optional transmitter/receiver positions.

## PDF page 17 — switch locations

The numbered playfield drawing locates the matrix contacts from the rear loops, top lanes and cannon through the ramp and eject paths to the lower lanes, slingshots, trough and shooter lane. The page also documents typical normally-open/normally-closed switch wiring and diode-on-terminal-strip wiring.

## PDF page 18 — lamp matrix

The page prints the complete controlled-lamp grid (`#1` through `#80`), its 18 VDC drive/return wiring, lamp construction and unused positions. Labels cover the A-C-D-C inserts, ramps and loops, T.N.T. and R-O-C-K families, album/song awards, bumper lamps, cabinet FIRE button, jukebox horns and the remaining feature inserts.

## PDF page 19 — lamp locations

The numbered playfield drawing locates the controlled inserts and spot lamps. The page also documents the LED, bayonet-lamp and diode-on-terminal-strip constructions used by the Pro playfield.

## PDF page 20 — coil chart

The detailed chart enumerates the Q1-Q32 driver outputs and optional Q33-Q40 bank with device label, coil or motor part, voltage, wire color and I/O Power Driver Board connection. It is the source for the release, launch, cannon, eject, gate, diverter, flipper, bumper, slingshot, flasher, knocker and optional shaker/ticket circuits recorded by the definition.

## PDF page 21 — coil locations

The numbered playfield drawing locates the Q-driven mechanisms and flashers, including the trough release and shooter, cannon, ejects, gates, diverter, three bumpers, two slingshots, two flippers and optional shaker/coin-meter assembly. The adjacent diagram identifies the 50 VDC Q-driver wiring convention.
