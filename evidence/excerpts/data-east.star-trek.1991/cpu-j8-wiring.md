# CPU jumper J8

Data East Star Trek English Manual with Schematics, PDF p. 2 (starred CPU
Jumper Table, before the printed contents) and p. 56 (printed p. 52, CPU Board
Logic Diagram, Sheet 2 of 4). Read against the rendered pages. The circuit
crop includes PIA 11B, PA7 pin 9, the grounded J8 link and the parallel
pull-up RA19-9 (4.7 kΩ to +5 V). An installed J8 grounds PA7. Removing J8 lets
the pull-up bring PA7 high.
