# Cabinet wiring, matrix contacts and the transporter F/X unit

Data East operations manual, IPDB PDF pp. 27, 43, 46, 49, 52–53 (printed pp. 23, 39, 42, 45, 48–49).

- Printed p. 42 shows open contacts for plumb tilt SW1, start SW3, coin SW4–SW6, slam tilt SW7, the STEP and FWD/REV switches, and the two cabinet flipper contacts. The forward/reverse switch makes in reverse. The coin door G.I. branch is separate from the controlled start-button lamp.
- Printed p. 48 shows the fitted matrix contacts as normally open in series with their diodes. The generic diagram also draws symbols in unused positions. Fitment comes from its switch list and the part-number chart. The p. 48 list calls 15 and 16 left and right cabinet flipper switches, as does the p. 25 parts table. The p. 24 switch matrix separately prints Left EOS and Right EOS. The cabinet drawing documents the physical contacts separately from the matrix. PinMAME copies the cabinet inputs into these positions.
- Printed p. 49 identifies 8, 15, 22, 29 and 36 as speaker or backbox lamps. Its lamp matrix names 36 as Backbox #5. The lamp location sheet also places a 36 callout on the playfield sketch and the retained table renders it there, but those readings do not establish another fitted socket. Printed p. 49 shows two playfield G.I. feed pairs through 4M/F6: pins 1/2 YEL and WHT-YEL, pins 3/4 VIO and WHT-VIO. The two bulbs drawn per pair are circuit symbols, with no socket inventory or coordinates.
- Printed p. 23 describes a single motor relay driven by CPU Q26. The cam pin moves the grid plate laterally across the fixed artwork. Switch 31 is TOP (crew visible) and 32 is BOT (crew gone). Set the top switch to close near the cam's top 15 degrees. Set the bottom switch to close half a motor revolution later, when the crew image disappears. The two switch closures do not overlap. The service test pulses the relay repeatedly while Start is held. A binding or sticking grid plate, switch overlap, or blurred fixed artwork alignment can prevent the intended visual effect.
- Printed p. 45 connects the GI harness to the power-supply and PPB boards. It distinguishes the playfield, backbox insert panel, and cabinet loads. It does not give the individual playfield socket positions.
- Printed p. 39 shows the Turboboost kicker 500-5406-00 with a pivot arm, spring and 22-600 coil 090-5023-00. Printed p. 29 instead calls the coil 22-800. The assembly parts drawing provides the service part number. The same p. 39 callout shows coil 090-5001-01 (AE-23-800) in the knocker assembly 500-5081-00 and names kickback assembly 500-5080-00 with the lugs on the side.
