# Left and right slingshot switch locations

The Data East switch list assigns two 180-5054-00 contacts to each slingshot input, switch 19 on the player’s left and 51 on the right. Both contacts on a side report the same matrix address.

The side view of the installed right slingshot in Pinside archive photograph 4351 shows two contact blade stacks against the rubber, one on either side of the kicker arm. Classic Playfield Reproductions’ bare-board photograph de-startrek04.jpg shows the matching pattern on both sides. On each white island, the two small bores nearest the diagonal rubber edge correspond to the switch blades. The other openings sit farther from the rubber.

The retained VPX maps the 1992 × 4295 image `images/Playfield.png` across the playfield bounds. Left, top is (0, 0). The four measurements are aperture centers on the playfield plane. The width and height normalize the two axes independently. The active blade extends above the board, so these coordinates describe its entry point. Comparing the artwork with the table’s lamps 1, 49 and 40 independently checks the orientation and alignment.
