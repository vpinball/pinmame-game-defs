# Whirlwind stock playfield: the two three-bank-island sockets

Pinside's Whirlwind Club, page 122, post 6085 by LWB250:
https://pinside.com/pinball/forum/topic/whirlwind-club-members-only/page/122#post-9282237.
The owner reports fitting the playfield sockets with #47 incandescent
lamps. The published photograph shows two separate glass bulbs in two
adjacent round openings between the lower jet bumpers and three-bank
drop-target slot. These are two physical sockets, not two views of one
bulb, a plastic support post, or a reflection.
