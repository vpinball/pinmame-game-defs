# Whirlwind — three left-curve bulbs and a gate opening

Physical photographs by Strangeways in “Williams Whirlwind Restoration”,
<https://aussiepinball.com.au/index.php?topic=7512.0>.
These show the original playfield before and during servicing; replacement
parts elsewhere in the restoration do not establish untouched factory wiring.

The same four wood mouths are approximately (36,241),(47,282),(100,307),
(165,305). Thus counting every round opening would add the gate as a bulb.
Conversely, an overlapping bracket does not exclude the third fitted bulb.

This establishes **three physical GI sites in this curve**. It does not
identify their relay branch or prove a complete playfield inventory.
Four socket-like backs in a different replacement-wood underside photograph
have not been individually registered to these openings; they cannot turn
this curve into a four-bulb circuit.
