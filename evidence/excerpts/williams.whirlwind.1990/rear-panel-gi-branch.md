Method: factual paraphrase by repository curator after reading the complete retained

repair; they are not a factory socket inventory.

- **41, jhanley:** tracing his own Whirlwind established that the light-bar
  GI is parallel with the rear playfield GI. A two-contact inline connector
  between head and cabinet joins the circuits; the wire colours agree on
  both sides even though their gauges differ.
- **44, Pmaino:** the repaired connection used purple and white/purple.
  The matching disconnected end was bundled away. Reconnection restored
  the panel GI.

The operations manual's power schematic independently identifies the
purple / white-purple playfield branch as the upper-GI relay controlled by
public solenoid 11. The factory D13356 drawing independently identifies
its five GI socket positions LMP6–LMP10. Together these sources support
their membership in output 11; the repair alone does not count them.

Posts 9/12/16 describe four upper playfield lights as owner observations.
They do not identify every aperture or demonstrate an exhaustive circuit
census. The thread also contains earlier incorrect voltage and relay-count
advice, subsequently corrected. Neither that advice nor the four-light
statement supplies a validated total or a missing socket position here.
