The small flashlamp legend names p as playfield; the other two abbreviated
location words are poorly reproduced in this scan and are not reconstructed
from the operations manual's different legend. Literal `1p,1g` and `1p,1b`
cells above are retained rather than silently corrected.

Complete numbered notes, paraphrased: (1) Listed non-flipper wires are coil
grounds at the diode's unbanded terminal. Flipper orange/violet and
orange/grey run from CPU to flipper switch. (2) Braced flipper connections
run from that switch to its coil. (3) Solenoid 12 off selects A circuits;
on selects C circuits. Bracketed colours name the corresponding A/C wires
from the auxiliary power-driver J1 connection. (4) Relay board 4a is C11998-1;
4b is C11902-1. (5) The A/C relay is on D12247 in the backbox.

## Reconciliation used by the definition

Rows 23/25/26 give **2/1/2 rear flashlamps**. This agrees with the complete
D13356 five-flasher elevation and the physical LMP1–LMP10 board pattern.
The operations manual's `3bp` left-flasher entry is therefore a quantity
erratum. The handbook's `p` does not put the rear panel behind the backglass:
the machine's construction/location drawing places D13356 on the playfield.

Other handbook differences are preserved as source readings, not adopted
indiscriminately. The operations manual's assembly, location and wiring
pages establish the later coil parts, all thirteen C-bank playfield bulbs,
and the motor/relay topology. In particular this early table's 04C/07C
location suffixes do not override those physical construction facts.
