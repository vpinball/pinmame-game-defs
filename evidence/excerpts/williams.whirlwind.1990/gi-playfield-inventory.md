# Whirlwind playfield GI: physical inventory and coordinate reconciliation

Method: the repository curator visually reviewed the retained native photographs, factory
power wiring and three-bank drawing, exact Bigus bulb objects, and the 4K
state-specific socket depictions. The two tables share no geometry authority
with a physical photograph: photographs establish fitted hardware; the
factory schematic establishes circuit routing; table bulb meshes supply
coordinates. A light collection or baked glow supplies no physical quantity.

## Physical observations

The stock playfield contains 21 fitted GI bayonets: two deep left, three
along the left white curve, three outside the rear-right guide, two in the
inner-right white island, two beside the three-bank, one left-points, two
right-points, one per sling, and two per return-lane island. Matrix wedge
bulbs, leaf-switch legs, bumper spoons and the switch-wire passage beside
left points are excluded. The separate five rear-panel GI lamps and backbox
strings are counted separately; these are not 26 playfield bayonets.

The first two photographs below show the original hardware before removal.
In `under-upper.jpg`, native box (170,270,1080,910) contains three rear-right
bayonet bodies. Solid violet and white/violet feed the inboard holder;
two local yellow links reach the other two holders. One contact is detached
during teardown, with the cable end visible. Box (1090,345,1470,1000) shows
two inner-right bodies, their own violet/white-violet feed and short yellow
links. Box (2870,250,3610,1210) shows the two deep-left bodies and the three
curve bodies. The last short connection passes beneath the loom clamp.
`under-wide.jpg` supplies the opposite view, including actual violet feeds,
the left group and three curve bodies at box (115,800,980,1690). Yellow local
jumpers occur on violet-fed branches and cannot identify a circuit by colour
alone. The independent stock glass-bulb observations remain in the left-curve,
right-rear and three-bank excerpts.

The factory power schematic routes upper relay 11 on VIO/WHT-VIO and lower
relay 16 on YEL/WHT-YEL. The upper groups total ten playfield bulbs. The
4K upper-only/off states depict these exact socket globes, including all
three curve lamps; its lower-only state depicts the other eleven, including
both bank bulbs. A first-hand regional voltage comparison independently
places the bank and kickers together and the rear/left-lightning group on
the other branch (Pinside `proper-operation-of-williams-whirlwind-pinball`,
post 1, pinball_player). This is corroboration of regional routing, not a
healthy-voltage specification or a count inferred from prose. The physical
feeds, factory routing, complete stock inventory and two-table reconciliation
jointly determine membership. No quantity comes from the renderer groups.

Relay 11 therefore supplies 15 bulbs: ten playfield bayonets plus five GI
bulbs LMP6-LMP10 on D13356. Relay 16 supplies 29: eleven playfield bayonets
plus eighteen controlled backbox lamps. The seventeen constant backbox lamps
and two coin-door lamps are outside both switched inventories. Six #455
autonomous blinkers are included in the eighteen, never added again.

## Exact positions

The committed `whirlwind-1990-gi-inventory.json` seed contains all raw object
centres, object SHA-256 values, independent stock-bore reads, eighteen non-GI
calibration features and the homography. Bigus bounds are 964x2162; 4K bounds
are 952x2164. Normalize each table by its own bounds, never interchange them.

All twenty shared sites have explicit Bigus `is_bulb_light=true` and
`show_bulb_mesh=true`. Their centres agree with independent 4K socket-mouth
reads within 0.019435 normalized Euclidean distance (limit 0.025). The bank pair
uses the separately measured factory coordinates, not the lone Light81.

Bigus script lines 38/41 bind relays 11/16 to SolUpperGI/SolLowerGI;
lines 100-119 set the actual UpperGI/LowerGI collection members to state 0
when enabled and state 1 otherwise. Complete member names and positions
are retained in its `collections.json` and seed. Light81's UpperGI membership
is a table defect: both physically fitted bank lamps are lower 16. Light66
duplicates the single fitted left-sling bulb; Light67 occupies an actual
switch-wire passage. Exclude both from the physical census.

The 4K script lines 662-786 selects playfield_render.image among texGIoff0,
texGIon0, texGIonupper0 and texGIonlower0. Face-specific OBJ v/vt indices
rectify these textures into model XY; this is not full-image normalization.
Dark socket annuli are read separately from elevated white globe highlights.
The lower-only texture's alpha-trimmed white rectangle is not a lamp state.
A separate world bulb mesh supplies the forward curve cross-check. The
baked depictions corroborate sites and state, and are not used as extra
emitters or as the published coordinate source.

The independent bare-wood check uses six jet axles, three disc openings,
the round eject bore, four loop slots and four return slots. These eighteen
controls exclude every GI aperture. Maximum control residual 6.762 VPU; all 21
withheld bores agree with 4K within 0.008534 normalized. Rear corners and
return sites outside that mechanism-control hull are explicitly extrapolated
checks and their photo-derived coordinates are never published. Exact
Bigus bulb centres supply those coordinates. Factory bank apertures are
inside their separate nine-control hull and independently match the wood.

Known-working original script runtime remains authoritative. Its empty
UpperGI, one LowerGI light and table overlay simplify the physical inventory.
No change to polarity follows from renderer intensity or beam conditions.
