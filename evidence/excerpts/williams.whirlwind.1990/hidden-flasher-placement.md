# Hidden Million and Million Plus flashlamp placements

Method: manual physical-image review and reproduced two-view pinhole projection. Transcribed/reviewed by repository curator, 2026-11-07. This is factual derived evidence, not a reproduction of whole photographs. Native sources remain external.

Factory operations printed 72/73 names two bulbs on each 04C/08C circuit: one raised ramp/Million pair on 04C (public 28), one Million Plus/Compass pair on 08C (public 32). The insert-feature leader alone does not locate its bulb. The retained original f128/f132 effects and the 4K insert helpers are not physical socket geometry.

The complete raw control reads, bare-photo-to-world homography, glass reads, brackets, immutable reproduction inputs and their hashes are in `tools/seeds/williams/whirlwind-1990-hidden-bulbs.json`. Both targets are inside the convex hull of their non-bulb controls. Bounds 952x2164 are the retained 4K table; normalized coordinates share left 0/right 1,rear 0/front 1 convention with the original 964x2162 table. Depth only removes perspective in XY; no canonical Z or exact filament is claimed.

Million: two stock Sept 12 views show a large clear pear globe beside the long amber triangle, with a separate conventional diode/bayonet nearby. Nine non-bulb anchors initially register the right camera; 88 same-wood matches transfer the left. Because that first anchor cluster does not enclose the bulb, three additional forward openings were independently read on the left view: amber round window, red horizontal opening and long routed slot. A guessed T-nut is rejected (219–249 px residual), never included. Joint camera fits use the 9 right plus 3 left absolute anchors. Focal 2650–3375 native pixels brackets the actual Samsung SM-G930P 4.2 mm/26 mm-equiv EXIF; five glass-read pairs test partial occlusion. Every accepted physical body solution is inside the combined control hull. At 3010 px nominal center (0.5266,0.3310) rounds to the published point; right-control median 11.6 px, forward-left residuals 6.5/17.9/22.2 px and same-wood median 4.1 px. Raw residuals and all focal/read variants are retained. A few wide edge-read combinations imply glass inside wood and are rejected. No C4 cable color is asserted through the loom. Physical identity converges from factory named location, actual separate #906-type wedge globe at the amber opening and the accounted-for other flashlamps.

Million Plus: Pinside SimplePin posts 6093 and 6112 expose the same black wedge holder and pear globe over the red triangle before/after a lamp-loom stage; actual glass reads (686,1283)/(932,1264) after 270 degree rotation. Eight original non-bulb controls were supplemented by the independently read long routed slot and right outer round wood bore, enclosing the bulb in the full ten-control hull. These additions deliberately supersede the older unbounded fit. Independent 29/46/69 same-wood match sets locate the second camera. No usable focal EXIF: 1550–2600 pixel bracket gives x 0.6130–0.6187,y 0.3506–0.3526; first-anchor RMS 4.8–10.4 px, second-view median 2.0–8.9 px, individual glass image residuals about 2.1 px or less. The 1300 px solution puts glass inside wood and is rejected. The published (0.616,0.352) represents the bounded physically plausible bracket. The older unbounded camera/planar comparators differ by about 0.011 normalized and do not supply the canonical center. A nearby known 03C holder has identical construction; its held-out planar check lands within 0.005 normalized of the factory location. Stock Sept 12 photographs independently show the continuous black/grey striped 08C feed entering this holder. Their loom-covered neck is identity evidence, never a substitute glass center.

All photo targets are held out from wood fits. Square pixels, centered optical axes, focal brackets and no local radial distortion are explicit camera assumptions. The conservative 0.015 uncertainty also covers model registration and partial occlusion. Earlier collapsed SIFT fits, bad lock-anchor fit, uncorrected wood-plane glass projections and stock 43 neck-only reads are rejected as canonical coordinate sources; retained diagnostics keep their original candidate flags. Primary acceptance is the combined physical inspection and reproducible sensitivity analysis, not a changed diagnostic flag.
