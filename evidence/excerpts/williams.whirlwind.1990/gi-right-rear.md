This establishes three fitted bulbs in this rear-right region. An older
owner description of two lamps in each rear corner is not an exhaustive
inventory. This photograph alone does not identify a numbered GI branch or
prove the complete playfield quantity. The separate reviewed
`gi-playfield-inventory.md` establishes their upper 11 membership using
physical feeder/jumper wiring and regional voltage evidence. Three Bigus
rendering candidates alone cannot supply that wiring evidence.
