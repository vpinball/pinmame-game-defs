`PL` = playfield-mounted bulb, `BB` = back-box-mounted bulb, in the "Flashlamp Type" column's own
notation. Reading that column exhaustively: solenoid 17 drives one playfield #89 bulb only; 18 drives
one playfield #906 plus one backbox #906; 19 drives one playfield #89 plus one backbox #906; 20
drives one playfield #906 only; 21 drives two playfield #89 bulbs plus one playfield #906 plus one
backbox #906 (four bulbs total, the richest flasher on the machine); 22 drives two playfield #89
bulbs; 23 drives one playfield #89 plus one playfield #906 plus one backbox #906; 24 drives one
playfield #89 plus one backbox #906.

Solenoid 27 ("Revolving Lamp" here; "Mars Lamp" on the Solenoid/Flasher Locations page, same part
14-7971 — an internal manual naming inconsistency for one device, not two) is the only row on this
entire table with a blank Playfield connection and a populated Insert/Hood-column connection
(`J123-4`) instead — i.e. it is wired exclusively to the backbox, never to the playfield.
