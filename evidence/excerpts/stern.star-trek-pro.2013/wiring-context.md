# Star Trek Pro — board and wiring context excerpt

Source: `Star-Trek-Pro-Manual.pdf`, PDF page 116, crop box 0.05,0.06,0.95,0.92. The crop transcribes the I/O Power Driver PC Board, CPU/Sound PC Board, and 128-dot-matrix display board connector overview.

The I/O board drawing identifies AC voltages/fuses, high-current and low-current solenoid connectors, flash-lamp and lamp-column/row connectors, and the CPU-board harness. The CPU/Sound board drawing identifies J1 switch strobes, J2 dedicated switches, J3 dedicated switches, J6 switch returns, J12/J13 connectors, SW1 DIP positions, and the 128-by-32 dot-matrix display harness; this is board-level wiring context rather than an additional physical device inventory.
