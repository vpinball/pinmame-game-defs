# Star Trek Pro — shared switch matrix excerpt

Source: `Star-Trek-Pro-Manual.pdf`, PDF page 66, crop box 0.05,0.06,0.95,0.92. The crop transcribes the complete visible four-row switch matrix, dedicated switches D-1–D-24, and CPU/sound-board DIP positions shared with the Premium/LE manual layout.

The drive rows are GRN-BRN J1-P1, GRN-RED J1-P3, GRN-ORG J1-P4, and GRN-YEL J1-P5. The table identifies the four-ball trough 18–21, jam opto 22, shooter lane 23, center target, ramps, warp/loop and orbit switches, slings, pops, banks, dedicated coin/flipper/cabinet switches, and the DIP positions; the printed switch names and physical location labels are the Pro source of truth, while LE-only RGB and auxiliary-board channels are excluded from the Pro definition.
