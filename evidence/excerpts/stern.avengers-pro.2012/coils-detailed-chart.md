# Avengers Pro coil chart

Source: `Avengers-Pro-Manual.pdf`, PDF page 16, printed page 14.

The complete `COILS DETAILED CHART TABLE` lists Q1-Q32 and their physical functions, drive groups, wiring colors, control connectors, voltages, and coil or bulb types. It covers the trough kicker, auto launch, Hulk motors and eject, drop reset, gates, shaker option, pops, slings, flippers, Hulk arms, flashes, Loki lockup, Hulk magnet, Tesseract, and backpanel outputs.
