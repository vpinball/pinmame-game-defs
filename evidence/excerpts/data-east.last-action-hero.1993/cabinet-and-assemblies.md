# Last Action Hero (Data East 1993) - G.I. fuses, Cabinet Parts, Playfield Major Assemblies, Lamp Bulb Part Numbers and the cabinet diagnostic switches

Transcribed by hand from native-resolution (300 dpi) renders of printed pages 2, 34, 35, 38 and 46 (PDF 6, 38, 39, 42 and 50) of the IPDB scan `Data_East_1993_Last_Action_Hero_Manual.pdf`. The scan has no text layer, so every cell below was read from the render. Pages 34, 35 and 38 carry the footer `Parts Identification`; page 46 is the `Cabinet Wiring Diagram`.

## PPB board fuses: Detail A (printed page 2)

The drawing is captioned `DETAIL A` and shows part of the `PPB Board`. Fuse `F5` is labelled `5A S.B.` with an arrow from `50VDC`; fuse `F6` is drawn on its side and labelled `5A`. The four fuses F1-F4 stand in a row under the bracket `G.I. Fuses All 5A S.B.`, each with a leader to its label.

| Fuse | Rating as printed | Label |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F1 | All 5A S.B. | Playfield |
| F2 | All 5A S.B. | Backbox Door & Speaker Panel |
| F3 | All 5A S.B. | Playfield & Coin Door |
| F4 | All 5A S.B. | Backbox Door |
| F5 | 5A S.B. | 50VDC |
| F6 | 5A | (none) |

The rating `All 5A S.B.` is printed once for F1-F4 and repeated on each of their rows here. The drawing does not say which of the four G.I. branches the G.I. relay switches; the schematic book's General Illumination Lines (printed page 50) and Power Supply Board Schematic (printed page 59) do, and `schematic-book.md` transcribes them.

## Cabinet Wiring Diagram: diagnostic switches (printed page 46)

The page is printed turned a quarter-turn on the sheet. CPU connector `CN14` is bracketed `DIAGNOSTIC SWITCHES` under `FROM CPU BOARD`. Each of its four lines passes the interconnect `1M/F`; three reach the coin door, whose dashed box is entered at connector `1F/M4`, and one ends at the `MEMORY LOCK SWITCH`, drawn between the two connectors outside the coin door.

| CN14 pin | 1M/F pin | Wire | 1F/M4 pin | Drawn device |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 4 | BLK-RED | (none) | MEMORY LOCK SWITCH, whose other contact joins the WHT line |
| 2 | 3 | WHT | 10 | the common contact of both coin-door switches, and the MEMORY LOCK SWITCH |
| 3 | 1 | GRN | 11 | STEP |
| 4 | 2 | BLU | 12 | FOR / REV |

The coin door's two switches are drawn as two open contacts sharing one common; the second is labelled `FOR / REV` with `CLOSE ??????` printed under it, so the page does not say in which position of the latching FOR/REV switch its contact is closed. The memory lock switch is drawn open.
