## V. Sound

> The Sound Board installed in this game has been programmed for sound only.

## A. Control board switch adjustments

> NOTE: The following switch adjustments pertaining to **SYSTEM 80B** only. There are 32 switches on the control board which permit adjustment of the game parameters. These switches are contained in four packages of eight switches each, as shown below.

### Two-switch functions

| Switches | Printed function | Settings |
|---|---|---|
| 15, 16 | MAXIMUM CREDITS | OFF OFF 8; OFF ON 10; ON OFF 15; ON ON 20 |
| 23, 24 | HIGHEST GAME TO DATE AWARDS | OFF OFF NONE (NOT DISPLAYED); OFF ON NONE; ON OFF 2 REPLAY; ON ON 3 REPLAY |

Switches 1-5, 9-13 and 17-21 are the coin chute switches above. No switch number between 1 and 32 is left without a printed function. The page prints no sound-board switch settings.
