# Diode boards (A7, A17, A19)

This page carries component-location drawings and parts lists only. No diode designators are printed on A7 or A17; the connectors have printed pin-1/pin-10 end marks but no pin-to-diode table. Only A19 prints diode designators (D1-D10).

Drawing: flat board with a slotted bottom edge, two mounting holes (callout 2), eight-and-eight unpopulated hole pairs on the left and seven diodes (callout 3) populated at the right. No designators or connector pin numbers printed.

## SWITCHING DIODE BOARD (A19)

Drawing: three 6-position connectors with diodes D1-D10.

| Connector | Pins as printed | Diodes drawn beside it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A19P1 | pin "1" at the top, "6" at the bottom of the column | D1, D2, D3, D4 (D4 outlined by a hand-drawn box; a hand-drawn line runs from the pin 5 position around D3 to D4, and the pin 5 and pin 6 position leads are shaded) |
| A19P2 | "1" at the top, "6" at the bottom | D5, D6, D7, D8 |
| A19P3 | "1" at the top, "6" at the bottom | D10 (on its upper-middle pin), D9 (on its lower pin) |

The drawing joins each diode body to the connector pin position at its left end by drawing position only; the drawn leads are D1 and D2 to A19P1, D3 and D4 near pins 5/6 of A19P1, D5-D8 to A19P2 positions 1, 3, 4 and 5/6 (by position, see Uncertain readings), D9 and D10 to A19P3.
