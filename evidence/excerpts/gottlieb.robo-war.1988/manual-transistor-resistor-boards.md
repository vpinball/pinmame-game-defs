# Transistor Driver Boards (A16) and Resistor Board (A13)

The page carries component-location drawings and parts lists only. No schematic is printed on this page; the only connector information is the connector pin numbering drawn on the component-location views (A16P1 pins 10..1, A13P1 pins 7..1). No pin-to-component wiring is printed.

## Transistor Driver Board (A16), first version (upper drawing)

Component location: transistors Q3, Q2, Q1 (large metal-can, left to right) across the top; resistors along the lower middle, left to right R8, R6, R5, R3, R2 (R6 and R3 drawn as the larger body resistors); connector A16P1 at the bottom edge numbered "10" at the left end and "1" at the right end. Each transistor flange is drawn with two mounting screws; no other labels.
