## Callouts printed on the playboard drawing

Switch callouts: SW 30, SW 31, SW 33 (twice, one at each kicking rubber), SW 35, SW 36, SW 40, SW 41, SW 42 (twice: once below the single left drop target and once below the left target of the two-target center bank, beside SW 52), SW 44, SW 45, SW 46 (at the right flipper), SW 50, SW 51, SW 52, SW 53, SW 54, SW 55, SW 56 (at the lower right below the apron line), SW 57 (lower center, on the apron area), SW 60, SW 61, SW 62, SW 63, SW 64, SW 65, SW 66, SW 70, SW 71, SW 72, SW 73, SW 74, SW 75, SW 76. No SW 43 callout is printed; the single left drop target, which the switch list calls SW 43 "#1 Left Drop Target", carries the second SW 42 label.

Lamp callouts: L3, L5-L11, L15-L47 (every number in that range). Solenoid callouts: SOL.1, SOL.2 and SOL.5 (each with an asterisk, on three domes at the upper left), SOL.6 (with an asterisk, dome at the upper right), "#67 SOL.3" twice with asterisks (on the Stargate ramp), SOL.4 "#67" (right side) and SOL.7 "#67" (left side). Twenty triangular inserts carry "#1" to "#10", each number twice. Other printed text inside the frame: "CONSERVATIVE" and "LIBERAL" at the left outlane post, and "SEE PAGE 7" at the lower right post.
