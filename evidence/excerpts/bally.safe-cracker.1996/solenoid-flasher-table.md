# Safe Cracker — Solenoid/Flasher Table

Transcribed from `Bally_1996_Safe_Cracker_Manual.pdf`, PDF page 116, printed page `2-44`: the `SOLENOID/FLASHER TABLE`
with its main table (solenoids 01-28 and 35-40, including the `□L.P.D.C.` rows 37-40), the General Illumination block
(strings 01-05), the Flipper Circuits block (29-36), the legend line and the footnotes. The lower part of the same PDF
page (the first sixteen lines of the `SOLENOID/FLASHER LOCATIONS` parts list) is not transcribed here. The same table is
reprinted on PDF page 129 (printed page `3-5`) and was compared cell by cell. Read from the rendered page (300 dpi
scan), not from the OCR text.

Main table columns, in print order: `Sol. No.`, `Function`, `Solenoid Type`, `Voltage Connections` (sub-columns
`Playfield`, `Backbox`, `Cabinet`), `Drive Xister`, `Drive Connections` (sub-columns `Playfield`, `Backbox`,
`Cabinet`), `Drive Wire Color`, `Solenoid Part number Flashlamp Type` (sub-columns `Playfield`, `Backbox`). The
header prints `Xister` (not `Xistor`) and `Solenoid Part number` with a lower-case `n`. `(blank)` is a cell with
nothing printed. No cell of the table is printed with a grey halftone/shaded background. Every `Cabinet` cell of the
main table and of the General Illumination block is blank (`Voltage Cabinet` and `Drive Cabinet`).

Notes on this block:

- The table prints rows 01-28, then jumps to 35 and 36 (`AUTO PLUNGER`, `LOCK UP RELEASE`), then 37-40. No rows
  numbered 29-34 appear in the main table; numbers 29-36 are used by the Flipper Circuits block below, which also
  prints rows `35` and `36` (see the note under that block).
- No row of the main table prints `NOT USED`.
- In the `Solenoid Type` column, rows 37-40 print a small box-like glyph immediately before `L.P.D.C.`, transcribed
  here as `□`; the legend line below spells the abbreviation as `□L.P.D.C. = Low Power Device Controls`. The glyph
  could be a lower-case `o`/`a`-like symbol rather than `□`; see Reading uncertainties.
- Rows 37-40 print no Drive Xister (the cell is empty) and carry their only connections in the Backbox voltage
  (`J138-2`) and Backbox drive (`J110-1`, `J110-3`, `J110-4`, `J110-5`) columns. Their drive wire colours are
  `Brn-Wht`, `Org-Wht`, `Yel-Wht`, `Grn-Wht`.
- Rows with a Backbox voltage connection: 02 (`J135-2`), 04 (`J135-2`), 23 (`J134-5`), 24 (`J134-5`), 26 (`J140-2`),
  37-40 (`J138-2`). Rows with a Backbox drive connection: 02 (`J118-2`), 04 (`J118-5`), 23 (`J112-8`), 24 (`J112-9`),
  26 (`J109-2`), 37-40 (`J110-x`). In those rows the Playfield voltage and Playfield drive cells are blank. Row 26 is
  the only row whose Backbox drive connector (`J109-2`) is on the same connector number as the Playfield drive of
  its neighbours (`J109-1`, `J109-3`, `J109-4`).
- Backbox part cells: 02 and 04 print `04-10424`, 23 and 24 print `04-10440`, 26 prints `20-10307`, 37-40 print
  `A-20909`.
- Row 18 is the only row printing two lamp numbers (`#89, #906`); its function reads `JETS & BACK RIGHT (2)`.
- Function names are printed exactly as shown, in capitals; the `Solenoid Type` column prints `High Power` (01-08 and
  35), `Low Power` (09-16 and 36), `Flasher` (17-24), `Gen. Purpose` (25-28) and `□L.P.D.C.` (37-40).
- Wire colours are printed in the capitalisation `Vio-Brn`, `Blk-Org`, `Org-Wht`, etc. (initial capital, rest lower
  case), unlike the capitals-only function names.

Layout notes for this block:

- The `Function` cell (`Lower Right Flipper`, `Lower Left Flipper`, `Upper Right Flipper`, `Upper Left Flipper`) is
  printed on the line of the Hold row (30, 32, 34, 36) with the Power-row half blank; no rule separates the two
  halves in the `Function` column, so the text is one cell spanning the pair and is shown above on the Hold row only
  (the Power row is marked blank).
- The `Coil Part No.` and `Coil Color` cells for the pairs 29-30 (`FL-20867`, `WHITE`), 31-32 (`FL-20867`, `WHITE`) and
  33-34 (`FL-11753`, `YELLOW`) are each one merged cell spanning the pair, printed level with the Hold row (the
  text sits at the lower line of the pair). In the table above the value is repeated on the Power row for
  readability; it is printed once per pair. For the pair 35-36 the page prints `SEE` / `ABOVE` once on each of the two
  rows (two separate cells, not merged): `SEE` in `Coil Part No.` and `ABOVE` in `Coil Color` on both row 35 and row
  36.
- Wire colour cells: Power wires are printed left-aligned in the column (`Yel-Grn`), Hold wires right-aligned
  (`Org-Grn`).
- The voltage connection cells print the connector pin and the wire colour in parentheses (`J119-1 (Red-Grn)`) with a
  single space between them; the right edge of the cell border touches the closing parenthesis.
- The Flipper block prints no `Backbox` or `Cabinet` columns.
- Surprising overlap: the Flipper Circuits block prints rows `35` (`Upr. Lt. Power`, `Q81`, `J120-3`, `Yel-Gry`) and
  `36` (`Upr. Lt. Hold`, `Q83`, `J120-1`, `Org-Gry`), the same Sol. No., transistors, drive connectors and drive
  wire colours as the main-table rows `35 AUTO PLUNGER` (`Q81`, `J120-3`, `Yel-Gry`) and `36 LOCK UP RELEASE` (`Q83`,
  `J120-1`, `Org-Gry`). The main table prints the voltage as `J119-8,9` while the flipper block prints
  `J119-8 (Red-Gry)`. Both are reproduced as printed.

## Legend line and footnotes (verbatim)

```
J1xx = Power Driver Board; 24-6549=#44 bulb; 24-8704=#89 bulb; 24-8768=#555 bulb; 24-8802=#906 bulb
**These G.I. strings do not brighten and dim, they are always ON.
□L.P.D.C. = Low Power Device Controls
```

The `#89` bulb (`24-8704`) is used by row 18 (`#89, #906`). The footnote marker `**` is printed in front of the
function text of the General Illumination rows 02, 04 and 05 only. The first legend line prints `J1xx` with a lower-case
`x`; the `□` is the same glyph as in rows 37-40 of the main table.

## Comparison with the reprint (PDF page 129, printed page `3-5`)

The table is printed a second time at the top of PDF page 129, folio `3-5` (the page carries nothing else). It was
compared with the `2-44` reading in every block: solenoids 01-28 and 35-40 (all twelve columns, including the blank
cells and the empty Drive Xister cells of rows 37-40), the General Illumination block (rows 01-05), the Flipper
Circuits block (rows 29-36, including the merged coil cells and the `SEE` / `ABOVE` cells), the column headers, the
legend line and the two footnotes. No differences found. Both prints show `#89, #906` in row 18, `J119-8,9` in rows 35
and 36, `J140-2` in row 26 and the `**` marker on the same three G.I. rows. The reprint is the same table (one source),
so the agreement is not independent evidence.

## Reading uncertainties

- The glyph printed before `L.P.D.C.` in rows 37-40 and in the legend line is a small box-like symbol; it is
  transcribed `□` [?]. It is printed identically in both copies. The meaning of the abbreviation is given by the legend
  line as `Low Power Device Controls`.

No other uncertain readings.
