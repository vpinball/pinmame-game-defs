# Safe Cracker — Adjust Moving Target Assembly

Transcribed from `Bally_1996_Safe_Cracker_Manual.pdf`, PDF pages 68 and 69, printed folios `1-52` and `1-53`: the
`Adjust Moving Target Assembly` procedure, whole. Read from the rendered pages (120 and 150 dpi), not from OCR. The
step text is given as printed; bold italic warnings are marked as such. Each page carries one drawing of the
assembly; its printed labels are listed, followed by what the drawing shows that is not printed as a word.

## PDF page 68, printed 1-52

Heading (bold, underlined): `Adjust Moving Target Assembly`

1) `Manually move Target to the rear-most position.`

2) `Loosen the 2 screws on the Opto Adjustment Bracket. Adjust bracket so that the bottom edge of the Lower Interrupter "Finger" is slightly below the top of Opto 3.`
   Bold italic: `IMPORTANT: Opto path should not be broken.`

3) `Tighten the Opto Adjustment Bracket screws.`

Drawing labels, as printed: `REAR TARGET POSITION` (with an arrow to the target at the top of the pivot arm);
`OPTO ADJUSTMENT BRACKET`; `OPTO ADJ. BRKT. SCREWS (2)`; `LOWER INTERRUPTER "FINGER"`; `LOWER INTERRUPTER "FINGER"
IS SLIGHTLY BELOW OPTO 3` (beside a dashed line); `OPTO 3`.

Not printed as words: the target arm is drawn leaning towards the rear; the `OPTO 3` leader ends at the lowest of the parts on the bracket at the right, below
the interrupter fingers.

## PDF page 69, printed 1-53

4) `Dislodge the Coil Armature Bracket from the Pivot Arm teeth. This will allow the Target to snap to it's forward-most position.`

5) `Loosen the screw on the Arm Stop Bracket.`

6) `Adjust bracket so that the top edge of the Upper Interrupter "Finger" is even with the top edge of Opto 3.`
   Bold italic: `IMPORTANT: Opto path must be completely broken.`

7) `Tighten all screws. Target Assembly is now adjusted.`

Drawing labels, as printed: `FORWARD TARGET POSITION` (with an arrow to the target at the top of the pivot arm);
`ARM STOP BRACKET`; `ARM STOP SCREW`; `UPPER INTERRUPTER "FINGER" IS EVEN WITH TOP EDGE OF OPTO 3` (beside a dashed
line); `OPTO 3`; `PIVOT ARM`; `UPPER INTERRUPTER "FINGER"`; `COIL ARMATURE BRACKET`.

Not printed as words: the target arm is drawn upright (forward); the coil at the left with its armature bracket
reaching the toothed sector of the pivot arm; a spring from the pivot arm to the frame at the right.

Page 1-53's text spells `it's` for `its`, as printed.
