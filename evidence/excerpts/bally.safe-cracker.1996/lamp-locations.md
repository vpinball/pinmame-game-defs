# Safe Cracker — Lamp Locations

Transcribed from `Bally_1996_Safe_Cracker_Manual.pdf`. The Lamp Locations material occupies three PDF pages:

- PDF page 118, printed page `2-46`, lower part (below the Lamp Matrix): a two-column parts list for playfield lamp
  items 11-68 (no drawing on this page).
- PDF page 119, printed page `2-47`, titled `Lamp Locations`: a playfield plan-view drawing with a numbered balloon for
  every playfield lamp 11-88, and the parts list for items 71-88.
- PDF page 120, printed page `2-48`, titled `Lamp Locations`: a drawing captioned `Backbox Lamps` with balloons `L1`-`L48`
  and a large circle `131`, and the parts list for items L1-L48.

Header columns on all three lists, as printed: `Item No.` | `Bulb No.` | `Lamp Assy. No.` | `Description` (the
header words wrap onto two lines: `Item` / `No.`, `Bulb` / `No.`, `Lamp` / `Assy. No.`). There is no Socket column.
`---` is the literal printed dash string for a blank cell. Read from the rendered page (300 dpi scan), not from the OCR
text.

## Observations

- The playfield list (11-88) is split between PDF page 118 (items 11-68, no drawing) and PDF page 119 (the drawing and
  items 71-88). The drawing on page 119 carries a balloon for all 64 playfield items.
- Items 74-76 are `Bottom Left 3-Bank ...` on the list, whereas the lamp matrix (`lamp-matrix.md`) prints `BOTTOM L.`
  only for cell 74 and `BOTTOM R.` for cells 75 and 76.
- Items 81-83 (jets) use assembly `B-9414-3`; 84-85 use `04-10083`; 86-87 use `A-16041`; 88 uses `20-9663-16` with no
  bulb number.
- The L40 gap on the backbox drawing and the centre circle `131` are as printed.

## Reading uncertainties

None in the parts lists. In the drawings all balloon numerals were legible at 300 dpi.
