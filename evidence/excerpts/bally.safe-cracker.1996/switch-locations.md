Transcribed from `Bally_1996_Safe_Cracker_Manual.pdf`. The `SWITCH LOCATIONS` parts list is printed in a
two-column layout that starts at the bottom of PDF page 114 (printed page `2-42`: left list column F1-28, right
list column 31-46) and continues under the playfield drawing at the bottom of PDF page 115 (printed page `2-43`:
left list column 47-66, right list column 67-86). The playfield drawing with numbered balloons occupies the upper
part of PDF page 115. Read from the rendered page (300 dpi scan), not from the OCR text.

Header of each of the four list columns, as printed: `Item` / `No.` | `Switch Part` / `No.` | `Description`. The
page heading `SWITCH LOCATIONS` is printed above the two columns of page 114 (centred over the gutter); page 115
repeats the column headers without the heading. A vertical rule separates the two list columns of each page.
`---` is the literal printed dash string for a blank switch part number. A description row whose second line is
`(Trans.)` or `(score)` is the continuation line of the same item (its part number is on the second line); it is
joined to the item's row here with ` / `.

## Observations on the printed text

- Items 87 and 88 (matrix cells printed `NOT USED`) have no row in the list; the list ends at item 86.
- Items F7 and F8 are printed `Not Used` in the list, while the matrix (PDF 114, top) prints F7 `Upper Left Flipper
  EOS` and F8 `Upper Left Flipper Opto`, and the list prints F5 `Upper Right Flipper EOS` and F6 `Upper Right
  Flipper Cabinet` (matrix F5 `Upper Right Flipper EOS`, F6 `Upper Right Flipper Opto`). The Section 3 reprint of
  the matrix prints `(NOT USED)` under F5 and F7.
- The list calls F2, F4, F6 `... Flipper Cabinet` (with a leading `*`), where the matrix calls them `... Flipper Opto`.
- List items 51-55 name the five `ALARM` standups `(A)LARM` ... `ALAR(M)`, matching the primary matrix labels; the
  Section 3 reprint of the matrix prints `STANDUP 1` ... `STANDUP 5` instead.
- List items 56, 57, 58 are `Vari Target C`, `B`, `A` (as on the primary matrix; the reprint matrix has `A`, `B`, `C`).
- Item 43 prints `*Token Chute Jam`; items 81 and 82 print `*Left Token Level` and `*Right Token Level`.
- Item 53 prints switch part `A-20976-6`, while 51 and 52 print `A-18530-6` and 54 and 55 print `A-17799-6`.
- Item 18 prints `5647-12693-65`; items 11 and 12 and 77 print `5647-12693-26`; item 68 prints `5647-12693-15`.
- Items marked `*` (Not Shown) in the list: F2, F4, F6, 14, 21, 22, 24, 43, 81, 82. All ten have no balloon on the
  drawing; the `Not Used` items F7, F8, 23, 38 have none either (see below).
- No `†` mark appears in any item row; the `† Located Under Playfield` footnote is printed once, under the left
  column of page 114.
