Page footer: `2-42`.

No lamp-matrix position is printed `NOT USED` on this page: all sixty-four controlled-lamp
addresses carry a fitted bulb. Five addresses carry two bulbs each - 13, 28 and 68 through an
explicit `(2)` marker, and 55 and 71 through a second assembly row of a different bulb type.
Items 78, 86 and 87 have no lamp-assembly part number because the bulb and socket
(`24-8776`) are part of the jet-bumper body; item 88 is the bulb inside the illuminated start
button and shares its assembly part number `20-9663-16` with switch 13.
