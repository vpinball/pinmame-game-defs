Transcription notes on this page, recorded rather than silently corrected:

- Item 31 is printed `TROUGH ELECT`. Every other source for this address, including the
  printed switch matrix on 2-51 and the solenoid table's own `TROUGH EJECT` coil, reads
  *eject*; the printed text is preserved here as it appears.
- Item 57's assembly is printed `A-17795-6` where its two siblings 56 and 58 both read
  `A-17799-6`. Preserved verbatim.
- Optos on this page are identified by a paired `(LED)` / `(PHOTO TRANS)` part number in the
  assembly column together with a blank switch part number. That signature appears on exactly
  the fifteen items 31-38, 41, 42, 44, 45, 46, 63 and 64; `A-22026` (items 63/64) is the Motor
  2-Opto Board rather than an LED/phototransistor pair, and is identified as an opto by its
  own board page and by the printed switch matrix shading. Items with an assembly part number
  and a blank switch part number that are *not* optos (23, 56, 57, 58, 77, 81, 82, 83, 84, 85,
  74) are standup-target or opto-less assemblies whose switch is part of the assembly, so the
  blank switch cell alone is not an opto cue.
