## Drawing

The same schematic playfield plan as Figure 4, with solenoid numbers placed on or beside the
device they drive. Callouts read on the render:

| Callout | Where it is drawn |
| --- | --- |
| 06 | top right corner, beside the upper right eject hole |
| 05 | right side, beside the right eject hole below the right rubber pair |
| 04 | left rail, beside the left eject hole |
| 17 | inside the upper left jet bumper |
| 19 | inside the upper right jet bumper |
| 18 | inside the lower left jet bumper |
| 20 | inside the lower right jet bumper |
| 15 | twice: on the left slanted insert above the left kicker and on the right slanted insert above the right kicker |
| 22 | on the left kicker (slingshot) |
| 21 | on the right kicker (slingshot) |
| 07 | outside the playfield outline at the foot of the left outlane |
| 01 | below the apron outline, centre |
| 08 | below the apron outline, at the right beside the foot of the shooter lane |

Solenoids 02, 03, 09-13, 14 and 16 carry no callout on the drawing. 20 is drawn inside the lower
right jet bumper, which is how the chart's repeated `Bottom Left` at 20 is known to be an error.
