The p34 schematic prints `6 BALL ASSY LOCKOUT`, `BALL RELEASE`, `BALL LAUNCH`, `EJECT`,
`VUK`, `SUPER VUK`, `LEFT DROP TARGET`, `KNOCKER`, `TOP DROP TARGET`, `L/R COIL RELAY`,
`BACK PANEL DIVERTER`, `RING DIVERTER` and `SHAKER MOTOR` for the corresponding loads.
P33 assigns Q30's board as PPB, while p34 draws Q30 on the P/O CPU board.
This board-designator disagreement changes no public binding or physical device.

Reconciliation: p33's 8R `X1 P/F, Insert X2` is retained literally above.
P34 instead draws four bulbs and labels `(2) PLFD (UPPER RIGHT)`, `(2) INSERT`,
`(4) 89`. P44's complete socket inventory lists32 No.89 bulbs, matching eight
circuits of four; the ROM's 8R test also prints `2-INS 2-R/UPPER`. These other
regions settle the p33 count as a misprint: the definition uses four at8R,
two playfield and two backbox insert-panel bulbs. See flash-loads.md and its
native-resolution schematic crop. No socket coordinate follows from that count.
