# Skateball VPW 1.0 — supplemental lighting audit

VPW's 2024 table is based on a hauntfreaks modification of JP Salas's original, so it is related to the primary 1.0.4 evidence and is not an independent coordinate validation. The retained VPX is SHA-256 `7afc7026cfeb51b7346c8080dfc91a5d49e9ecb7ddd97a330b8139f0347b48fc`; its extracted script is `d2f021710d7260d26f825176574ae2b3d7968d00973a865eefd75c734dabb0ff`. The external extraction manifest is `5a9c466b3c89d14f3a0ca4bde4fcdefbb76ade1cfb05d03484ed1c5fde2018d3`, 2,507 files and 699,407,300 bytes. The extraction and original remain under `vpx-sources/bally/skateball-1980/vpw-1.0/`.

The 85 extracted Light objects all have `is_bulb_light` true and none has `show_bulb_mesh` true. The table also has 773 Primitive objects. In the script `vpmMapLights allLamps` maps members of `allLamps` by timer interval; `L25` and `L57` are members with intervals 25 and 57. The lighting arrays named `BL_L_L25` contain `LM_L_L25_Bumper2_Ring` and `LM_L_L25_Bumper2_Skirt`, and `BL_L_L57` contains `LM_L_L57_Bumper1_Ring`. These later-table lighting associations agree in side with the factory's right/left labels; those array names and generated lightmaps do not locate physical sockets.

The `BM_Parts` combined primitive and, for example, `LM_L_L25_Parts` both store x 476 and y 990.05804. Other `LM` primitives have different stored positions; these positions are not taken as socket locations. The world-space OBJ export (SHA-256 `7501ac70d1e31d88f385d94ede21d05bdf11a04a2141bfc9ee5a0542a643e57b`) was split into 965 connected `BM_Parts` components using `tools/curate_twilight_zone.py` `world_mesh_pieces`, welding vertices at 0.1 VPX units. The retained component inventory `vpw-bm-parts-components.json` has SHA-256 `22e1f79f39723a254c1a5d9d6b2954c0ae85ece1a5a21789c7ae9f1e1d278025`; the top-view map and isolated top-component profiles are retained beside it. The three largest components coincide in plan with top lane inserts, and their z extent is approximately 0.47–10.12 VPX units. Their flat profiles resemble inserts and do not establish distinct physical bulbs. This investigation did not establish a reliable socket identity and wiring map; no physical socket coordinates are inferred from the components.
