# AC/DC LUCI Premium bell and block assembly

Source: ACDC_Luci_web.pdf, PDF page 53, bell and block, plunger, and kicker assemblies.

The drawing identifies the Premium bell pivot block, carbon ball, bell clamp, bell opto bracket, and the adjacent plunger/kicker assemblies. It supports the captive-ball swinging bell mechanism and its opto sensing hardware rather than treating the bell as a playable trough ball.
