# AC/DC LUCI Premium tri-color LED map

Source: ACDC_Luci_web.pdf, PDF page 21, AC-DC Premium tri-color LED map.

The map places the tri-color LED assemblies and labels their driver-board connectors, including the face, bell-arrow, loop-arrow, and lane groups. It also identifies matrix 84 as the dot-matrix-display entry on the 520-5331-00 driver board.
