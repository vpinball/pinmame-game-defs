# Transcription: Krellan Kingpin slot machine drum and hardware notes

Source: `human-review.krellan.kingpin` -- Krellan's unofficial "Capcom Kingpin \"Manual\"" web page,
http://www.krellan.com/pinball/kingpin/ (retained copy SHA-256
`d1aee4120403c311cafaa0252aaecf02b2fc357f610741bdadca5a31b425230e`, fetched 2026-09-25). The author states
no printed factory manual exists and that the lamp positions were "personally verified ... using operator
mode on a real Kingpin machine". Cells are extracted verbatim from the retained HTML.

Hardware statements on the page (verbatim fragments):

- "Install 4 balls in Kingpin."
- "the playfield area near the left orbit is hinged at the rear" (gun-lock kickout; the ball is then "fired directly down at the player")
- "Kingpin contains one main toy, a spinning slot machine"; "they do not spin independently"
- "the motor does not stop cleanly aligned on a symbol" (the software rounds to the nearest row)
- "Kingpin uses unusual flasher bulbs: #67's and #906's"
- "I do not know the exact placement of many of these flashers"
- "The two known ROM versions are 1.4β and 1.5β" (as printed; the page does not say which one PinMAME's kpb105 is)
- "Left Ramp Down (active when lowered)" (switch 47)
