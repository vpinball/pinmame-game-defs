## Flash-lamp bulb count compared with the coil tables

Printed pages 36-38 give four `#89` bulbs to each of the eight right-set drives and four to drive 15 (`(1) PLFD (3) INSERT`): thirty-six `#89` flash lamps in all. By Note 3 of the coil table (the `X#` count is on the playfield and the rest of the four are in the insert) and the drawings, 27 are on the playfield or back panel (1R 4, 2R 4, 3R 2, 4R 2, 5R 4, 6R 4 on the back panel, 7R 2, 8R 4, 15 1) and nine are in the backbox insert (3R 2, 4R 2, 7R 2, 15 3), which is also the count of flash-lamp balloons on the `Backbox Flash Lamps` drawing. The bulb table above lists thirty-four `#89` bulbs. The two-bulb difference is not attributable to a drive from this manual.
