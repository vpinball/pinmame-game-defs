# Grand Lizard factory spatial measurements

Production manual PDF 49-50, printed 45-46. Native-pixel reads and affine
controls are committed in `tools/seeds/williams/grand-lizard-1986-spatial.json`.
The corresponding native-resolution-derived crops are attached to these
excerpts; the original manual remains external. A source reader transcribed the pixel
centers without deriving them from the table lamp positions; a curator reviewed the
rendered maps, source roles, corrected assignments and projection limits.

PDF 49 numbered circles/insert symbols project onto the underlying insert
lamp, rather than measuring a socket. Four flipper pivots independently align
the drawing to retained VPX coordinates; each page has its own affine fit.
Every point stays observed. Measurements outside the control hull extrapolate
the fit, and all are subject to approximately four native-pixel reading error.
The 57 mapped playfield lamp addresses are 7-64 excluding unused 51. The original
numbers 42-47 are physically mirrored: left 42/43/44 follow the measured
left lower/middle/upper symbols printed 45/46/47, right 45/46/47 follow the
right lower/middle/upper symbols printed 42/43/44. The separate ROM charge
depletion runs settle the circuit identities. Lamp 30's arrow is measured on
the manual because the table visual proxy is offset; 59 is partly obscured by
the ramp tongue and is correspondingly uncertain.

PDF 50 flasher leaders 6 touch two circular lamp symbols, 7 touches one clear
circle, and 12 touches one. These four symbol centers are measured. The other
7 leader and both 19 leaders run to outlines, so no bulb is placed from them.
Output 13 has a head/backbox circuit and two head bulbs on PDF 41's exploded
mounting, but no two individually locatable top-view sockets; it stays unplaced.
Symbol positions and leader counts never establish unprinted circuit totals.
