# Grand Lizard coin-lockout circuit and unresolved unit count

Primary source: Williams Grand Lizard Preliminary Manual, PDF 14, printed 12,
Cabinet Wiring Diagram. The accompanying crop retains the native scan pixels
for 7P4/7J4 pins 3/4, both coil/diode branches and the 7L16 COIN LOCKOUT label.

7J4 pin 3 feeds the common upper node of two parallel coil branches. Each
coil has a separate antiparallel diode; the left diode is labeled 7D10.
Both lower coil nodes return to 7J4 pin 4. The common load label is 7L16
COIN LOCKOUT. No mechanical enclosure or separate actuator identities are
drawn in this crop. It establishes two electrical windings, not the number
of complete physical lockout assemblies or independently acting units.

The production manual PDF 29, printed 25, lists output 16 as Coin-Lockout
Relay, Brn-Gry, CPU 1P12-9, cabinet 7P1-18/7P4-4, Q6, Coinco part 404603-2.
That table has no quantity column. Production PDF 31, printed 27, uses the
plural phrase "coin lockout relays" in its coin-chute test explanation but
does not give a numerical inventory. A single channel label or part-number
row therefore does not support quantity one; two winding symbols alone do
not settle whether quantity two means two physical units.

Output 16 remains fitted, with its factory relay kind, part and wiring.
Its physical unit quantity is deliberately unspecified. A complete coin-door
assembly drawing or an original, traced coin-door photograph is needed to
map these windings to physical actuators and settle the total.
