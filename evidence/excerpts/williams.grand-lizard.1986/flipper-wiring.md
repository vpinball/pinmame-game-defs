# Preliminary flipper power branch

Preliminary manual PDF 15, left half of printed 13 Power Wiring Diagram.
Native-render crop visually reviewed by a curator. It depicts upper-right,
lower-right and lower-left flipper coils and normally-open cabinet buttons.
The right button contacts are mechanically ganged; the left cabinet button
is separately drawn. Supply from CPU 1P19-1 is ORG-VIO and 1P19-2 is ORG-GRY.
Those button contacts establish open-rest cabinet construction. The drawing's
EOS symbols and omission of upper-left are inaccurate for final production;
see the final solenoid table/assembly and physical-wiring excerpt, not this
preliminary drawing, for the four-flipper inventory and EOS construction.
