# Owner repair observations: Grand Lizard

Source: Jeff-Z.com, Grand Lizard Repair & Maintenance Log, October 2016-February 2017, https://www.jeff-z.com/pinball/grandlizard/repair/repair.html. Retained originals: `review-artifacts/grand-lizard-1986/physical-photos/`; URLs, acquisition times and hashes: `jeff-z-downloads.jsonl`. Copyright remains with the owner; photographs are not redistributed.

Visual inspection of `jeff-z-shop-20.jpg` shows two flash bulbs and sockets on the head mounting bracket, flanking its central switch. This corroborates B-10823 (production manual PDF 41). It supplies neither global coordinates nor the backbox lamp count.

The log reports a disconnected left lower-flipper auxiliary contact and a right contact wired to matrix 48, agreeing with the factory and `FLIP_SWNO(0,48)`.

The owner describes series-paired flash lamps without A/C multiplexing, but does not assign every pair to an address. Complete quantities for outputs 7, 13 and 19 remain unsettled. `jeff-z-shop-16.jpg` shows the insert panel's front, without circuit wiring.

Method: visual inspection and paraphrase. Transcribed by: primary curator. Reviewed: true. The source discloses replacement bulbs and repairs; photographed parts are not assumed factory-original.
