# Grand Lizard operation and mechanism evidence

Production manual PDF 8-10 (printed 4-6), PDF 34 (printed 30), PDF 43-46
(printed 39-42). Curator visually checked native renders. Prose below is a
factual paraphrase; the table columns quoted below are literal, with line wraps
joined. No numeric VPX tuning value is promoted to factory mechanical timing.

The four player displays, Credits and BALL IN PLAY/MATCH are in the backbox.
PDF 34 illustrates C-10866 seven-digit alphanumeric player panels, C-8364
seven-digit numeric panels and C-8365 four-digit BALL-IN-PLAY/MATCH panel.
PDF 8 says Credits and BALL IN PLAY/MATCH are two two-digit display groups.

PDF 9's rules distinguish L-I-Z and A-R-D: completing L-I-Z earns one left
Magna-Save unit; completing A-R-D earns one right unit. Completing LIZARD
advances the bonus multiplier and first completion lights spinner 2000.
The three-bank advances the cave award on its first three completions; later
completions light/award extra ball and special. A hit on the flash bank starts
its timed completion challenge. Both ramps pass through the lizard head;
the A ramp's underside holds up to three balls, detected by Multi-Ball1/2/3.
Locking three, or locking one/two then hitting the top release target, begins
multiball. Two balls give 2X all scores and three give 3X. The right chute to the
top awards Bonus Holdover when lit. The manual supplies rule-level timing
(a short SPEED 50 target window), not a numerical physical kick or reset time.

PDF 8: coin-lockout de-energizes while the credit maximum is reached, rejecting
new coins until credits fall below the maximum. Slam tilt ends the game; ball
roll or playfield tilt loses the ball; the third plumb-bob closure tilts the
ball. A startup switch report can identify switches unused for 60 balls; the
ROM may compensate rules for a failed switch. A recreation should not mistake
that compensation for the intended normal mechanism behavior.
