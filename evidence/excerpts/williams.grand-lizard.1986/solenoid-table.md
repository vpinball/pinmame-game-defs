Notes (literal text, line wrapping joined):

1. Wire colors, except flipper Orn-Vio and Orn-Gry, are ground connections (to coil terminal with unbanded end of diode). Flipper Orn-Vio and Orn-Gry wires connect from CPU Board to flipper switch.
2. Solenoid 16 has a Coinco part number.
3. Connections shown in parentheses are from flipper switch to flipper coil.

The COIL TEST is test 04; it cycles 01-22 and shows each solenoid name, unless
halted with MANUAL-DOWN. Advance in MANUAL-DOWN steps one selected driver.
No coil address is printed for any of the four flippers.
