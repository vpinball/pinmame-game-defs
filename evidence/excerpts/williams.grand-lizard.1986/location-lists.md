# Grand Lizard location-list tables

Production manual PDF 48-50, printed 44-46. Complete Switches, Lamps and
Solenoids/Flashers printed tables, visually checked against native renders.
The adjacent numbered drawings are not reproduced here; they are independently
retained and cited by spatial evidence. Spacing/line wraps are normalized only.
An asterisk is a printed footnote, not part of a normalized part number.
