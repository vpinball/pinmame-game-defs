# Cirqus Voltaire service-bulletin excerpts

## SB 101 — December 9, 1997

The bulletin applies to the first 200 samples. It says the disappearing jet-bumper release coil frame can bend downward so the bumper does not drop. Its photographs show the correct latch orientation and the added bracket, part 01-14803, that prevents the coil frame from bending.

## SB 102 — December 9, 1997

The bulletin announces upgrade kit A-22270. It says the kit corrects ball traps or hang-ups, reduces balls hanging or sticking on ramp switches, and prevents the display plastic from pinching the playfield. The retained first page includes Upgrade A's display-panel bracket instructions and drawing.

## SB 104 — April 2, 1998

The adjustment sheet lists five checks: a possible ball hang-up on the plastic in front of the display, fastening that plastic with a wood screw, a ball sticking under the upper plastics below the display, a ball falling off the left wire ramp, and changing the pivot on the three center lock switch blades.
