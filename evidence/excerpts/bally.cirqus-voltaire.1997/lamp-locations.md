Lamp 88's assembly (`20-9663-16`) is the identical illuminated-button assembly part number printed
for switch 13 (Start Button) in `switch-locations.md` -- confirming it is a real cabinet button lamp
even though the retained VPX table's `UpdateLamps` routine has no active call for it (both its
`Lampm 88, l88`/`l88b` lines are commented out). Column headers on this page: `Item Number`, `Lamp
Assembly Part Number`, `Bulb Type`, `Bulb Part Number`, `Socket Part Number`, `Description`.
