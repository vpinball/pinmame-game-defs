# TRON Legacy Pro coil chart

Source: Tron-Manual.pdf, PDF page 51, PRO COILS DETAILED CHART TABLE.

The Pro chart enumerates Q1-Q32, including the trough up-kicker, auto launch, disc direction and motor, Recognizer motor/relay, orbit post, pops, flippers, slingshots, Zen/video/back-panel/bottom-arch/disc/Recognizer flashers, and the optional shaker and coin-meter outputs. Connector, voltage and driver data are listed for each circuit.
