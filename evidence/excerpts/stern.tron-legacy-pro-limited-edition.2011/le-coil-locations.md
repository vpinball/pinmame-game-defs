# TRON Legacy Limited Edition coil locations

Source: Tron-Manual.pdf, PDF page 58, LE COIL & FLASH LAMP LOCATIONS.

The LE drawing places the numbered coils and flash lamps on the physical playfield, including the 4-bank, disc, Recognizer, ramp, pop, slingshot and back-panel assemblies. It includes the typical trough up-kicker wiring schematic used for the high-current coil circuit.
