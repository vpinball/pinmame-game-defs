# TRON Legacy Pro switch matrix

Source: Tron-Manual.pdf, PDF page 49, PRO SWITCH MATRIX GRID.

The Pro matrix covers switches 1-64 across four drives and sixteen returns. The grid labels the trough, shooter, outlanes, ramps, loops, disc, Recognizer, 3-bank, spinners, orbit and back-panel motor contacts; dedicated D-1 through D-32 and the CPU/Sound DIP switches are printed below it.
