# TRON Legacy Pro coil locations

Source: Tron-Manual.pdf, PDF page 52, PRO COIL & FLASH LAMP LOCATIONS.

The Pro drawing places the numbered coils and flash lamps on the physical playfield, including the disc, Recognizer, ramps, pops, slingshots and bottom-arch assemblies. It also includes a typical Q1 trough up-kicker wiring schematic.
