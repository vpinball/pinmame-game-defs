# TRON Legacy Limited Edition coil chart

Source: Tron-Manual.pdf, PDF page 57, LE COILS DETAILED CHART TABLE.

The LE chart enumerates Q1-Q32 and identifies the 4-bank drop-target mechanism, disc motor and direction relay, Recognizer motor/relay, orbit post, flippers, slingshots, dome/disc/back-panel flashes, and the remaining playfield flashers. It also records the connector, voltage and driver details for each output.
