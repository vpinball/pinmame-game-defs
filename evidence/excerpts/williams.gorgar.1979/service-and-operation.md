# Snake Pit and game operation

Instruction booklet 16P-496-103, pages 1–2, 6–7, and service bulletin SS20 pages 1–2, separately cited in the definition. This is a factual summary of the visually read pages.

- One ball is served at Start. Player-up flashes until a scoring switch closes. The right and left flipper buttons switch the flipper coils through the game-on relay.
- Completing G-O-R advances the magnet award. The upper-left pit switch triggers the under-playfield magnet and flashes the magnet lights. The hit scores 1,000 or the active award, and a value of at least 20,000 resets it to 1,000. Completing G-A-R spots GAR, advances the jets and lights the bottom outside specials in alternation.
- The eject saucer awards its own flashing value (5,000 base), or an extra ball when lit. GOR and GAR advance those awards. A through E and targets 1–4 advance bonus. A-B-C advances the bonus multiplier.
- Playfield and ball-roll tilts tilt the current ball on the first closure. The plumb bob allows an adjustable number of warnings. Slam tilt ends play.
- SS20 identifies repeated closure or contact bounce at the magnet leaf switch as a cause of the 2.5 A solenoid fuse opening and specifies an approximately 1/8-inch gap and 1/16-inch followthrough.
- SS20 identifies the early shared return from 3P3-3 as a cause of erratic special-solenoid operation if it opens. Its change isolates the magnet and flash-lamp ground from the special-switch return. This does not change which public addresses operate the relay and the lamps.
