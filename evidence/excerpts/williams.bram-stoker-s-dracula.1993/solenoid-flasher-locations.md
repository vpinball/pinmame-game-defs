General Illumination: 01-05 all `24-8768`, no separate assembly number, described
"Lower Playfield, Insert" / "Upper Playfield, Insert" / "Center Playfield, Insert" /
"Insert" / "Cabinet, Insert" respectively (all five marked `*`, cross-referenced to a
"Not Shown" footnote).

Flippers: `FL-15411`/`A-15205-L-4` "Lower Left Flipper"; `FL-15411`/`A-15205-R-4` "Lower
Right Flipper" — **no third or fourth flipper row is printed**, i.e. no Upper Left/Right
Flipper coil exists on this diagram.

Footnote: `* Not Shown`.

This page independently corroborates `solenoid-flasher-wiring.md`: solenoids 33-36 are
printed here under their real playfield function names (Diverter, Gate, Release Post,
Gate Actuator), not as generic "flipper" circuits, and the only two flipper rows present
are the lower positions.
