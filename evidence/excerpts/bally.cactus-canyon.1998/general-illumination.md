# Cactus Canyon — General Illumination

Transcribed from `Cactus_Canyon_Manual.pdf`, PDF pages 94 and 100, printed pages 2-36 and 2-42, the
general-illumination location and wiring blocks printed on the same pages as the solenoid/flasher
tables. The retained PDF carries an OCR text layer, but it is garbled on these dense tabular pages,
so this is confirmed against the rendered pages, which are the source of record.
