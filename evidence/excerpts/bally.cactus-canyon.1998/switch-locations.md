## Opto sweep — every cue, checked column by column against PinMAME

Sweep both cues (matrix shading and a populated Opto Assembly Part Number with blank Switch Part
Number), not just one, then compare the union against `ccGameData`'s inverted-switch mask index by
index.

Cue A (matrix shading "OPTO, TYPICALLY CLOSED", see `switch-matrix.md`): column 3 in full (31-38)
plus F2/F4/F6/F8.

Cue B (parts list above: an LED+phototransistor board pair, or a documented single-channel opto-board
assembly from `opto-board-assemblies.md`, with the Switch Part Number column blank): 31,32,33,34,35
(A-18617-1/A-18618-1); 36,37,41,42 (A-16908/A-16909); 71 (A-22407, confirmed single-channel Train
Single Opto board); 77,78 (A-22443, confirmed 2-channel Mine Dual Opto board); F2,F4 (A-17316,
confirmed 2-channel Flipper Opto PCB); F6,F8 (A-17316 template, unfitted — no upper flippers on this
machine).

Rows that show a blank Switch Part Number but are not opto (single combined switch+bracket assembly,
confirmed by the absence of any board-page opto assembly and by PinMAME's own mask leaving their
column bit at 0): 28, 44, 86, 87 (A-20499-12 standup-target assemblies) and 46 (A-20783-7 Beer Mug
switch assembly). These prove that "blank Switch Part Number" alone is not a reliable opto signal;
the board-assembly pages are the tie-breaker.

Union of cues A and B, excluding F6/F8 (unfitted, no upper flippers): {31, 32, 33, 34, 35, 36, 37, 41,
42, 71, 77, 78, 112 (F2), 114 (F4)} — 14 physically normally-closed opto switches.

`ccGameData`'s inverted-switch mask (`src/wpc/sims/wpc/prelim/cc.c`):
`{0x00, 0x00, 0x00, 0x7f, 0x03, 0x00, 0x00, 0xc1, 0x00, 0x00, 0x00, 0x00}` indexed `[Coin, col1, col2,
col3, col4, col5, col6, col7, col8, col9, col10, Cust]` where `Cust` (index 11) is
`CORE_FLIPPERSWCOL` (`core.h` line 334).

- col3 = `0x7f` = bits 0-6 = rows 1-7 = switches 31-37 -> matches (38 is Not Used, needs no bit).
- col4 = `0x03` = bits 0-1 = rows 1-2 = switches 41-42 -> matches.
- col7 = `0xc1` = bits 0,6,7 = rows 1,7,8 = switches 71,77,78 -> matches.
- `Cust`/flip column = `0x00`. This is identical to Medieval Madness, Attack From Mars, and Monster
  Bash at this same array index: WPC-95 reads that column through `WPC_FLIPPERSW95`
  (`src/wpc/wpc.c` line 630) with its own fixed hardware inversion, independent of the driver's
  `invSw` array. F2/F4 are therefore not a conflict.

Result: full agreement. Every opto address the manual documents (union of both cues, correctly
excluding the unfitted upper-flipper opto template and the four non-opto blank-Switch-Part-Number
rows) is exactly the set PinMAME's `ccGameData` normalizes. There is no
`conflict.*-opto-not-normalized` entry for this machine, unlike Monster Bash's Dracula-position
column.
