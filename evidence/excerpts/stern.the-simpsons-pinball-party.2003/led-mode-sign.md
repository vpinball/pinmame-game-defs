The lamp-location drawing stamps `73` to `80` in white boxes ("Lamps mounted

That part number disagrees with the three pages above, which all name
`520-5225-00` for the LED board and reserve `520-5219-00` for the TV's Color
Dot Display. Recorded as printed; the curation follows the three agreeing,
more specific pages.
