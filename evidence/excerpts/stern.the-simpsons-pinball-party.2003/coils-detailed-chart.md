which of the 32 positions are physically flashers: 21, 22, 23, 25, 26, 27, 28,
29, 31, 32 (ten total). Position 30 ("Drop Bank Trips") uses a #906-adjacent
GA-turn coil rating, not a bulb, and is not in the flasher-test list, so it is
a coil (drop-target reset kicker), not a flasher, despite sitting inside the
17-32 "typically flashers" numbering band the diagnostics-menu overview text
describes.

## Typical Coil Wiring (schematic box, same page)

Coil driven between Power Supply Voltage and the drive transistor's collector,
flyback diode `1N4004` across the coil (Cathode/banded side to the power-
supply side, Anode/non-banded side to the drive-transistor side).

## Bulb Types used for Flash Lamps

`#89 Bulb` (Bayonet), part 165-5000-89. `#906 Bulb` (Wedge Base), part
165-5004-00. Neither bulb type is used on this machine's 32-position chart
(all ten flashers print `#906 Bulb 165-5004-00`); `#89` is listed as a
reference key only.
