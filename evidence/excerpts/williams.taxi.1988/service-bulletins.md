# Taxi service bulletins — factual evidence notes

Official Williams/Bally/Midway Service Bulletin Book retained through IPDB. Sol visually inspected native scans; these are paraphrases, not copied repair instructions.

PDF 15, printed 13, SS54 (December 1, 1988): Spinout ramp wear caused by ball travel can damage the bowl wall. A-12471 wall protector is fitted to R-12262; the illustration positions its upper edge approximately 3/8 inch below the bowl wall top. This is a wear-protection update, with no added switch or coil.

PDF 16..17, printed 14..15, SS55 (November 18, 1988): System 11B D-12246 power supply can develop excess positive/negative 100 V display supply after regulator failure. The bulletin changes R2/R5 resistor values from 680 ohm to 1.2 kohm and ZR2/ZR4 to 1N4764. This is a backbox power/display reliability issue, not a new playfield address. Continuation was acquired and OCR retained; only the visually checked first-page component facts are asserted here.
