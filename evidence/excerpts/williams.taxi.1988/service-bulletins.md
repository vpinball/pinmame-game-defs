# Taxi service bulletins — factual evidence notes

Official Williams/Bally/Midway Service Bulletin Book retained through IPDB. Sol visually inspected native scans; these are paraphrases, not copied repair instructions.

PDF15, printed13, SS54 (December1,1988): Spinout ramp wear caused by ball travel can damage the bowl wall. A-12471 wall protector is fitted to R-12262; the illustration positions its upper edge approximately3/8 inch below the bowl wall top. This is a wear-protection update, with no added switch or coil.

PDF16..17, printed14..15, SS55 (November18,1988): System11B D-12246 power supply can develop excess positive/negative100V display supply after regulator failure. The bulletin changes R2/R5 resistor values from680ohm to1.2kohm and ZR2/ZR4 to1N4764. This is a backbox power/display reliability issue, not a new playfield address. Continuation was acquired and OCR retained; only the visually checked first-page component facts are asserted here.
