# Taxi matrices — complete printed tables

Williams manual PDF 73. Flattened column-major; address=(column-1)*8+row. Empty cells are printed blanks. Source names kept verbatim except the PIN•BOT separator is normalized to a hyphen, with line wrapping normalized, including the coin reversal; canonical names use the cabinet circuit and parts list. Sol visually verified both matrices at native resolution.

Coin-label erratum: this table prints Left at 4 and Right at 6. PDF 62 parts and PDF 67 actual 7SW4/7SW6 wiring give Right at row 4, Left at row 6. All retained ROM tables agree with the latter. Earlier diagnostic chart PDF 34 names 51/52, unlike this final matrix, the complete switch parts list, all four retained ROM tables (including P5), and the retained script. Those obsolete chart labels do not establish fitted extra switches.
