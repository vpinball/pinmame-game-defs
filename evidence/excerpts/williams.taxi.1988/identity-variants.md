The page was visually inspected via the Cloudflare-capable browser; its title, manufacturer, model 553 and System 11B fields agree with the factory cover and pinned Taxi driver. IPDB states manufacture August 31, 1988. Factory manual cover identifies TAXI, model 553, September 1988; preliminary operations manual is August 1988. Physical title is Williams Taxi, 1988, System 11B. The playfield has two lower flippers, three jets, two slings, two three-target banks, one standup, ramp returns, the manual-plunger Spinout and a catapult.

IPDB describes sample Marilyn artwork/software changing to Lola on production backglass, playfield and instruction card. The native drivers retain both names; identical controller initialization supports shared I/O but does not establish all prototype manufacturing or competition rules differences. taxi_l3/LU1/LG1/P5 use Marilyn nomenclature; taxi_l4/L5C Lola, taxi_l5cm Marilyn competition.

All four locally supplied U27 tables (L4,L3,LG1,P5) enumerate the same switch/service coil addresses; P5 and Marilyn revisions rename the middle passenger bank. Source does not supply a separate prototype mechanical simulation. No independent prototype construction drawing or complete legal L5C rule/ROM study was obtained. Variant differences remain an authoring-critical missing dimension; do not claim prototype hardware is identical merely because init_taxi is shared.
