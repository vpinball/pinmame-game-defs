Literal details kept as printed:

- 15 prints its playfield connection as `5J5-2: 5J6-2`; the neighbouring controlled rows print `5J2-`
  pins. Transcribed as printed.
- 17 prints its CPU-board pin as `1P10-7`; the other five special solenoids print `1P19-` pins.
  Transcribed as printed.
- 10, 11 and 12 carry no flashlamp column and share relay part 5580-09555-01, distinguished only by
  the note markers.

Notes as printed under the table:

> [1] Wire colors, except flipper Orn-Vio and Orn-Gry, are ground connections (to coil terminal
> with unbanded end of diode). Flipper Orn-Vio and Orn-Gry wires connect from CPU Board to flipper
> switch. [2] Flipper connections shown in braces are from flipper switch to flipper coil. [3] "A"
> circuits are pulsed, when Sol. 12 is de-energized; "C" circuits are pulsed, with Sol. 12
> energized. Wire colors in brackets are those from respective "A" and "C" terminals corresponding
> to the J1-terminal connection listed for the Aux Power Driver Bd, which controls the device
> pulsing by Sol. 12. [4a] Relay is mounted on the playfield, p/n C-11998-1. [4b] Relay is mounted on
> the Insert Bd, p/n C-11998-1. [5] Relay is mounted on Aux Power Driver Board, p/n D-12247 in the
> backbox.
