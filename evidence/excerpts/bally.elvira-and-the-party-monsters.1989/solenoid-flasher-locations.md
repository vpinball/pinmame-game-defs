# Bally Elvira and the Party Monsters (1989) — Solenoids & Flashers Location Diagram

Source: Bally Midway *Elvira and the Party Monsters* Operations and Parts Information Manual
16-2011-101, IPDB machine 782 file `Elvira_and_the_Partymonsters_OCR_searchable.pdf`, PDF page 67,
printed page 2-15. Read from the native 300 dpi render.

The drawing uses two kinds of callout: a rectangular box for a coil and a starburst circle for a
flashlamp circuit. Each callout has one or more leader lines to the device positions.

Coil boxes, as drawn:

| Callout | Position on the drawing |
| --- | --- |
| 01A | bottom centre, at the outhole end of the trough |
| 02A | lower right, at the shooter-lane feed |
| 03A | centre right, at the drop-target bank |
| 05A | top centre, at the eject hole |
| 06A | top left, at the popper |
| 08A | upper left, at the lock release in the left lane |
| 14 | upper left, beside the popper, below 06A |
| 17 | inside the upper-left bumper |
| 19 | inside the upper-right bumper |
| 21 | at the lower bumper, above 03A |
| 22 | left of centre, below the left ramp, at the flip-up targets |
| 18 | inside the left slingshot |
| 20 | inside the right slingshot |

Flasher starbursts, as drawn, with the number of leader lines each carries:

| Callout | Leaders | Leader ends |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 16 | 3 | three sockets in the upper-left quadrant around the popper and the 14 box |
| 01C | 1 | between the upper bumpers |
| 02C | 1 | at the drop-target bank, next to 03A |
| 03C | 1 | at the right ramp entrance |
| 04C | 1 | at the left ramp, left of centre |

| 06C | 1 | the right return lane |
| 07C | 1 | the left return lane |
| 08C | 1 | runs up and right from the left side toward the skull passage |
| 15 | 1 | a socket on the left side rail, below 08C's leader |
| 13 | 1 | a socket on the right side, beside the right lane |

No callout exists for 04A, 07A (knocker), 09 to 12, or the flipper coils.

Two leader counts differ from the bulb quantities printed in the Solenoids & Flashers table on
printed page 2-14: callout 16 has three leaders where the table prints "#906 flashlamp 2p", and
callout 15 has one leader where the table prints "#906/#89 flashlamps 2p,1i". Callout 05C has no
leader at all while the table prints "2p,1i"; its starburst is drawn over the large round centre
insert, which can hold both playfield bulbs, so no count is contradicted there.
