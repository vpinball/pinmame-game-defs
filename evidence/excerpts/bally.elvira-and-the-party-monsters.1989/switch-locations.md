Notes as printed:

- `*P/N for entire Diagnostic Switch Assembly, including High Score Reset Switch.`
- `**Optotransistor on Backbox Interconnect Board.`
- `***Paired Kicker Actuating Sw: B-12459; B-12715`

The `**` note is printed but no item in the list carries a `**` marker; the `*` note attaches to
item 8 and `***` to items 33 and 34.

The bumper part is printed `p/o C-12872` on this page, while the Playfield Parts list (printed
2-12, item 13 `Thumper Bumpers`) prints `C-12842`.

Numbered drawing (right side of the page): callouts 51, 50, 52, 49 and 56, 55 stand to the left of
the playfield with leader lines into the upper-left lock lane and the flip-up targets; callouts on
the playfield itself read 32 (upper-left popper), 48 (eject hole, top centre), 22 (top right),
45, 46, 47 (three top lanes), 35, 36, 37 (bumpers), 23 (right side), 29 (left of the left ramp
entrance), 30 (left ramp entrance), 53, 54 (flip-up targets), 41, 42, 43 (drop targets, stepping
down to the right), 44 (right ramp entrance), 25, 26, 27, 28 (left standups, top to bottom), 15, 16
(right standups), 33, 34 (left and right slingshots), 17, 18 (left outlane and return), 19, 20
(right return and outlane), 58, 57 (left and right flippers), 11, 12, 13 (trough, 11 nearest the
shooter lane), 21 (shooter lane) and 9 (outhole).
