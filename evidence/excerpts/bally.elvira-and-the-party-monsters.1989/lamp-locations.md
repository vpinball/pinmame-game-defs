# Bally Elvira and the Party Monsters (1989) — Lamp Location Diagram

Source: Bally Midway *Elvira and the Party Monsters* Operations and Parts Information Manual
16-2011-101, IPDB machine 782 file `Elvira_and_the_Partymonsters_OCR_searchable.pdf`, PDF page 91,
printed page 2-39. Read from the native 300 dpi render.

The page is a single playfield outline with lamp numbers printed beside the inserts they identify.
Numbers 1 through 56 appear on the drawing; 57 through 64 do not appear anywhere on it.

Positions as drawn, rear (top of page) to front:

- Top: 45, 46, 47 across the three top lanes; 44 and 43 stacked down the centre lane below the eject
  hole; 42 and 41 continuing down the centre; 54 and 55 inside the two upper bumpers and 56 inside
  the lower bumper.
- Upper left: 25 on the left ramp; 14 and 15 beside the flip-up targets; 18 and 17 beside the left
  lane rail (17 lower left, 18 just above and right of it); 13 below 18; 28 and 12 in the triangular
  inserts below the left ramp entrance.
- Centre: 38, 39, 40 in a diagonal row stepping down to the right beside the drop targets; 48 the
  large round centre insert; 27 and 26 below it on the left; 37 right of centre; 33, 34, 35, 36 in
  two pairs right of centre.
- Left side: 21, 22, 23, 24 top to bottom beside the four left standups.
- Right side: 53, 52, 51 stepping down the right lane; 49 and 50 beside the right standups.
- Lower playfield: 1 to 6 in an arc above the flippers, left to right; 7 and 9 on the left, 8 and 10
  on the right, 16 between them; 31 and 29 at the left return lane and outlane; 32 and 30 at the
  right return lane and outlane; 19 between the flippers.
- Slingshots: **20** is printed beside the insert inside the **left** slingshot and **11** beside the
  insert inside the **right** slingshot.

The slingshot callouts are the opposite way round from the Lamp Matrix on printed page 2-38, which
names 11 "Left Slingshot" and 20 "Right Slingshot".
