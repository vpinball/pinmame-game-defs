## Special and controlled solenoid logic — PDF page 44, printed page 1-38

The two drawings at the top of the page, as printed:

- `"On" State Logic - Special Solenoid`: `From PIA` through a `7407` and a `Spl Sol. Trigger` input on
  `1J18` meet at a `7402` NOR gate annotated `Low, with flippers enabled`, which drives a `2N 4401`
  and a `TIP 122` switching the coil return on `1P19`, with `Sol. B+ +25 V` on the coil and the
  legend `Special Sol. "ON"`.
- `"On" State Logic - Controlled`: `From PIA` and `BLANKING` into a `7408` AND gate driving the same
  `2N 4401` / `TIP 122` stage on `1P19`, legend `Cntrld Sol. "ON"`.
- `"Off" State - Special Solenoid: The Special Switch Trigger Input goes low. Meanwhile, the PIA line
  remains high. The remaining signals reverse their states.`

## ELVIRA Rules and Features — PDF page 4 (unnumbered)

Boxed text, quoted in part:

- DEAD HEADS STANDUP TARGETS: "Completing all four Dead Head standup targets lights one Dead Head on
  Backboard ... Completing all three Backboard Dead Heads lights MILLION on Eject Hole."
- FLIP UP TARGETS: "Completion of the two Flip Up targets lights BARBEQUE on Eject Hole."
- BALL LOCK FEATURE: "Completion of the B-A-T lanes or the JAM drop targets enables the Skull to lock
  a ball."
- SKULL PASSAGE: "Going through the Skull locks balls if enabled and scores 3 Million when lit."
- PIZZA STANDUP TARGETS: "Completion of the right standup targets lights one more Pizza Passage
  light and the Return Lanes."
- PIZZA PASSAGE: "Going through the Pizza Passage awards and advances the current Pizza Passage
  value."
- MONSTER SLIDE RAMP FEATURE names the Left Ramp; PARTY PUNCH RAMP FEATURE names the Right Ramp.
- "JUMPER" BUMPERS: "Actuating the Bumpers increases BOOGIE BONUS when BOOGIE BONUS is lit."
