# X-Men Pro: complete coil chart

Source: Stern_2012_X-Men_Pro.pdf, SHA-256 d6ae672cf65299ff60e108a6b039275dd2393619b6c9e930c681cd285f635fbe, PDF page 16, printed 14. Scan supplied by Pinside owner redundor, post 2591274. Visually checked against the 2550 x 3300 native composite; OCR was used for search only.

The original prints four blocks: High Current Coils Group 1 (#1-8), High Current Coils Group 2 (#9-16), Low Current Coils Group 1 (#17-24), Low Current Coils Group 2 (#25-32). The merged Driver Output PCB cell in each block is I/O Power Driver, repeated here for every row rather than pretending it is separately printed. The Drive Transistor is Q followed by the printed row number. [blank] means an actually blank cell. Coil gauge/turn and part number share a source cell; the semicolon below separates those two printed lines. ND suffixes include the handwritten corrections visible on the scan; they are not inferred from neighboring rows. Source punctuation and printed wire spelling are retained; line breaks are normalized to spaces.

Each row uses Drive Transistor Qn and the merged I/O Power Driver PCB cell described above. Group 2 visibly reuses J8-P1/P2/P4/P5/P6/P7/P8/P9; this is preserved literally. The combined LE manual instead gives J8-P11/P12/P14/P15/P16/P17/P18/P19. That is a connector-detail disagreement, not a different device or controller address. The Pro table governs the structured reading, and the alternate is stated on the affected records.

Row 20 is deliberately not collapsed to an entirely empty row: its function is blank while its power, voltage and bulb cells remain populated. The Pro location drawing has no 20 callout and the Pro firmware coil selector skips 19, 20, 23 and 30. The residual bulb cell alone does not identify a fitted device. Q27 is the Pro spotlight, Q31 the figure lamp, and Q32 the 50 V Wolverine magnet; the LE's motor/arch/figure assignments do not apply.
