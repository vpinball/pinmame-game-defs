# Exact Pro VPT coordinate recovery and its limits

The known-working XMen FS (physmod5).vpt is 25,464,832 bytes, SHA-256 00784b76eb35991d4bb4b13939862f67506f06cb017426668d57a66ded8829d8. The original and its old extracts stay unchanged.

7-Zip 22.01 reads its Compound container without entering VPXTool's failing legacy image parser. Command: `7z x -o<new-output> <exact-vpt>`. The retained extraction `external:pinmame-vpx-sources/stern/x-men-pro-2012/extraction/7zip-22.01-20261106` contains 1,940 raw streams, 24,518,088 bytes. The official `tools/build_external_evidence_manifest.py --game xmn_151` manifest is a10a88327cc1133e613b440c432448a47d45e3dcbf79b40ae5f7cf0cb951c386. All streams were compared byte-for-byte with the independently retained OpenMcdf extraction. Equality verifies container extraction, not a full semantic parse.

`tools/xmen_pro_vpt_geometry.py` reads bounded BIFF record lengths, one explicit UTF-16 NAME, direct binary32 VCEN centers, Surface DPNT/VCEN points and explicit TMIN lamp numbers. It retains flasher FLAX/FLAY only as render candidates. It rejects malformed framing, missing/duplicate fields, non-finite coordinates, missing/extra GameItem streams, ambiguous names and any changed raw manifest. Three VP9 Decal/font streams remain opaque and cannot be anchors. Other record payloads are not interpreted; no primitive origin or parser default is accepted as geometry.

GameData explicitly gives 1,702 items and bounds LEFT=0, TOPX=0, RGHT=959, BOTM=2162. Its embedded CODE payload is 42,442 bytes, SHA-256 90cd88f121798f7c91ba738713c2aae99784751637380beb864ee3e2bcde25cb, exactly matching both retained script extracts. Selected coordinate fields are portable in `tools/seeds/stern/x-men-pro-2012-vpt-geometry.json`; external-root verification reparses the raw streams and compares the whole selected result.

Runtime bindings are read from that script: swN Hit/UnHit or Spin handlers name inputs; Bumper2b/1b/3b pulse 30/31/32; LeftSlingShot and RightSlingShot pulse 26/27. The two magnets bind Magnet1 to solenoid 32 and Magnet3 to 4. The VUK captures at sw55a, while sw55 is a separate elevated ejection helper. Actuator projections use the physical capture assembly, never that helper. EOS contacts project onto their own lower-flipper pivots and remain observed. Trough 18-22 are generated by bsTrough and have no individual object centers; BallRelease remains one eject anchor, not five sensors.

The script calls vpmMapLights GoodLights but sets UseLamps=0 and uses its own ChangedLamps/FadeL N,lN,lNa loop. This explicit loop supplies the controlling address. Copied TMIN=52 on all eight character-ring lights and TMIN=100 on the three bumper lights are not their runtime identities. The two render layers share one lamp, not a quantity of two. The normalized point is the primary Light center divided by 959/2162 and rounded once to six decimals. Surface targets/slings use the arithmetic mean of their own drag points. These are observed layout points, not independently validated bulb sockets. No Flasher sprite is a socket, and no LE coordinate or GI map populates the Pro records.

One specifically reviewed Ramp is an exception to generic Ramp rejection: wr3/GameItem1566 is the visible noncollidable Magneto figure billboard using literal image magneto. Its bottom endpoint (522,489), not a centroid, projects 31 onto its own figure assembly at (0.544317,0.226179), observed only. The reader additionally retains only this Ramp's IMAG/HTBT/HTTP/WDBT/WDTP/CLDR/RVIS fields. Image52's declared DATA payload is a 1,138,374-byte PNG, SHA-256 321e5c738da86722547b45d8363258888cf9285022db76e24be35b91aa86197c; the previous root PNG artifact includes 16 trailing BIFF ENDB bytes and is preserved unchanged. `figure_image()` reproduces the clean successor payload from the exact raw stream. The original raw extraction and manifest remain immutable.

VPXTool git:v0.33.3 still fails its full image extraction with exit 101; the old failure evidence remains intact. The 1.02 ancestor was also retained and inspected through 7-Zip: it has 2,448 objects and 122 lights, with no individual GI census and no physical f59 emitter. It supplements its descendant and supplies no independent physical validation. Neither an unsupported field nor an empty render collection proves missing hardware.
