The retained actual component photograph, 522x453, SHA-256 9c8299d1ab6ee4f67904b52cd8525a4d185532bb05a9877cc6bbcadb7369b808, was previously misread. Pixels (355, 149) and (314, 169) identify nylon cable-tie heads; (270, 190) identifies a white harness clip/connector. They are **not switch actuators**. All three spatial placements are withdrawn. The source pixels, former coordinates and rejected projection inputs remain historical evidence under `rejected_trough_contact_projection`; they supply no active physical point, longitudinal fraction or contact geometry.

The cutaway still establishes microswitch order 18/19/20 and the vertically stacked 21/22 beams. The latter retain their separate observed projection onto the actual exit assembly. No retained accepted reading locates each mechanical actuator, and no equal spacing or replacement point is invented. The curator refuses reinstatement of the withdrawn photo projection.

A second longitudinal reading from the labeled cutaway was considered. Its visible continuous switch-side segment can corroborate 18/19/20 order and approximate spacing, but the drawing break interrupts the full guideplate and the roller tips stand above that side face. Rectifying that segment would not independently recover the entrance-to-exit fractions or remove the component photograph's height offset. No quantitative agreement is asserted from a broken ruler or by treating raised roller tips as coplanar mounting holes.
