Its LEFT EJECT TOP at public 59 agrees with this chart and the location drawing; public 63 is a different, unused address. The owner's later LED shopping sheet lists a blue LED already at 59 and different bulb bases at 50, 56 and 70. That conversion sheet is an owner's observation, not an amendment of the printed table. No conversion color is presented as a factory lamp color or a separate emitter.
