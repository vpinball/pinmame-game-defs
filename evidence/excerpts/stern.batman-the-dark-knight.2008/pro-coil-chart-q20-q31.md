# Batman: The Dark Knight Pro coil chart Q20-Q31

Source: Batman_TDK_Manual.pdf, PDF page 15, coil and flasher detailed chart.

The chart identifies the back-panel, Joker and Scarecrow flashers, optional coin meter, pop-bumper flashers, Joker motor, Scarecrow motor relay, right slingshot flasher, Joker motor relay and Scarecrow motor across the Q20-Q31 range. Q20 is shown as the power/control continuation for the output group, while the named rows identify the physical loads.
