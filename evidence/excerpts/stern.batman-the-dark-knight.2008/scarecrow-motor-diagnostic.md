# Batman: The Dark Knight Scarecrow motor diagnostic

Source: Batman_TDK_Manual.pdf, PDF page 50, game-specific diagnostics.

The service menu's Scarecrow motor test operates Q31 (Scarecrow Motor), relay Q28 (Scarecrow Motor Relay), and switches 56 through 61 (Crane Position Switches). The manual warns not to move the crane by hand and to turn off power before servicing, which constrains the mechanism to motor-driven motion and sensor-based endpoints.
