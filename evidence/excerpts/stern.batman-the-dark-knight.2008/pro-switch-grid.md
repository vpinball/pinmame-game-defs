# Batman: The Dark Knight Pro switch matrix

Source: Batman_TDK_Manual.pdf, PDF page 6, switch matrix and playfield switch locations.

The page lays out matrix switches 1-48 and their physical labels, including the left and right trough contacts, shooter lane, lanes, outlanes, return lanes, slingshots, pop bumpers, Joker positions, Batmobile crash, and Scarecrow VUK. It is the manual proof for the Pro matrix address inventory; the dedicated cabinet and flipper contacts continue on PDF page 8.
