# Batman: The Dark Knight Scarecrow crane type 2 parts

Source: Batman_TDK_Manual.pdf, PDF page 175 (printed Section 4, Chapter 2, page 96), Type 2 crane arm parts list.

The parts list identifies the square-hub crane arm assembly, crane pendulum assembly, modified one-inch carbon ball, pendulum spring and hub, and the Scarecrow-arm sensor bracket. These parts explain the physical pendulum and endpoint-sensing arrangement behind the six crane-position switches.
