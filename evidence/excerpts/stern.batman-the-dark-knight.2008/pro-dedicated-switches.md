# Batman: The Dark Knight Pro dedicated switches

Source: Batman_TDK_Manual.pdf, PDF page 8, dedicated switch and cabinet input table.

The continuation lists dedicated D-12 through D-16 inputs, the flipper end-of-stroke and cabinet service contacts, and the crane contact. It also records the cabinet connector wiring and states that no switches are located on the back panel. These are separate from the 1-64 playfield switch matrix.
