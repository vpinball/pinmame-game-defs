# Avengers Limited Edition coil chart

Source: `Avengers-LE-Manual-compressed.pdf`, PDF page 67, printed page 8.

The complete `COILS DETAILED CHART TABLE` lists Q1-Q32 with their physical functions, drive groups, wiring colors, control connectors, voltages, and coil or bulb types. It covers the trough kicker, auto launch, Hulk motors and eject, drop reset, orbit gate, shaker option, pops, Loki lockup, slings, flippers, relay, flashes, bridge motor and relay, Hulk magnet, Tesseract, and backpanel outputs.
