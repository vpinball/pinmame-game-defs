# Avengers Limited Edition GI LED locations

Source: `Avengers-LE-Manual-compressed.pdf`, PDF page 117, printed sheet Y15.

The `AVENGERS LIMITED EDITION` GI LED locations drawing identifies the green, red, and blue LED assemblies and places their G/R/B markers across the playfield. The legend includes the corresponding 112-5033-04, 112-5033-02, and 112-5033-05 part numbers.
