# Avengers Limited Edition auxiliary board wiring

Source: `Avengers-LE-Manual-compressed.pdf`, PDF page 108, printed sheet Y5.

The `AVENGERS PREMIUM` eight-transistor driver-board wiring diagram maps Q41-Q48 to the coil cable and shows the board's D0-D7 switch-cable data, strobe, power, and ground connections. The board layout and 520-5325-00 revision marking are visible below the wiring diagram.
