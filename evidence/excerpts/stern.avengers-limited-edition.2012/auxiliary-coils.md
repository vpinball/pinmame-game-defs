# Avengers Limited Edition auxiliary coil chart

Source: `Avengers-LE-Manual-compressed.pdf`, PDF page 69, printed page 10.

The auxiliary driver table identifies physical drivers 41-48 and their assembly names: center four-bank drop reset, right and left ramp control gates, Hulk magnet, green relay, Hulk arms, right orbit control gate, and red relay. It also records each drive transistor, power-line color and voltage, control-line color, and coil type.
