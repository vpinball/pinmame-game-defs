# Avengers Limited Edition lamp matrix

Source: `Avengers-LE-Manual-compressed.pdf`, PDF page 65, printed page 6.

The complete `LAMP MATRIX GRID [#1 - #80]` lists the Limited Edition LED board positions, lamp numbers, physical labels, and part numbers. It visibly covers the Thor, Captain America, Hawkeye, Hulk, Black Widow, Tesseract, ramp, lock, pop, Iron Man, top-lane, shield-agent, and orbit positions.
