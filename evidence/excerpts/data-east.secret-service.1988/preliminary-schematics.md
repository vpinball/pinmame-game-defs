# Secret Service preliminary-manual schematic observations

Source: preliminary instruction manual, PDF pages 11-14 (printed pages 10-13). The scan's printed page 20 is absent. This document is used only for schematic facts that the production manual does not supply; the production manual governs disagreements.

- PDF page 11 / printed page 10 shows separate high-current `RIGHT FLIPPER SWITCH` and `LEFT FLIPPER SWITCH` cabinet circuits, with BLU-VIO and BLU-GRY leads. They are not numbered as switch-matrix addresses.
- PDF page 12 / printed page 11 shows the 8x8 playfield switch wiring, column-major SW9-SW64, separately from the cabinet flipper-button circuit.
- PDF page 13 / printed page 12 shows the 8x8 playfield lamp wiring LB1-LB64 and a separate G.I. lamp circuit.
- PDF page 14 / printed page 13 shows the +32V L/R power-select relay on the MRB, the shared Q46-Q39 drive pairs, the post, kickback and Kickbig relay/coil circuits, five switch-triggered coils, and three flipper assemblies: Left Flipper, Right Flipper, and Upper Right Flipper.
- That preliminary solenoid schematic labels the SP2 device `YELLOW POP BUMPER`, while the production Coil Test and Coil Identification Table both label SP2 `Clear Pop Bumper`; the production label governs.
- The preliminary schematic prints the right-flipper power lead `BLU-YEL`, while the production Coil Identification Table literally prints `BIU-YEL`; the production cell is retained and the discrepancy is explicit.
