# Factory operation and service controls

Williams instruction booklet 16P-501-103, game 501, October 1980, PDF/printed pages 1–3 and 7.
Paraphrased by the curator after visual inspection of native-resolution pages 1,2,3,7. These are
operating instructions, not a reconstruction of the missing blue manual or schematic set.

Page 1 specifies level 6 CPU (level 4 socketed alternatives lose memory protection), green game and
flipper ROMs, J3 connected/J4 removed, D8224 sound board with J1 removed for speech, Sound ROM 2,
optional speech ROMs 5T5011/5012/5013, C8363 master display and seven-digit slaves. Page 1's
Sound ROM 2 statement is retained literally here: pinned PinMAME and the table's alpok_l6 instead
load sound3.716. Filename and board-ROM label are not presumed equivalent.

Start serves one ball. Additional players can enter before ball 2. The four King lanes advance
Royal Flush multiplication; the right button rotates King/jet lamps. The left return lane spots
a King. Lit Jokers advance bonus multiplication. Making the diamond targets in 10,J,Q,K,A order
scores the indicated Royal Flush value. Out-of-order targets lower that value and reset
individually; the Spade saucer can raise the value. The three Ace holes advance P-O-K-E-R;
K lights the two extra-ball arrows and R alternates outlane specials. Operator settings govern
award/memory behavior. These are rule effects, not switch-to-coil electrical connections.

Page 3 diagnostic procedure: Manual-Down + Advance blanks displays; another Advance followed
by Auto-Up runs the digit test. Advance enters lamp test 01. Manual-Down + Advance enters
solenoid test 02 at 01; further Advance pulses each next selector. Auto-Up + Advance enters
switch test 03. Credits shows the test number and ball-in-play shows the selector/switch number.
Power off/on exits diagnostics.

Page 7: set all Master Command switches off before selecting a function. Physical switch 8
zeros audit totals; 7 restores factory settings and audits (door open); 6 selects Auto-Cycle.
Enter executes the selected function; LEDs blink once. Restore requires two power cycles.
Auto-Cycle sequences digit test, 64 lamp flashes and solenoids. Figure 2 labels the lower
eight-position bank NOT USED. The upper bank's remaining five switches have no function printed.
The board diagnostic and Master Command Enter are separate buttons; Figure 1 identifies
coin-door High Score Reset, Advance and Auto-Up/Manual-Down.
