# Physical photographs and bank construction

The curator visually inspected the retained production photographs and original bank images on
2026-11-06. These observations establish construction/count/side/order, not calibrated socket
coordinates. Original images stay external; portable paths and hashes are in the spatial report.

IPDB 48 Mark Steinman's image 24 overall and images 21/23 lower/upper regions agree with the
factory layout: three flippers (two right), four jet bumpers, one left sling/kicker, three Ace
saucers and five independent diamond drops. Jean-Pierre Renault image 19 shows four seven-digit
player displays, the credit/ball window and the backglass indicators. The factory matrix governs
lamp identities. The two extra-ball arrows and the spinner/Royal Flush 3X lights are separate
physical insert locations sharing their respective addresses.

CUJO's Alien Poker five-bank rebuild thread, page 1 post 1 (2024-10-22), photos 172054383,
172100854 and 172112803, shows five individual coil/plunger mechanisms and slider PCBs, plus a
separate perpendicular actuator/common release mechanism. Photos 153927626/154126859 show
removed copper horseshoe PCBs marked 1B-2001-124-4 with contact wear. These are mechanical
sliding contacts; NMP sensor replacements discussed later in the thread are not factory parts.
The booklet identifies all five individual reset coils and separate release 21; the working script
defines their directions. No generic common-reset five-bank model is substituted.

The owner reports a target hanging just above playfield level and out-of-sequence targets failing
to reset. Post 5 describes guides, springs, brackets and horseshoe contact/PCB wear in an actual
rebuild, including targets bouncing upright with weak springs. These are attributed owner failure
observations, not measured ROM timing or factory repair instructions. Capture covers posts 1–35
and only the start of 36; no claim uses the truncated tail.

Factory parts-catalog viewer pages 112–119 were inspected. Its D8362 common-lift/trip assembly
has SA6 coils and does not establish the identity of Alien Poker's bank with five SA3 reset
coils. The relevant generic drawings are not used to fill that gap. Oliver Kaegi's original
outhole photo alien_mp0003 shows the single production coil; original underside image 0006 shows a
separate leaf playfield-tilt assembly. Converted trough/relocated hardware cannot locate the
production tilt switch or supply production geometry.
