# Transformers Pro coil chart

Source: Transformers-Manual.pdf, PDF page 131, PRO COILS DETAILED CHART TABLE.

The Pro chart enumerates Q1-Q32, including the trough and auto-launch, Megatron lockup, orbit gate, Optimus Prime, pops, slingshots, flippers, Decepticon/ramp/side/Bumblebee flashes, ejects, Megatron flash, Optimus motor relay and Optimus/AllSpark flashers. Connector, voltage and driver data are listed for each circuit.
