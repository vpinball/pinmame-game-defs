# Transformers Pro coil locations

Source: Transformers-Manual.pdf, PDF page 132, PRO COIL & FLASH LAMP LOCATIONS.

The numbered drawing places the Pro coils and flash lamps across the physical playfield, including the Megatron lock, ramp, Optimus, slingshot, Bumblebee and AllSpark assemblies. It provides the physical companion to the Q1-Q32 chart.
