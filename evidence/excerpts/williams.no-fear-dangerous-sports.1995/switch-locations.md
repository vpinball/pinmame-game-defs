### 3. Matrix names vs location-list descriptions

### Switches (p-109 matrix vs p-110 list)
Different words:
- F7: matrix `Upper Left E.O.S.` (Black-Gray J906-5) vs list `NOT USED`.
- F8: matrix `Upper Left Opto` (Black-Blue J905-5) vs list `NOT USED`.
- F2: matrix `Lower Right Opto` vs list `Lwr Right Flipper Cab.`; F4: `Lower Left Opto` vs `Lwr Left Flipper Cab.`; F6: `Upper Right Opto` vs `Upr Right Flipper Cab.` (Opto vs Cab.).
- 18: matrix `RIGHT RETRUN` vs list `Right Return` (typo only).

Abbreviation / added word only: F1 `Lower Right E.O.S.` vs `Lwr Right Flipper E.O.S.`; F3 and F5 likewise (Lower/Upper abbreviated, word Flipper added).

All other switches (11-17, 21-28, 31-38, 41-48, 51-58, 61-67) agree in wording apart from case. Matrix NOT USED cells 12, 36, 43, 44, 45, 52, 53, 65 and 68-88 agree with the list (list covers 68-88 by one line). List `Trough 1 (Right)` = matrix `TROUGH 1 (RIGHT)`.
