# No Fear: Dangerous Sports — Power Driver Board connector list

From `Williams_1995_No_Fear_Dangerous_Sports_English_Manual.pdf` (operations manual 16-50025-101, April 1995, a 144-page scan without a text layer; SHA-256 `176d5c887bf89ca35c0a6d558a757a1939da7145814f477ce099a16d50e2752b`), PDF pages 141-143 (printed 3-28 to 3-30), Power Driver Board Assembly A-12697-3 interboard wiring list. Transcribed from the 200 dpi renders by a delegated reader; the curator then checked the PDF page 142 list (G.I., flashlamp and low-power coil connectors) against the rendered page. Wire colours, abbreviations and punctuation as printed; "Key", "N/C" and "Not Used" kept as printed. Cells are kept as printed, including misspellings and abbreviations; `—` marks a cell that is blank on the page. `p-NNN` is the PDF page; the printed page number follows it.
