# No Fear: Dangerous Sports — Unit disassembly and assembly parts lists

From `Williams_1995_No_Fear_Dangerous_Sports_English_Manual.pdf` (operations manual 16-50025-101, April 1995, a 144-page scan without a text layer; SHA-256 `176d5c887bf89ca35c0a6d558a757a1939da7145814f477ce099a16d50e2752b`), PDF pages 67-73 (printed 1-47 to 1-53, unit disassembly: skull, ramps, magnets, underground trough), PDF pages 86-98 (printed 2-13 to 2-25: the flipper, slingshot, trough, popper, drop target, skull base, back panel, tilt, knocker, kicker, eject and pivot assemblies) and PDF pages 103-106 (printed 2-30 to 2-33: the upper playfield parts list, its location drawing, the lower playfield parts list and the ramps). PDF pages 99-102 (posts, unique parts, the universal power interface and the cordset chart) are not used by this record and are not transcribed.

Section 3 was transcribed by a delegated reader from Windows OCR text, with part numbers and key words partly checked against the 200 dpi renders; it is not checked line by line. Section 5 transcribes every row of every parts table on its pages: a delegated reader worked from zoomed crops of the 200 dpi renders, and the curator checked each table row by row against the rendered page. Cells, column headers and sub-headings are kept as printed, including misspellings and abbreviations; `—` marks a cell that is blank on the page. `p-NNN` is the PDF page; the printed page number follows it. Circled item callouts on the drawings are not transcribed.

## 3. Unit disassembly (p-067..p-073; OCR+, text is clean print)

p-067 (1-47): "NO FEAR Unit Disassembly for Repair  Major Component Service Instructions  REMEMBER ALWAYS TURN OFF AND UNPLUG YOUR GAME BEFORE ATTEMPTING TO REPLACE OR REPAIR ANY COMPONENTS."
p-068 (1-48) SKULL ASSEMBLY: "1) The Skull Assembly is in two pieces: the upper part is the face and the lower part is the base. The base contains the lower jaw. 2) To remove the face, unscrew the small Phillips screw located at the top middle of the skull forehead. Carefully, stretch the right side of the face and pull it off of the base first, then do the same to the left side. When re-attaching the face, snap the left side on first, then the right side. Do not stretch both sides of the face at the same time, or the eyes will pop out. 3) The four bulbs are now accessible. This assembly uses two #906 bulbs (large bulbs), and two #44 bulbs (small bulbs). 4) To remove the base from the playfield, raise the playfield and unplug the connector going to the Skull Assembly. Lower the playfield. 5) Remove the two hex head screws that hold the base to the playfield. 6) The front of the base is held down by a tab that slides into a slot in a metal bracket. Slide the base back so that the tab clears the slot. 7) Lift the base off of the playfield." Labels: FACE, PHILLIPS SCREW, FIRST SIDE OFF, LAST SIDE ON, BASE, #906 BULB, HEX HEAD SCREWS, CONNECTOR.
p-069 (1-49) RAMPS: "Left Wire Ramp 1) Remove one ESN nut by the drop hole and one hex head screw near the Hurry-up sign. 2) Unscrew two small Phillips screws from the switch. 3) Slide the ramp forward, out of the bracket holding it from the rear." "Right Wire Ramp 1) Remove one ESN nut by the drop hole and one hex head screw near the mountain. 2) Slide the ramp forward, out of the bracket holding it from the rear."
p-070 (1-50): "Ramp Assembly #1 1) Raise the playfield all the way up, and lean it against the backbox. Unplug connectors for the lamps and the switches. Lower the playfield so that it rests on the safety brackets. 2) Remove the Left Wire Ramp 3) Remove the two small Phillips screws located on the sides of the left flap. 4) Remove the four hex head screws that secure the ramp to the back panel. 5) Remove the two ESN nuts that hold the ramp to the tall posts located near the upper flipper, and remove the hex head post that holds the ramp to a short post next to the drop hole ring. 6) Carefully, slide the ramp out of the slot on the back panel and remove the ramp from the playfield." "Ramp Assembly #2 1) Raise the playfield all the way up, and lean it against the backbox. Unplug connectors for the switches. Lower the playfield so that it rests on the safety brackets. 2) Remove the Right Wire Ramp and Ramp Assembly #1 3) Remove two small Phillips screws located at the sides of the right flap. 4) Carefully, slide the ramp out of the slot on the back panel and remove the ramp from the playfield."
p-071 (1-51): "Catcher Ramp 1) Remove the two ESN nuts from the posts located on either sides of the metal Ramp Catcher. 2) Reach under the metal plate on the Catcher Ramp and find the two spring clips. Press down on the spring clips to open them. 3) Slide the Catcher Ramp off of the Right Wire Ramp." "Ramp Catcher 1) Remove the two ESN nuts from the posts located on either sides of the Ramp Catcher where it joins with the Catcher Ramp. 2) Remove the two ESN nuts from the posts located at the point where the Ramp Catcher curves. 3) Lift the ramp off the playfield."
p-072 (1-52) MAGNET ASSEMBLIES: "1) Remove Left Wire Ramp and Ramp Assembly #1. 2) Remove hex head screws on the desired Opto Mounting Bracket. 3) Slide the Opto Mounting Bracket forward, until the assembly clears the back panel. 4) Lift the magnet off of the bracket. When replacing magnet, be sure the thermal breaker faces the rear of the game. The thermal breaker is located in the bulging part of the magnet. It is a sensor that detects if the magnet gets to hot, and cuts it off if it does." Labels: RIGHT / CENTER / LEFT OPTO MOUNTING BRACKET.
p-073 (1-53) UNDERGROUND TROUGH: "1) Raise the playfield all the way up, and lean it against the backbox. 2) Unplug the connector leading to the opto plates. 3) Remove all 15 hex head screws that hold the Underground Trough to the playfield, and lift off the trough."
