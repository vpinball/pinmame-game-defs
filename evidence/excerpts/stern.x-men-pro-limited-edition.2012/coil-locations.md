# X-Men coil and flasher locations

Source: `XMenManual_042214-high-resolution.pdf`, PDF page 61, printed page 9.

The page is the complete numbered coil/flasher location drawing. It places Q1-Q32 on the playfield and back panel, including the two flipper coils, the Magneto magnet and flashers, the lock/kicker assemblies, ramp and orbit diverters, disc/Iceman motors, and the lower feature flashers. The adjacent typical-coil wiring schematic identifies the Q1 wiring pattern, diode, driver transistor, 50 VDC supply, and I/O power-driver connector.

This drawing is the physical-location authority for the main output list; the detailed chart supplies the electrical and part-number fields.
