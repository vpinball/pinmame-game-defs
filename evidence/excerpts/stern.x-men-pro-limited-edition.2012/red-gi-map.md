# X-Men Limited Edition red GI map

Source: `XMenManual_042214-high-resolution.pdf`, PDF page 109, printed GI sheet Y13.

The page is the complete `X-MEN L.E. RED GI MAP`. Its leader lines identify the red LED string across the playfield, including the upper lanes, ramps, orbit/side channels, Magneto area, center and lower playfield, and the flipper region. The retained crop is rotated for reading but preserves the original page and all leader lines.
