# X-Men switch locations

Source: `XMenManual_042214-high-resolution.pdf`, PDF page 57, printed page 5.

The page is the complete numbered playfield switch-location drawing, with the switch numbers placed beside the physical devices. It also includes the normal-open/normal-closed switch wiring diagrams, dedicated-switch schematic, diode-on-terminal-strip schematic, and the legend distinguishing above-playfield, below-playfield, and opto switch pairs.

The visible callouts locate the kickouts, trough, ramps, orbit, spinner, center lock, drop targets, Nightcrawler/Iceman assemblies, Magneto area, pop bumpers, slingshots, flippers, and the 18-22 trough group. This is the physical-location authority; the matrix page remains the address and wiring authority.
