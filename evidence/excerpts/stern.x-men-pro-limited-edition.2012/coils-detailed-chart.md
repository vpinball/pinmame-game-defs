# X-Men main coil and flasher chart

Source: `XMenManual_042214-high-resolution.pdf`, PDF page 60, printed page 8.

The page is the complete `COILS DETAILED CHART TABLE` for Q1-Q32. It identifies the trough up-kicker, auto launch, lower-left eject, Magneto magnet, left up-kicker, center-lock lift and latch, optional shaker, pops, upper-right and lower flippers, slingshots, feature flashers, disc motor, orbit diverter, Iceman motor, back-panel flashers, Magneto spotlight, bottom arch, and Magneto figure.

For every row the crop preserves the physical Q number, power-line color/connector, voltage class, driver-transistor control line, and coil or bulb type. The optional Q8 shaker and Q24 auxiliary output are visibly marked optional in the manual.
