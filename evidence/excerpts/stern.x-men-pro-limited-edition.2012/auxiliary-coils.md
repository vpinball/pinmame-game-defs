# X-Men auxiliary coil chart

Source: `XMenManual_042214-high-resolution.pdf`, PDF page 62, printed page 10.

The page identifies the LE auxiliary Q41-Q48 outputs: Q41 Wolverine magnet, Q42 left Nightcrawler up, Q43 right Nightcrawler up, Q44 white relay, Q45 red relay, Q46 blue relay, Q47 left Nightcrawler latch, and Q48 right Nightcrawler latch. It also records the VIO/BRN power-line colors, J2 connector positions, 50 VDC versus 20 VDC voltage classes, and the ORG-BRN through ORG-GRY transistor-control lines.

These outputs are LE-only evidence and must not be transferred to the Pro definition.
