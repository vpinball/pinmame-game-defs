# X-Men Limited Edition white GI map

Source: `XMenManual_042214-high-resolution.pdf`, PDF page 110, printed GI sheet Y14.

The page is the complete `X-MEN L.E. WHT GI MAP`. Its leader lines identify the white LED string and its playfield distribution. The image is retained as a cabinet/manual placement aid and is not used to invent separate PinMAME lamp addresses.
