# X-Men lamp locations

Source: `XMenManual_042214-high-resolution.pdf`, PDF page 59, printed page 7.

The page is the complete numbered lamp-location drawing. It places the callouts for the ramp, orbit, lock, Magneto, Wolverine, Iceman, bumper, target-bank, lane, and flipper-area LEDs on the playfield. The crop also preserves the diagnostic lamp-menu strip at the top of the drawing.

The numbered drawing is used for physical placement and quantity; the preceding lamp-matrix grid is used for the electrical address and label. The two sources are intentionally kept separate in the definition.
