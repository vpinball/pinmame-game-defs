# X-Men four-ball trough assembly

Source: `XMenManual_042214-high-resolution.pdf`, PDF page 3, printed page b3.

The exploded/cut-away drawing identifies the four-ball trough assembly as `500-6318-24-ND`. Switches 18, 19, and 20 are three microswitches along the trough pan. The dual opto transmitter and receiver boards at the exit carry switch 21 on the lower beam and switch 22 on the upper beam.

The manual's Ball Trough Test later names switch 22 the stacking opto. Because the two beams share one vertically stacked exit assembly, switch 22 and switch 21 have the same position in the normalized two-dimensional playfield plane; their physical distinction is vertical, outside the schema's x/y plane.
