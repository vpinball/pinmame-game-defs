Note on 84/85: the manual prints two switch part numbers per slingshot — `SW-1A-114` labeled
"(kicker)" and `SW-1A-120` labeled "(score)" — under one item number/one public address, consistent
with the standard WPC slingshot assembly carrying both a direct-fire kick switch and a separate
debounced score switch wired to a single matrix position.
