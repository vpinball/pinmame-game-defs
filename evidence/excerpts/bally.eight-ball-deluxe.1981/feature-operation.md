# Feature Operation and Scoring (Section IV)

Source: `Eight_Ball_Deluxe_OPS.pdf`, PDF pages 8-9 (printed pages 3-4), "IV. FEATURE OPERATION AND
SCORING", items A through K. Transcribed (paraphrased for length where the original text is
redundant repeated-adjustment boilerplate; scoring values and feature names are verbatim).

- **A. ABCD FEATURE**: Making A & B top rollovers lites the top arrow to alternate, scoring 25,000
  points. Making A-B-C-D rollovers drops one or two drop targets from the 7 bank (depending on
  switch #8's setting) and lights the right lane lite for 20K the 1st time, 40K the 2nd, 60K the
  3rd, and so on. A-B-C-D completion also flashes a thumper bumper for 3000 points: 1st completion
  flashes the left bumper, 2nd the right bumper, 3rd the bottom bumper; bumper flashing resets each
  ball.
- **B. SAUCER FEATURE**: Saucer scores the top-right-lane SPL award and scores 500 or 7000 points
  for each lit "ball on pool table" light, plus super bonuses of 56K and 112K timer multipliers
  (depending on switch #16's setting).
- **C. INLINE DROP & BANK SHOT TARGET FEATURE**: 1st target down scores 5000 points and lights 2X;
  2nd scores 10,000 and lights 3X; 3rd scores 15,000 and lights 4X; 4th scores 20,000 and lights 5X.
  Bank shot (the "IN LINE BACK TARGET") scores 50,000 the 1st time; the 2nd time (one replay), bank
  shot values alternate or camp on 50,000 after SPL is made (depending on switch #24's setting).
- **D. NO RECALL ON MULTIPLIERS.**
- **E. SINGLE DROP TARGET FEATURE**: Scores 500 points if no right-lane lite is lit, or scores and
  advances the lit lane value to the next higher value. Stays down until the ball is ejected
  through the saucer and returns through lane A or B.
- **F. ROLL OVER BUTTON FEATURE**: Scores 500, 10,000, 30,000, 50,000, EXTRA BALL, 70,000, SPL, then
  camps on 70,000 (or a similar sequence ending without SPL on some balls, depending on switch #14's
  setting).
- **G. 7 BANK DROP & DELUXE TARGETS FEATURE**: Knocking down targets 1-7 (the same physical bank
  aliased 9-15 for its "deluxe" pass) scores 2000 points per target and 3000 points per deluxe
  target; the single "8 ball" target flashes once the 7-bank is completed, and deluxe lamps do not
  light until the 8-ball target is hit. Once 8-ball is made, deluxe lites flash briefly and stay
  lit; knocking a deluxe lite the 1st time scores 50,000 and lights deluxe SPL, or resets the 7
  targets (depending on switch #23's setting). Switch #21 can recall/reset the deluxe lites after
  each ball; switch #22 controls whether the backbox deluxe lamps advance on any playfield deluxe
  hit or only on a deluxe-SPL hit; switch #24 also affects deluxe scoring.
- **H. OUTLANE SPECIAL FEATURE**: Once right-lane values are advanced to the top, both outlanes
  light to alternate for SPL.
- **I. 8 BALL SPL FEATURE**: Can be turned on with 56K and 112K bonus, or with 112K bonus only.
- **J. LEFT & RIGHT LANE STROBE LITES FEATURE**: When a ball rolls through a lane or hits the single
  drop target or an inline target, the lites on both lanes strobe.
- **K. BACK BOX DELUXE FEATURE**: Lighting all 6 backbox deluxe lites awards 0 to 3 replays
  (depending on self-test position 22's setting).

These feature names are the manual's own vocabulary for the machine's rules and are used as the
primary source for lamp labels that are not otherwise self-evident from the retained VPX table's
object naming or the switch/solenoid tables above.
