The printed layout puts the flipper's descriptive name (or, for the two unfitted upper flippers, "NOT USED") in the Function column of the *first* row of each Power/Hold pair for the "NOT USED" case (33, 35) but on the *second* row for the fitted lower flippers (30, 32) — i.e. "NOT USED" prints on the Power row while the flipper name prints on the Hold row in every one of the four pairs; the Power row's Function cell is otherwise blank when the flipper is fitted.
