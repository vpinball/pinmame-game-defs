## Opto-constructed switches (LED + photo-transistor pair)

Every one of these rows prints two part numbers stacked on two lines: an `(LED)` emitter part and a `(PHOTO TRANS)` receiver part, rather than a single mechanical switch part number. These are the only opto-constructed switches on this page:

- 31 — A-18617-1 (LED) / A-18618-1 (PHOTO TRANS) — trough eject sensor
- 32 — A-18617-1 (LED) / A-18618-1 (PHOTO TRANS) — Trough Ball 1
- 33 — A-18617-1 (LED) / A-18618-1 (PHOTO TRANS) — Trough Ball 2
- 34 — A-18617-1 (LED) / A-18618-1 (PHOTO TRANS) — Trough Ball 3
- 35 — A-18617-1 (LED) / A-18618-1 (PHOTO TRANS) — Trough Ball 4
- 36 — A-16908 (LED) / A-16909 (PHOTO TRANS) — Lock Up 2
- 37 — A-16908 (LED) / A-16909 (PHOTO TRANS) — Lock Up 1
- 41 — A-16908 (LED) / A-16909 (PHOTO TRANS) — Past Spinner
- 42 — A-16908 (LED) / A-16909 (PHOTO TRANS) — In The Sewer
- 43 — A-16908 (LED) / A-16909 (PHOTO TRANS) — Lock Jam
- 44 — A-16908 (LED) / A-16909 (PHOTO TRANS) — Past Crane

All other switches on the page (flippers, cabinet switches, outlanes/return lanes, crane top switches 15/38, ramp exit, car targets, slingshots, 3-banks, bowl/ramp/scoop/dog-entry rollovers) print a single conventional switch part number (or `SW-1A-xxx` leaf-switch part, or a `5647-12693-xx` rollover/leaf part) and show no opto construction.

## Diagram

The right two-thirds of the page carries a full-page top-down line drawing of the Junk Yard playfield (crane track across the top, spinner/lock/sewer/scoop cluster in the upper-center, jet-bumper-free open lower playfield, twin ball trough and shooter lane at the bottom under a labelled "CABINET" block). Numbered circles are scattered around the drawing's left and right margins, each with a straight leader line running in to a specific drawn playfield feature; the circled numbers correspond directly to the Item No. column above (i.e. circle "44" leads to the physical location of switch 44 "Past Crane", circle "18" leads to the shooter lane, etc.), so the diagram is the spatial key for this table rather than an independent parts list.

Approximate correspondence by playfield region, reading the callouts top-to-bottom on each margin:

- **Top / crane track:** circles for 44 (Past Crane), 28 (Crane Down), 38 (Top Right Crane), 63/62/61 (Upper Right 3-Bank top/middle/bottom), 68 (Bowl Exit), 45 (Ramp Exit), 15 (Top Left Crane) lead into the curved crane-track area spanning the top of the playfield.
- **Upper-center (toaster/gun, spinner, lock, sewer, scoop):** circles for 65/64 (Upper Left 3-Bank), 72 (Scoop Down), 11 (Toaster Gun), 74 (Dog Entry), 73 (Scoop Made), 43 (Lock Jam), 37 (Lock Up 1), 42 (In The Sewer), 41 (Past Spinner), 36 (Lock Up 2), F5 (Spinner) lead into the central cluster of ramps/scoop/lock/sewer/spinner features right of playfield center.
- **Left/right 3-bank standup clusters (lower playfield):** circles 58/57/56 (Lower Left 3-Bank top/middle/bottom) and 78/77/76 (Right 3-Bank top/middle/bottom) lead to the two standup-target banks flanking the lower playfield.
- **Outlanes/return lanes/slingshots:** circles 17/16 (Left Return Lane / Left Outlane) and 27 (Right Outlane) lead to the outer lower lanes; circles 51 (Left Slingshot) and 52 (Right Slingshot) lead to the two slingshot kickers just above the flippers.
- **Cabinet / bottom of playfield:** circles 14 (Plumb Bob), 21 (Slam Tilt), 13 (Start Button) lead to the cabinet block labelled "CABINET" at the bottom-left; circles 31/32/33/34/35 (trough eject + Trough Ball 1-4) and 18 (Shooter Lane) lead to the ball trough and shooter-lane channel drawn at the bottom-right, next to the same cabinet block.
- Item 12 (Rebound Switch) is printed with quantity `(2)`, and the diagram appears to show two separate leader circles both labelled 12, consistent with two physically separate rebound-switch locations sharing one logical switch item.

Note: at 200 dpi several of the closely-spaced circled numbers in the crane/3-bank clusters (e.g. distinguishing 65 vs 66, or 61 vs 62) were not reliably legible; the region-level correspondence above is confident, but exact circle-to-single-switch assignment within the 3-bank clusters should be treated as approximate pending a higher-resolution re-render.

## Uncertainty / unreadable cells

- **Item 31 description**: transcribed as printed, reading `Tough Eject` (no visible "r" between "T" and "ough"). This is very likely a manual typo for "Trough Eject" given items 32-35 immediately below are "Trough Ball 1-4" on the same opto part pair, and the physical device is the trough-eject kicker/opto paired with the ball-count optos — but attempts to re-render a zoomed crop to confirm the exact glyph were blocked by tooling/sandbox restrictions in this session, so the literal reading above is the best-effort transcription and should be re-verified against a sharper scan before being treated as authoritative.
- **Item 51 second line**: printed with what reads as an extra space, `A-17794 ( SCORE)`, versus item 52's `A-17794 (SCORE)` with no space. Transcribed literally as seen; could be a scan/print artifact rather than a genuine typographic difference.
- All other cells were legible at the rendered resolution with no ambiguity.
