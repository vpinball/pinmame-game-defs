# Starship Troopers — switch and lamp location drawings

Read from `Sega_1997_Starship_Troopers_Manual.pdf` (IPDB 4341), PDF page 7, the front-matter drawing printed `DR. 5`:
"SWITCH MATRIX GRID LOCATIONS" (left playfield) and "LAMP MATRIX GRID LOCATIONS" (right playfield), read from a 400 dpi
render. The crop `switch-lamp-locations.webp` renders the two playfield drawings. Both playfields are drawn upright,
rear at the top and the flippers at the bottom. Legend under the drawings: "= Switches/Lamps mounted above playfield."
(white box) and "= Switches/Lamps mounted below the playfield." (black box). The scan is binarized, so a box drawn on a
black area carries a white outline; the colour given is the box's fill.

Switch drawing, read box by box:

- Top left: 47 (black) beside the left orbit, under the four upper-left flash domes; 25 (white) at the left ramp
  entrance below them.
- Top centre: 9 (black) above the rear end of the warrior bug track; 41, 42 and 43 (black) left to right in the three
  top lanes.
- Top right: 48 (black) in the right orbit beside the four upper-right flash domes; 52 (white) at the right ramp
  entrance under it.
- Warrior bug area, upper left: 33 (white) at the rear of the track, 10 (black) beside it to the left, and 35 and 34
  (white) side by side at the front end of the track.
- Pop bumpers: 49 (black) at the top bumper, 51 (black) at the right bumper, 50 (black) at the bottom bumper; 26
  (white) between the top and right bumpers; 40 (black) right of 50.
- Between the bug track and the bumpers: 29 and 28 (black) stacked left of the top bumper; 30 and 31 (black) left of the super VUK; 46 (black)
  at the super VUK; 32 (black) right of it on the right ramp side.
- Left side: 27 (black) at the left ramp side; 20, 19, 18 and 17 (black) top to bottom along the left side; 45 (black)
  at the VUK below them.
- Centre: 38 (black) on the brain bug, 39 (black) left of it, 37 (black) to its lower right.
- Right side: 53 (white) at the right ramp exit, above the right flash domes; 24, 23, 22 and 21 (black) top to bottom
  along the right side.
- Lower playfield: 59 (black) at the left slingshot; 57 and 58 (black) at the left outlane and return lane; 62 (black)
  at the right slingshot; 61 and 60 (black) at the right return lane and outlane.
- Trough, lower right: 12, 13, 14 and 15 (black) stepping up toward the shooter lane; 16 (black) in the shooter lane.
- The four 7-segment displays are drawn at the centre above the flippers, showing "5 E / 6 A".

Lamp drawing, read box by box:

- Top: 25, 26 and 27 (black) in the B-U-G letters inside the round bug insert at the top centre; 34 (white) at the
  head of the warrior bug track; 47 (white) on the left ramp; 48 (white) on the right ramp.
- Bumpers: 28 (white) on the left bumper, 30 (white) on the right bumper, 29 (white) on the bottom bumper; 35 and 36
  (white) side by side below 29, with 39 (white) under them.
- Centre: 43 and 42 (black) above the left ramp, 32 (black) beside "NUKE", 31 (black) left of 44 and 45 (black); 22
  (black) by the "STARSHIP" sign and 41 and 21 (black) by "JACKPOT" on the left ramp; 24 (black) by "TROOPERS" and 23
  (black) by "JACKPOT" on the right ramp; 46 (black) to their right.
- Left: 18 and 17 (black) along the left side; 12, 11, 10 and 9 (black) top to bottom down the left stand-up bank; 56
  (black) below them; 64 (black) right of 56.
- Right: 20 and 19 (black) along the right side; 16, 15, 14 and 13 (black) top to bottom down the right stand-up bank.
- Lower centre: 8 (black) on the "STARSHIP" hotdog, 72 (black) on the "TROOPERS" hotdog, 6 (black) in the round
  "KLENDATHU" insert between them, 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 (black) in the planet inserts below, left to right; 7 (black) on
  "BALL SAVER" below the displays; 80 (black) between the flippers.
- Lanes: 37 (black) at the left outlane and 40 (black) at the left return lane; 40 (black) again at the right return
  lane and 38 (black) at the right outlane.
- The four displays are drawn at the centre labelled WARRIOR (top left), HOPPER (top right), PLASMA… (bottom left,
  partly hidden) and TANKER (bottom right). Lines lead from each to an enlarged segment key drawn over the lower
  playfield: 49-55 for WARRIOR, 57-63 for HOPPER, 65-71 for the bottom-left display and 73-79 for TANKER, each with
  segment boxes in the A-G positions of the "TYPICAL 7-SEGMENT ADDRESS" key (A top, B upper right, C lower right, D
  bottom, E lower left, F upper left, G middle).

Under the drawings: "NOTE: This Game features a 4-Digit 7-Segment Display (located over the Flippers). Each segment is
wired into the Lamp Matrix Grid (see previous page)." The switch notes: "All Switches require diodes. Some diodes are
located on Terminal Strips (under playfield) & not on the switch itself." The lamp notes: "All Lamps require diodes.
Some diodes are located on Terminal Strips (under playfield) & not on the lamp itself."
