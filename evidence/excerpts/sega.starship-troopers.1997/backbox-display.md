## Backbox layout locations

The page draws the CPU/Sound Board, the I/O Power Driver Board and the Display Power Supply Bd. as mounted in the
backbox, and a cut-away of the cabinet. Its note beside the cut-away, verbatim: "The Display Controller Board (holds the
Display ROM Loc: ROM0) is positioned behind the 128 X 32 Dot Matrix Display Board (both boards do not have fuses)."
Its fuse chart heads F1 "LOC: DISPLAY POWER SUPPLY (P.S.) BOARD", "3/4A 250v S.B., 90V DC, High Voltage Display", under
"Backbox Fuses".
