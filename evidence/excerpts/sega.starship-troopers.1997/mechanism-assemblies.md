## Opto boards (Section 5, Chapter 4)

Trough Up-Kicker OPTO boards, printed page 95 (PDF page 109): transmitter 520-5124-00, receiver 520-5125-00. Theory of
operation: with light on the receiver, Q1 is off, so Q2 is off and "acts as an OPEN SWITCH"; when the light is
interrupted (BLOCKED), Q2 conducts and "acts as a CLOSED SWITCH".

Playfield Sw. OPTO "Long-Hop" Boards, printed page 97 (PDF page 111): transmitter 520-5082-00, receiver 520-5083-01.

- Theory text, verbatim: "The light falling on LED (CR1) generates a voltage which is applied to the input (Pin-2) of
  the LM311 Comparator (U1). R1 bleeds off excess charge. At about a volt input from LED (CR1) the Comparator (U1)
  trips & drives either Q1 (during switch line strobes) or the indicator LED (CR2) (in between strobes). If a switch
  line is being strobed, the emitter of Q1 drops to the saturation voltage of the Switch Line Driver, about .3 volts.
  This plus the .7 volt drop on the base give a 1v forward bias voltage to Q1, which is lower than the 1.7v drop on
  LED (CR2) so the current flows through the Transistor during strobes. This drives Q1 on and makes the switch. If
  the strobe line is high, then the 1.7v path through LED (CR2) is lower than Q1's bias voltage so current flows
  through LED (CR2) and the indicator lights. D1 prevents reverse bleed, R2 and R3 form the voltage divider for the
  trip point, R4 is a current limiter for both Q1 and CR2, C1 and C2 are general noise-filter caps."
- Schematic: CR1 MT5000UR to U1 LM311 pin 2 (−); R2 330K Ω and R3 47K Ω divide +5v onto pin 3 (+); R4 220K Ω; Q1
  2N3904; D1 1N914; CR2 RED T1-3/4 DIFF. LED. Receiver CN1: pin 1 RED (+5V), pin 2 WHT/XXX (Switch Row/Return +), pin
  3 GRN/XXX (Switch Col/Drive −), pin 4 BLK (Ground). Transmitter CN1: pin 1 RED (+5V), pin 2 BLK (GROUND), through
  R1 180 Ω to CR1.
- Parts table, "Playfield Switch OPTO "Long-Hop" Boards Component Layout & Parts", every row as printed. The ITEM

## Ball paths (Section 2, Chapter 2, Playfield Overview)

Printed pages 6-10 (PDF pages 20-24), quoted from the callouts. The lower part of PDF pages 20 and 22 does not decode
(Poppler reports bad CCITTFax codes), so those two callouts end mid-sentence in the source.

- Page 6, Left Orbit: "A Left Orbit Shot with the Left Magnet off will continue around completing the Orbit Shot."
  Right Orbit: "A Right Orbit Shot with the Right Magnet off will continue around completing the Orbit Shot." VUK:
  "Shoot either the VUK or the Super VUK." Shooter Lane Ramp: "The shot from the Shooter Lane will plunge the ball into
  the Right Orbit. Be Careful! A weak Plunger Shot not strong" (the rest does not decode).
- Page 7: "A Hard Left Orbit Shot with the Left Magnet on will deflect the ball down into the Top Exit Hole, bringing
  the ball into the Under-Trough to the super VUK. A Soft Left Orbit Shot with the Left Magnet off can also enter this
  Exit Hole or the Top Lanes." "Each hit to the Warrior Bug will push it back until the ball can enter the Bug Exit
  Hole to the Undertrough advancing the ball to the Super VUK getting shot out towards the right flippers." "A Hard
  Right Orbit Shot with the Right Magnet on will deflect the ball down into the Top Lanes, bringing the ball into the
  Pop Bumper area. A Soft Right Orbit Shot can bring the ball into the Top Lanes or the Exit Hole. The ball can enter
  the Pop Exit Hole and drop into the Mini-Under-Trough advancing to the Super VUK getting shot out towards the right
  flippers."
- Page 8: the Plastic Ramp "with Retrieval & Tac Ships, 3 each 4X Light Assemblies"; "Wire Ramp, 4X Light Assy. (over
  Wire Ramp), Pulse Cannons (over the Return Lanes), Hopper Bug (over VUK), Tanker Bug and Light Sign over Super VUK and
  the Bottom Arch." Right Ramp: "A Hard Shot to the Right Ramp will exit to the Right Return Lane. A Soft Shot may exit"
  (the rest does not decode).
- Page 9: "A Hard Shot to the Left Ramp will exit via the Wire Ramp exiting to the Left Return Lane. A Soft Shot to the
  Left Ramp will bring the ball down into the Top Exit Hole, bringing the ball into the Under-Trough to the Super VUK
  and will get kicked out into play towards the Right Flippers." Brain Bug: "If that Brain Bug Pops up from under the
  playfield, hit it with the ball!"
- Page 10 shows the bottom of the playfield with the bottom-mounted assemblies, listed as the 4-Ball Trough,
  Autoplunger, Left & Right Flippers, Mini-Right Flipper, Slingshots, VUK, Brain Bug, Super VUK, Plastic Trough, Metal
  Mini-Trough, Stand-Up Targets, the 7-Segment LED pcb and the bottom-mounted switches, and carries the label "Large
  Plastic & Mini-Under-Trough Ball Shot Paths".
