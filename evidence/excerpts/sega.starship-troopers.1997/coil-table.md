# Starship Troopers — Coils Detailed Chart Table

Read from `Sega_1997_Starship_Troopers_Manual.pdf` (IPDB 4341), PDF page 8, the front-matter drawing printed `DR. 6`
("COILS DETAILED CHART TABLE"). The page is a scan with no text layer; every cell was read from a 400 dpi render and
checked against a Windows OCR pass. The `Driver Ouput Board` column prints `I/O Pwr. Drvr.` on every row and is
omitted. The last column prints the coil gauge-turns over the coil part number, or the bulb type over its part number;
both are joined with ` / ` here. `✻n` is the printed bulb count. Headings keep the page's spellings (`Ouput`,
`Connnection`). "DOTS" is the vertical "Diode On Terminal Strip" tag printed beside a name; the legend above the flash
group reads "Diode On Terminal Strip (if noted)".
