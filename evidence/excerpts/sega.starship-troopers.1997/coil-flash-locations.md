# Starship Troopers — coil and flash lamp location drawings

Read from `Sega_1997_Starship_Troopers_Manual.pdf` (IPDB 4341), PDF page 9, the front-matter drawing printed `DR. 7`,
coil locations (left playfield) and flash lamp locations (right playfield), from a 300 dpi render. The crop
`coil-flash-locations.webp` renders both, rear at the top. Legend: "Legend Note: = Coils/Flashes mounted above
playfield." (white box) and "= Coils/Flashes mounted below the playfield." (black box). The scan is binarized, so a box
drawn on a black area carries a white outline; the colour given is the box's fill.

Coil drawing:

- 5 and 6 (black) side by side at the top centre, each on a round black shape at the rear edge of the playfield.
- 20, 19, 18 and 17 (white) stacked top to bottom on the warrior bug track, upper left.
- 9 (black) at the top bumper, 11 (black) at the right bumper, 10 (black) at the bottom bumper; 4 (black) below the
  bottom bumper.
- 7 (black) on a large black assembly at the centre of the playfield.
- 3 (black) at the left side, at mid height.
- 12 (black) at the left slingshot and 13 (black) at the right slingshot.
- 14 (black) on an assembly right of centre above the trough; 15 (black) at the lower left and 16 (black) at the lower
  centre, on the flipper assemblies.
- 1 (black) on the long trough assembly at the lower right; 2 (black) at the shooter lane, right edge.
- The four 7-segment displays are drawn at the centre, showing "5 E / 6 A".

Flash lamp drawing:

- Top left: F1, F3, F2 and F4 (white) on four domes down the upper-left corner, F1 highest. Top right: F1, F3, F2 and
  F4 (white) on four domes along the top right, F1 leftmost.
- Left side at mid height: F1, F3, F2 and F4 (white) on four domes, top to bottom. Right side at mid height: F1, F3, F2
  and F4 (white) on four domes, top to bottom.
- F5 (black) in a white circle with a bulb symbol at the head of the warrior bug track; F5 (white) three times on the
  track itself, one above and two side by side below it.
- F6 (black) three times in white circles with bulb symbols down the left ramp, and once more in a circle at the lower
  left of the centre inserts.
- F7 (black) three times in white circles with bulb symbols down the right side of the upper playfield, and once more in
  a circle at the lower right of the centre inserts.
- F8 (black) twice at the pop bumpers, in white circles.
- 23 (black) twice in white circles with bulb symbols, either side of the brain bug picture at the centre.

Under the legend: "TYPICAL COIL WIRING"; "Note: All Coils require diodes. Some diodes are located on Terminal Strips
(under playfield) & not on the coil itself."
