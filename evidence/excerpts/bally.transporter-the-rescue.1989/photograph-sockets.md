The reads below are physical twist-lock socket-base axes, not insert-art or glow-light centres. Photo top is apron/front and bottom is rear; the upper-right flipper and unequal jet layout establish x orientation.

Six independently read controls are the two lower flipper shafts, upper-right flipper shaft and three under-jet support-ring centres. The portable seed records all pixels and exact retained VPX control centres. An affine least-squares fit reverses the photo y direction and has RMS 0.006249 normalized; socket results are rounded to four decimals. The socket board lies inside the control convex hull. These placements remain observed: component depth, camera perspective and approximately 25-pixel control uncertainty prevent validated promotion. Many other sockets are hidden by wire looms; neither this board nor this photograph proves exhaustive lamp or GI fitment.
