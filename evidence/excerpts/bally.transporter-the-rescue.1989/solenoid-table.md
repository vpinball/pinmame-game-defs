Printed notes summarize: [1] wire colors except flipper Orn-Vio and Orn-Gry are ground connections to the coil terminal with unbanded diode end; those two flipper wires connect CPU board to flipper switch. [2] Braced flipper connections are from switch to coil. [3] A circuits pulse when solenoid 12 is de-energized and C circuits when energized; bracketed wire colors correspond to the J1 terminal on the auxiliary power driver board. [4a] Relay on playfield, p/n C-11998-1. [4b] Relay on insert board, p/n C-11998-1. [5] Relay on auxiliary power driver board p/n D-12247 in backbox.
