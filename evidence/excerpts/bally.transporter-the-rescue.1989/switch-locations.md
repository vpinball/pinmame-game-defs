# Factory switch locations

Manual PDF 54, printed 2-5. Method: independent pixel reading, followed by primary curator visual verification of the native page and corrections documented in `tools/seeds/bally/transporter-the-rescue-1989-callouts.json`. The crop contains the complete playfield drawing, excluding page margins. Coordinates were not used to invent pixel reads.

The drawing names the three jet switches 60-62, individual drop targets 19-24, top lanes 25-27, standup 16, ramp 17, popper 15, locks 33/34 and the lower lanes. Two leaders at each of 63 and 64 mark the paired sling-actuating switches. Labels 57/58 appear by flippers but the parts table establishes their physical optotransistors on the backbox board; those positions are not used as switch coordinates. Free-standing trough labels 10-13 do not mark individual contacts and do not validate their table positions.

Each page has its own five independently read control parts: three bumper-cap centres and two lower flipper pivots. Fourteen table switches are checked one-to-one against their own labelled physical part. The popper and the paired sling sensors use disclosed drawing measurements, and are not checked against this same drawing. Source pixel centres, control matches, transforms, residuals and excluded readings are retained in the seed and generated report.
