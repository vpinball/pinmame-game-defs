# Factory lamp locations

Manual PDF 56, printed 2-6. Method: independent native-image reading, visually verified by the primary curator. The crop preserves the full playfield lamp-location drawing.

Numbers 1-17 and 19-40 name the playfield lamp regions. The drawing uses round, triangular, arrow and rectangular insert outlines; it does not draw a distinct socket body behind each outline. In particular 13/14 are one rectangle each and 40 one arrow, while the lamp matrix marks each as two lamps. One symbol cannot establish two bulb centres. Labels 15/16/28/30/36 lie among overlaid ramp/plastic linework and supply no individual socket endpoint. These limitations are retained rather than resolving them from glow-light names.

The seed records all independently read symbols and a separate five-control fit on bumper caps and flipper pivots. This fit corroborates side, region and order; it does not convert art-light centres into observed physical sockets. All script-bound insert anchors therefore remain candidate placements. Backbox lamps 41-64 are established separately by the matrix and wiring; no playfield position is inferred for them.
