The source identifies the outhole, feeder, two drop reset banks, both Orion lock kickers, popper, gate, kickback, jets and slings. The switched labels retain A/C suffixes; they are not themselves public solenoid numbers. A1-A8 correspond to public 1-8 and C1-C8 to public 25-32 through the game's select relay at 12.

03C indicates the two top-left flashers at the rear rail; their individual centres come from the two modelled domes, not the single leader. The raw model y values just behind the rail are explicitly projected to the rear playfield boundary. 04C has two jet flashers. 01C/08C have leaders to their own 2X/3X lamp symbols; those factory symbols provide observed drawing measurements, not coordinates copied from glow lights. The 05C leader ends beside the right mechanism without marking one socket, and the 06C/07C regions lack unambiguous individual bulb centres in the model. Their physical fitment and quantity are known, while public flashers 29/30/31 retain omitted spatial records.
