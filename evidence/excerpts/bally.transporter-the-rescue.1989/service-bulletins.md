## Service Bulletin Book, PDF 29, printed 26: SB11, August 8, 1989

The bulletin names TRANSPORTER, ROM UPDATE, and identifies the latest game ROMs in U26/U27 as LA3. Its three changes are kickback grace time from 7 to 10 seconds; the Orion locked-ball search kick order changed to right lane, left lane, right lane; and initial ball search changed from 30 to 15 seconds, then 10 seconds for kicking locked balls. These are factory rule/timing changes, not calibrated ball-physics values.

## Service Bulletin Book, PDF 34, printed 31: SB13, August 21, 1989

The bulletin names TRANSPORTER and BACK BOX BOLT. It instructs removal of J-14 from the backbox interconnect PCB D-12313 to fit the left backbox bolt, followed by reconnection. It describes a service-access issue and changes no playfield device identity.
