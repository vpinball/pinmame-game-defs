# Medieval Madness manual proof

The crops cited beside these notes retain the printed source regions. They are deliberately narrower than the source PDFs and contain only the tables, drawings or test procedures used by the definition.

## Operations manual, Part I

- PDF page 2 is the front service insert. It prints the complete solenoid/flasher table, five general-illumination strings, four flipper winding pairs, the drawbridge motor and the castle/tower motor circuits, including driver transistors, board connectors, wire colors, voltages and coil or motor part numbers.
- PDF page 49 (printed 1-20) contains the Loop/Gate Test. It describes the left and right loop gates, their associated optos and the gate drive behavior.
- PDF page 50 (printed 1-21) contains the Tower Test and starts the Trolls Test. It identifies the tower entrance switch, the tower lock post, both troll targets and their up/down behavior.
- PDF page 51 (printed 1-22) contains the Drawbridge Test and Castle Gate Test. It describes the drawbridge motor and position switches and the castle gate motor, gate and lock switches.
- PDF page 52 continues the castle-gate procedure and contains the remainder of the Trolls Test.
- PDF page 53 (printed 1-24) finishes the Trolls Test and contains the Empty Balls Test used to exercise the trough, ejects and other ball-storage mechanisms.

## Operations manual, Part II — parts and locations

- PDF page 48 (printed 2-47) is the complete lamp-location drawing.
- PDF page 50 (printed 2-49) is the complete switch-location drawing.
- PDF page 52 (printed 2-51) is the complete solenoid/flashlamp-location drawing.
- PDF page 53 (printed 2-52) is the complete lamp matrix.
- PDF page 54 (printed 2-53) is the complete switch matrix.
- PDF page 55 (printed 2-54) is the complete solenoid/flasher table, including general illumination, flippers and both motor circuits.

## Operations manual, Part III — wiring and schematics

- PDF page 2 (printed 3-2) combines the switch matrix with its CPU-board circuit.
- PDF page 6 (printed 3-6) traces the controlled-coil wiring from the power-driver board to playfield loads.
- PDF page 10 (printed 3-10) documents the general-illumination relay and block circuits.
- PDF page 11 (printed 3-11) documents all four Fliptronic flipper circuits and the cabinet-button/EOS inputs.
