Printed flipper-circuit numbers 29-32 are not public addresses; PinMAME's `core_getSol` reads printed
29-32 as WPC J111 general-purpose GPIO/fast-flip virtual channels and only serves the real
lower-flipper coils at public 45-48. Printed 33-36 pass straight through as public 33-36 — no
translation needed for the diverter/magnet devices, since `hw.flippers` carries no
`FLIP_SOL(FLIP_UR)`/`FLIP_SOL(FLIP_UL)` bit for this machine (it has no upper flippers).
