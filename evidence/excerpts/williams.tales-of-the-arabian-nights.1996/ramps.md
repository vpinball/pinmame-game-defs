2-42, the Ramps assembly parts page that fixes the physical construction of the four ramp
mechanisms. The retained PDF carries a genuine OCR text layer, but the layout extraction badly
garbles multi-column tables, so this was re-verified against the 300 dpi rendered page image, which
is the source of record.
