## What this page settles and what it leaves open

Settled: lamps 33-39 (`#44 Red`) and 78-80 (`#44 Green`) are back-panel devices, not
playfield devices, and the ten clear `#44` sockets on the same panel are general
illumination rather than matrix lamps -- which is what the Lamp Locations page's own note
(`THE TOP TEN CLEAR BULBS ARE G.I.s; NOT CONTROL LAMPS`) says independently.

Left open: this page lists two `#89` flasher sockets on the back panel and labels both
`Q22 FLASH`, while the Coil & Flash Lamp Locations page draws one `22` and one `30` in its
own back-panel inset. The two pages therefore disagree about whether the back panel
carries a third flasher bulb driven by `Q30`.
