# Coils Detailed Chart Table

Transcribed from `Stern_2006_Pirates_of_the_Caribbean_Pinball_Service_Game_Manual.pdf`,
PDF page 10, printed "DR. 8". Header reads `COILS DETAILED CHART TABLE`. The whole region
is transcribed, including every `NOT USED` row and every blank voltage/power-line cell,
because a blank power line plus a populated drive transistor is what distinguishes an
enumerated-but-unfitted circuit from a fitted one.

Every group is labelled `I/O Power Driver` in the `Driver Output Board` column.

## Notes printed at the foot of the same page

```
Note:  In Test Flash Lamps Menu ("Flash" Icon), Flashers tested are all Flash Lamps located
between Q1-Q32 (This Game: Q20, Q22, Q30, Q31 & Q32).  -00B or -00T can be used for coil
replacements, but the diode must be removed.  Call for more info.

Coil Note: (rev) -ND means 'No Diode'.
```

## Observations about the printed table itself

- The table stops at #32. Stern's S.A.M. main I/O power driver board carries exactly 32
  drive transistors Q1-Q32, and this machine's manual prints no auxiliary driver-board
  table at all.
- The two flipper circuits are printed as single rows, `#15 LEFT FLIPPER` and
  `#16 RIGHT FLIPPER`. There is no separate power/hold pair, and no upper-flipper row.
- The `(rev)` glyph transcribed above is the manual's own circular-arrow symbol in the
  `Coil GA-Turn or Bulb Type` column, defined by the `Coil Note` as meaning that the
  `-ND` suffix is a no-diode part.
- `#7`, `#8`, `#12`, `#13`, `#14` and `#17` print `NOT USED` with a populated drive
  transistor and a populated control-line wire colour and connector pin, but with a blank
  power line colour, blank power voltage and blank coil part. The blank power line and
  blank coil part are what show nothing is fitted.
