  split.
- The flipper part number "FL 23/600-30/2600-50VDC" is printed wrapped over two lines ("FL 23/600-" /
  "30/2600-50VDC") in each flipper row.
- Items 11-14 print part number "5580-09555-00" with the footnote asterisk; the Solenoid Table on
  printed page 27 prints "5580-09555-01" for the same relays (solenoids 11-14, and note 4).
