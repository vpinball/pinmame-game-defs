Layout notes: the triangle appears on lamps 28-32, 36-40, 44-48, 52-56 and 60-64 (rows 4-8 of
columns 4-8, twenty-five cells) and nowhere else. The boxed 2 appears only at lamp 1 (Game Over
(Backbox)). In columns 4-8 the "(Top)" qualifier is printed only on row 4 and the "(Bottom)"
qualifier only on row 8; rows 5-7 print the colour and digit only.

Printed text on the same page: "To locate the wiring associated with a particular feature lamp, refer
to the Lamp-Matrix Table. CPU Board connections at jacks 1J6 (columns) and 1J7 (rows) are also listed
in the table." The table's own headers print the column connector as 1J7 and the row connector as
1J6; the prose is quoted as printed and disagrees with the headers on which jack carries columns and
rows.

## Comparison with the foldout copy

The unnumbered foldout, PDF page 71, prints this table (upper) above the Switch-Matrix Table. It was
read cell by cell against this transcription: all 64 cell names and numbers, all column headers
(transistor, wire, pin), all row headers (transistor, wire, pin), the boxed 2 at lamp 1, the
triangles on the same 25 cells, and the legend text agree with the numbered page. No differences.

Normalization: the boxed 2 and the triangles are written as "(boxed 2)" and "(triangle)".
