## Comparison with the foldout copy

The unnumbered foldout, PDF page 71, prints the PIN-BOT Lamp-Matrix Table above the PIN-BOT
Switch-Matrix Table. The lower (switch) table was read cell by cell against this transcription: all
64 cell names and numbers, all eight column headers (transistor, wire, pin) and all eight row headers
agree with the numbered page. The foldout copy carries hand-drawn pen marks (circles around the cells
near 17/18/25/26, 3/11/19/27 and 37, a strike-through on "Right" of "Right Coin Chute 4" and on
"Tilt" of "Ball Roll Tilt 2", small ticks beside 49, 52 and 55, and a smudge over the "4" of the
column-4 header); these are scribbles on the scan and change no printed cell.

Normalization: none beyond writing each printed cell as "name number" in row-major order.
