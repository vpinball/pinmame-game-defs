## Projection policy

- **cellar:** Vertical projection of the bound collision geometry into the main player's plan view; local cellar playing direction is reversed. No Z coordinate or second coordinate space is invented.
- **actuators:** K relay effect at its own cellar up-kicker; drop resets at their own bank's first target; main hole/kicker effects at their own sensor geometry. Trap-door effect at its closed plate/linkage world-mesh bounds centre; its mesh coordinate frame is checked against four independently placed bumper objects. These are mechanism anchors, not observed winding centres.
- **troughs:** Bound below-playfield ball-path triggers are observed projections, not an entrance-hole measurement.
