# Class of 1812 (Gottlieb, 1991) spatial review

Status: observed. The machine record stays `partial` at `machines/partial/gottlieb/class-of-1812-1991.json`; its spatial dimension stays open because of the 8 spatial blockers below.

The geometry source is the retained `Class of 1812  - NZ&TT 1.0.vpx` by tom tower & ninuzzu, SHA-256 `27e1842e63714b2507aa828c1f8751a0324f756259b453ddc8c8d95583cc1500`. Its embedded script (SHA-256 `ff98cc7740b1b81cedf0322090034879fc66ce8906759f8d5e897c00e1ac1914`) is the pinned known-working script before a 2018 patch that changed no playfield binding. Bounds are `left=0 top=0 right=952 bottom=1974`; every coordinate is x/952 and y/1974, rounded to at most six places. `evidence/excerpts/gottlieb.class-of-1812.1991/vpx-table-objects.md` lists each placed object, its coordinate and the script lines that bind it, and the curator's `verify_retained_table` re-derives every coordinate from the retained extraction and OBJ export.

## Evidence decisions

- A placement is the table object the script binds to the address: a trigger, target, spinner, kicker, bumper or slingshot wall for a switch, the bulb primitive the script draws a lamp on, the light under each flasher dome, the object a coil moves or kicks from, and each member of the GI collection `TiltRelay` switches that shows a bulb mesh. A matrix lamp without a bulb primitive is projected onto the image-mode `l<n>` insert overlay `InitLamps` binds to it.
- Switch 44 is placed at Kicker `Slot2`, the trough's second position, not at `Slot1` where the known-working script binds it: the ROM's trough probes show 44 closes only while both balls are home, and the table's 3.0.21 update binds it to `Slot2`.
- Render lights that show no bulb mesh and sit under no flasher dome (four G.I. lights and `F24`) make no socket and are not placed.
- Primitives whose stored position is not the part's centre (the slingshot arms, the heart, the teeth, the Bat-O-Meter bulbs and the billboard and crypt bulbs) are placed at the centre of their world-space mesh from `vpxtool export obj --units vpu`.
- The heart and teeth meshes centre behind the rear edge; IPDB photos 12 and 13 show both toys against the back panel, so their placements are clamped to y = 0 and the raw centre is disclosed in each device note.
- Coils with no table object of their own are documented projections onto their own mechanism's object; a bank reset lands on the mean of its four targets.
- Backbox and cabinet devices take controlled `not_applicable` records: the A and Q relays, the knocker, the coin meter, the cabinet and flipper-button switches, the test inputs and the two displays.
- No placement is validated: no factory drawing is retained to check it, and only one table lineage is.

## Blockers

- No factory drawing is retained: IPDB lists the English manual without hosting it (the file is a 0-byte placeholder) and no other attributable copy was found, so no placement can be checked against the machine's own switch, lamp or coil location drawings.
- Only one table lineage is retained: the NZ&TT 1.0 table by tom tower & ninuzzu, whose pinned known-working script is the same table's, and the 3.0.21 script is a later update of it. No independently built geometry confirms a placement, so every placement stays observed.
- The heart (solenoids 11 and 25) and the teeth (solenoid 12 and lamp 2) are table meshes centred behind the playfield's rear edge; IPDB photos 12 and 13 show both toys against the back panel, so each is clamped to y = 0 with its raw value disclosed, without a drawing to measure from.
- Solenoid 31 (T relay, playfield G.I.): the 19 placements are the members of the table's GI collection that show a bulb mesh; light18, light21, light22 and light23 show none and are not placed. A table's grouping is not the machine's wiring and the bulb count is unknown, so they stay observed without a quantity.
- Solenoids 13-21 are flashers that both scripts treat as backbox (translite) flashers, but no manual confirms it, so none of the nine is placed or classified. Solenoids 22 and 23 are placed at the lights under the two domes the script pairs with each, and 24 at the light under its blue dome. The scripts also draw 24 on a light under the ramp, a bulb-less render light and so a table effect; whether a second bulb is fitted there is unknown, and it is not placed. The ROM's coil test gives no bulb count for 22-24.
- The 82 matrix lamps drawn on the table's image-mode insert overlays ('1812 PFhole') are projected onto each overlay's centre: the table models no bulb under the inserts, so each point is the insert's centre, not a measured socket.
- The drop-bank resets 8 and 9 are projected onto the centre of their banks, and the Bat-O-Meter lamps 10-14 onto the bulb primitives of the table's vertical stack. IPDB photo 19 shows the Bat-O-Meter as a five-row sign at the upper left with its values in the same order, but a perspective photo gives no coordinates, so the points stay the table's.
- Lamps 24 and 87, which the ROM names NOT USED but drives in play, have no table object and are not placed; whether a bulb is fitted there is unknown.

## Promotion decision

Every controller address is enumerated with a semantic disposition taken from the ROM's own service tests, the mechanisms are inventoried, and polarity is settled for every switch by the read path, the ROM's Switch Matrix Test and the known-working script. Promotion to `author_ready` is refused, and `coverage.missing` is `["output_semantics", "mechanism_behavior", "spatial_placement"]`: lamps 24 and 87, which the ROM names NOT USED, publish state in play that no source explains, and no source shows the knocker (30) fitted; the ball lock the instruction cards describe was lit in the scripted game but never made, so the gate's path and the lock's hold and release are unknown; and every playfield placement rests on one community table lineage's geometry with nothing independent to check it. The service manual's lamp chart and its switch, lamp and coil location drawings, or a second table built independently from the machine, would settle the outputs, the placements and the backbox flashers 13-21. A gameplay run through the lock would show the ROM's lock sequence; the gate's physical route and the hold point need the manual, a factory drawing or an observation of a real machine.
